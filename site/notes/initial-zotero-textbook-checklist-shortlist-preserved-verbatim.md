Yes. The live Zotero library contains several strong checklist sources. All items below have PDFs and Markdown extractions.

## Best next checklists

1. **Modules and rings**
   - Zotero `LGNAU2R4`
   - Citekey `Lam99`
   - T. Y. Lam, *Lectures on Modules and Rings*
   - Covers projective, injective, free, flat, semisimple, Noetherian, Artinian, and finite-length modules.
   - This is the best source for the current module-category residue.
2. **Commutative algebra beyond Atiyah-Macdonald**
   - Zotero `72SP2PMS`
   - Citekey `MR92`
   - Hideyuki Matsumura and Miles Reid, *Commutative Ring Theory*
   - Covers dimension, regular sequences, depth, Cohen-Macaulay rings, completions, and local algebra.
3. **Commutative algebra for algebraic geometry**
   - Zotero `XM34VXLW`
   - Citekey `Eis95`
   - David Eisenbud, *Commutative Algebra with a View Toward Algebraic Geometry*
   - Adds resolutions, syzygies, graded rings, Hilbert functions, depth, and multiplicity.
4. **Ordinary category theory**
   - Zotero `SK2Y8WNL`
   - Citekey `Mac98`
   - Saunders Mac Lane, *Categories for the Working Mathematician*
   - Gives a canonical checklist for categories, functors, natural transformations, adjunctions, Kan extensions, limits, monads, and monoidal categories.
5. **Modern ordinary category theory**
   - Zotero `TIDLVIT4`
   - Citekey `Rie16`
   - Emily Riehl, *Category Theory in Context*
   - This is probably the clearest primary checklist for the project’s standard categorical vocabulary.
6. **Higher-category foundations**
   - Zotero `TS95LIXR`
   - Citekey `RV22`
   - Emily Riehl and Dominic Verity, *Elements of ∞-Category Theory*
   - Relevant to homotopy pullbacks, higher cells, equivalences, adjunctions, and limits of higher categories.
7. **Homological algebra**
   - Zotero `8XN9IJSP`
   - Citekey `Wei94`
   - Charles Weibel, *An Introduction to Homological Algebra*
   - Covers complexes, homotopy, homology, derived functors, Ext, Tor, resolutions, spectral sequences, and derived categories.
8. **Schemes and local algebra**
   - Zotero `ESZQ6XYL`
   - Citekey `Vak25`
   - Ravi Vakil, *The Rising Sea: Foundations of Algebraic Geometry*
   - The best large checklist for spectra, sheaves, schemes, stalks, fiber products, morphism properties, divisors, and cohomology.
9. **Algebraic number theory**
   - Zotero `8698XMKC`
   - Citekey `Neu99`
   - Jürgen Neukirch and Norbert Schappacher, *Algebraic Number Theory*
   - Covers number rings, fractional ideals, valuations, completions, local fields, adeles, ideles, and class groups.
10. **Local fields**
    - Zotero `2K45J388`
    - Citekey `Ser79`
    - Jean-Pierre Serre, *Local Fields*
    - Gives a compact checklist for discrete valuations, ramification, completions, unit filtrations, local extensions, and local class field theory.
11. **Bilinear forms over rings**
    - Zotero `3M28YXGC`
    - Citekey `MH73`
    - John Milnor and Dale Husemöller, *Symmetric Bilinear Forms*
    - Highly relevant to formed modules, radicals, duals, unimodularity, localization, Witt groups, and integral forms.
12. **Quadratic forms**
    - Zotero `58GTJTDZ`
    - Citekey `OM00`
    - O. Timothy O’Meara, *Introduction to Quadratic Forms*
    - Strong source for lattices, modularity, local theory, Jordan decompositions, genus, and equivalence.

## Useful secondary checklists

- Zotero `HMSEFIXJ`, citekey `Ger08`: Larry Gerstein, *Basic Quadratic Forms*. A smaller first checklist than O’Meara.
- Zotero `BA56XQA6`, citekey `Cas08a`: J. W. S. Cassels, *Rational Quadratic Forms*. Best for field-valued quadratic forms and local-global theory.
- Zotero `T2WVLTDB`, citekey `CS10`: Conway and Sloane, *Sphere Packings, Lattices and Groups*. Useful for lattice terminology and examples, but not foundational algebra.
- Zotero `D6XUJE8G`, citekey `Wei82`: André Weil, *Adeles and Algebraic Groups*. Useful for the adelic checklist, but not a basic first source.
- Zotero `4M9EBKXL`, citekey `Liu10`: Qing Liu, *Algebraic Geometry and Arithmetic Curves*. Best after the basic scheme vocabulary exists.
- Zotero `EHRS7CWC`, citekey `Bou83`: Bourbaki, *Algèbre Commutative*. Encyclopedic reference, but less convenient as the first checklist.
- Zotero `WSSX45DL`, citekey `Lan05`: Serge Lang, *Algebra*. Useful for a broad algebra catalogue, but much wider than the current residue.

The highest-value sequence is: `Lam99`, `MR92`, `Rie16`, `Wei94`, `Vak25`, `Neu99`, `Ser79`, `MH73`, then `OM00`.
