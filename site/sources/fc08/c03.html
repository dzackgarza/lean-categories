Convention/notation — geometric tangent space to

FC08-C03-U001 · Ch. 3, § Geometric Tangent Vectors; source L1463 · in Mathlib

Here is a preliminary definition of tangent vectors in Euclidean space. Given a point a ∈ ℝ ^n , let us define the geometric tangent space to ℝ ^n at ^a , denoted by ℝ _a ^n , to be the set a × ℝ ^n = ( a , v ) : v ∈ ℝ ^n . A geometric tangent vector in ℝ ^n is an element of ℝ _a ^n for some a ∈ ℝ ^n As a matter of notation, we abbreviate ( a , v ) as v _a (or sometimes ∣ v ∣ _a if it is clearer, for example if v itself has a subscript). We think of v _a as the vector v with its initial point at a (Fig. 3.1).The set ℝ _a ^n is a real vector space under the natural operations \[ v_{a} + w_{a} = (v+w)_{a}, \qquad c(v_{a}) = (cv)_{a}, \] and the vectors \(e_{i}|_{a}\), \(i = 1,\dots,n\), are a basis for \(\mathbb{R}^{n}_{a}\).

Formalized. Mathlib/Geometry/Manifold/IsManifold/Basic.lean::TangentSpace, TangentBundle; Mathlib/Data/Bundle.lean::Bundle.TotalSpace.toProd — TangentSpace for the standard vector-space model is definitionally the model vector space, while TangentBundle is its total-space bundle and Bundle.TotalSpace.toProd identifies a constant-fibre total space with base × fibre. This is Lee’s geometric tangent-vector model up to the canonical identification that suppresses the fixed base point in the fibre.
