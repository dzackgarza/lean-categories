Construction/definition — local trivialization of E

FC08-C10-U001 · Ch. 10, § Vector Bundles; source L7195 · in Mathlib

Let \(M\) be a topological space. A real vector bundle of rank \(k\) over \(M\) is a topological space \(E\) with a surjective continuous map \(\pi \colon E \to M\) such that: (i) each fiber \(E_{p} = \pi^{-1}(p)\) carries the structure of a \(k\)-dimensional real vector space; (ii) each \(p \in M\) has a neighborhood \(U\) and a homeomorphism \(\Phi \colon \pi^{-1}(U) \to U \times \mathbb{R}^{k}\), a local trivialization of \(E\) over \(U\), with \(\pi_{U} \circ \Phi = \pi\), where \(\pi_{U} \colon U \times \mathbb{R}^{k} \to U\) is the projection, and with \(\Phi|_{E_{q}}\) a vector space isomorphism from \(E_{q}\) to \(\{q\} \times \mathbb{R}^{k} \cong \mathbb{R}^{k}\) for each \(q \in U\).

Formalized. Specializing the fixed model fiber F to a k-dimensional real normed space gives Lee’s rank-k vector-bundle definition; linearEquivAt is the fiber isomorphism.

Definition/convention — smooth vector bundle

If M and E are smooth manifolds with or without boundary, π is a smooth map, and the local trivializations can be chosen to be diffeomorphisms, then E is called a smooth vector bundle. Definition/convention — global trivialization of E )

FC08-C10-U005 · Ch. 10, § Vector Bundles; source L7216 · not formalized

Example 10.2 (Product Bundles). Definition/convention — smoothly trivial

FC08-C10-U054 · Ch. 10, § Fiber Bundles; source L7697 · not formalized

A trivial fiber bundle is one that admits a local trivialization over the entire base space (a global trivialization). It is said to be smoothly trivial if it is a smooth bundle and the global trivialization is a diffeomorphism.
