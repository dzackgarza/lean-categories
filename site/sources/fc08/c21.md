### **Convention/notation — orbit space of the action** {#fc08-c21-u001}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC08-C21-U001` · Ch. 21, § Quotients of Manifolds by Group Actions; source L16299 · [`mathlib`]{.route-mathlib}
:::

Suppose we are given an action of a group G on a topological space M; which we write either as θ : G × M → M or as ( g , p ) ↦ g · p . (For definiteness, let us assume that G acts on the left; similar considerations apply to right actions.) Recall that the orbit of a point p ∈ M is the set of images of p under all elements of the group: G · p = g · p: g ∈ G . Define a relation on M by setting p ∼ q if there exists g ∈ G such that g · p = q This is an equivalence relation, whose equivalence classes are exactly the orbits of G in M . The set of orbits is denoted by M / G ; with the quotient topology it is called the orbit space of the action. It is of great importance to determine conditions under which an orbit space is a smooth manifold. It is important to be aware that some authors use the notation G backslash M for an orbit space determined by a left action of G on M; reserving M / G for one determined by a right action. We use only the latter notation, relying on the context, not the notation, to distinguish between the two cases.

### **Definition/convention — a proper action** {#fc08-c21-u005}

::: {.unit-meta}

To avoid pathological cases such as these, we need to introduce one more restriction on our group actions. A continuous left action of a Lie group G on a manifold M is said to be a proper action if the map G × M → M × M given by ( g , p ) ↦ ( g · p , p ) is a proper map. Note that this is generally a weaker condition than requiring that the map G × M → M defining the action be a proper map (see Problem 21-1).

### **Convention/notation — left coset of H** {#fc08-c21-u021}

Next we describe a construction that can be used to generate a great number of homogeneous spaces, as quotients of Lie groups by closed subgroups. Let G be a Lie group and H ⊆ G be a Lie subgroup.
