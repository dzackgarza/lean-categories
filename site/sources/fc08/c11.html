Convention/notation — vector v

FC08-C11-U008 · Ch. 11, § Covectors; source L7891 · after FC08-C11-U007 · not formalized

Because of Proposition 11.8, the real number \(\omega ( v )\) obtained by applying a covector ! to a vector v is sometimes denoted by either of the more symmetric-looking notations \(\langle \omega , v \rangle\) and \(\langle v , \omega \rangle\) ; both expressions can be thought of either as the action of the covector \(\omega \in V ^ { * }\) on the vector \(v \in V\) , or as the action of the linear functional \(\xi ( v ) \in V ^ { * * }\) on the element \(\omega \in V ^ { * }\) . Definition/convention — star-shaped subset

FC08-C11-U065 · Ch. 11, § Conservative Covector Fields; source L8634 · in Mathlib
