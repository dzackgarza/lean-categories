By Exercise 16.46(b) the Riemannian density is customarily written \(dV_{g}\), the context saying whether a density or a form is meant. When \(M\) is oriented either reading gives the same answer, since reversing the orientation changes the signs of both the integral and \(dV_{g}\); when \(M\) is nonorientable, only the density reading is available.
