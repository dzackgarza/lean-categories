Reparametrizing the graph in terms of $x$ and $y$ means inverting $\pi \circ \psi$, that is solving $(x,y) = (s+t+st,\ t)$ locally for $t$ and $s$, which gives $(t,s) = \left(y,\ \frac{x-y}{1+y}\right)$; so on the flowout manifold $z = s = (x-y)/(1+y)$ and the solution is $u(x,y) = (x-y)/(1+y)$, defined only where $y > -1$, not on the whole plane.
