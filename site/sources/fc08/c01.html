FC08-C01-U001 · Ch. 1, § Topological Manifolds; source L327 · in Mathlib

Suppose M is a topological space. We say that M is a topological manifold of dimension n or a topological n-manifold if it has the following properties: M is a Hausdorff space: for every pair of distinct points \(p , q \in M\) ; there are disjoint open subsets \(U , V \subseteq M\) such that \(p \in U\) and \(q \in V .\)
