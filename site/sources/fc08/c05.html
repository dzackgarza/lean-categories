Definition/convention — embedded submanifold of M is a subset S M that

FC08-C05-U001 · Ch. 5, § Embedded Submanifolds; source L2759 · not formalized

Suppose M is a smooth manifold with or without boundary. An embedded submanifold of M is a subset S M that is a manifold (without boundary) in the subspace topology, endowed with a smooth structure with respect to which the inclusion map \(S \hookrightarrow M\) is a smooth embedding. Embedded submanifolds are also called regular submanifolds; the difference \(\dim M - \dim S\) is the codimension of \(S\) in \(M\).
