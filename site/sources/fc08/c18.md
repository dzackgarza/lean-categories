We begin with a brief summary of singular homology theory. Suppose v _0 , … , v _p are any p 1 points in some Euclidean space R n . They are said to be affinely independent (or in general position) if they are not contained in any .p 1/-dimensional affine subspace. A geometric p-simplex is a subset of ℝ ^n of the form sum_i = 0 ^p t _i v _i: 0 ≤ t _i ≤ 1 text and sum_i = 0 ^p t _i = 1 , for some p + 1 affinely independent points v _0 , … , v _p .
