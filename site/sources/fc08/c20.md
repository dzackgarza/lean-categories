(The Fundamental Theorems of Sophus Lie). (i) FIRST FUNDAMENTAL THEOREM: The set of left-invariant vector fields on a local Lie group is a finite-dimensional Lie algebra under Lie bracket, and two local Lie groups with isomorphic Lie algebras are locally isomorphic. (ii) SECOND FUNDAMENTAL THEOREM: Given an open subset W ⊆ ℝ ^n , there is a one-to-one correspondence between smooth right actions of local Lie groups on W and finite-dimensional Lie subalgebras of X.W /.
