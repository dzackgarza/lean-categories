Definition/convention — open subsets

FC08-CA-U001 · Appendix A, § Topological Spaces; source L17896 · in Mathlib

Let \(X\) be a set. A topology on \(X\) is a collection \(\mathcal{T}\) of subsets of \(X\), called open subsets, such that (i) \(X\) and \(\emptyset\) are open; (ii) the union of any family of open subsets is open; and (iii) the intersection of any finite family of open subsets is open. A pair \((X,\mathcal{T})\) is called a topological space.

Formalized. Mathlib/Topology/Defs/Basic.lean::TopologicalSpace, IsOpen — TopologicalSpace is exactly a family of open sets closed under arbitrary unions and finite intersections; IsOpen names membership in it.
