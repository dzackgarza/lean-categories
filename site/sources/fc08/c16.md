Let $D \subseteq \mathbb { R } ^ { n }$ be a domain of integration, and let $\omega$ be a (continuous) n-form on $\bar { D }$ . Theorem 16.48 (The Divergence Theorem in the Nonorientable Case). Suppose $( M , g )$ is a nonorientable Riemannian manifold with boundary. For any compactly supported smooth vector field X on M; $$ \int_ {M} (\mathrm{div} X) \mu_ {g} = \int_ {\partial M} \langle X, N \rangle_ {g} \mu_ {\widetilde {g}},\tag{16.21} $$ where N is the outward-pointing unit normal vector field along @M; $\widetilde g$ is the induced Riemannian metric on @M; and $\mu _ { g } , \mu _ { \widetilde { g } }$ are the Riemannian densities of $g$ and $\widetilde { g }$ , respectively. Proof. Let $\widehat { \pi } \colon \widehat { M } \to M$ be the orientation covering of M . Problem 5-12 shows that $\hat { \pi }$ restricts to a smooth covering map from each component of $\partial \widehat { M }$ to a component of $\partial M$
