### Construction/definition — local trivialization of E {#fc08-c10-u001}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC08-C10-U001` · Ch. 10, § Vector Bundles; source L7195 · [in Mathlib]{.route-mathlib}
:::

Let $M$ be a topological space. A real vector bundle of rank $k$ over $M$ is a topological space $E$ with a surjective continuous map $\pi \colon E \to M$ such that: (i) each fiber $E_{p} = \pi^{-1}(p)$ carries the structure of a $k$-dimensional real vector space; (ii) each $p \in M$ has a neighborhood $U$ and a homeomorphism $\Phi \colon \pi^{-1}(U) \to U \times \mathbb{R}^{k}$, a local trivialization of $E$ over $U$, with $\pi_{U} \circ \Phi = \pi$, where $\pi_{U} \colon U \times \mathbb{R}^{k} \to U$ is the projection, and with $\Phi|_{E_{q}}$ a vector space isomorphism from $E_{q}$ to $\{q\} \times \mathbb{R}^{k} \cong \mathbb{R}^{k}$ for each $q \in U$.

::: {.unit-lean}
**Formalized.** [`Mathlib/Topology/FiberBundle/Basic.lean::FiberBundle, Bundle.Trivialization`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/Topology/FiberBundle/Basic.html#Bundle.Trivialization); [`Mathlib/Topology/VectorBundle/Basic.lean::VectorBundle, Bundle.Trivialization.linearEquivAt`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/Topology/VectorBundle/Basic.html#Bundle.Trivialization.linearEquivAt) — A `FiberBundle F E` supplies local trivializations of the sigma-type total space over the base, and `VectorBundle ℝ F E` requires those trivializations to be fiberwise linear. Specializing the fixed model fiber `F` to a k-dimensional real normed space gives Lee’s rank-k vector-bundle definition; `linearEquivAt` is the fiber isomorphism.

(Whitney Sums). Given smooth vector bundles $E' \to M$ and $E'' \to M$ of ranks $k'$ and $k''$, the Whitney sum has fiber $E'_{p} \oplus E''_{p}$ at each $p \in M$; its total space is $E' \oplus E'' = \coprod_{p \in M}(E'_{p} \oplus E''_{p})$ with the obvious projection, and since the fibers depend smoothly on $p$ the chart lemma makes it a smooth vector bundle over $M$.
