Definition — path and homotopy of paths

FC07-C01-U001 · §1.1 Paths and Homotopy; source L609 · after FC07-C00-U005 · in Mathlib

A path in X is a continuous f:I→X. Two paths f_0,f_1 with the same endpoints are homotopic rel {0,1} when joined by a homotopy fixing both endpoints.

Formalized. Mathlib/Topology/Homotopy/Path.lean::Path.Homotopy, Path.Homotopic — Homotopy rel endpoints is exactly Mathlib path homotopy; the endpoint constraints are built into the dependent path types.

Searched: P=db584cd6d46c92f209a44c0f1c829460d327499d; Apache-2.0; Lean 4.33.0

Example 1.1

FC07-C01-U002 · §1.1 Paths and Homotopy; source L618 · in Mathlib

Linear Homotopies. Any two paths f_{0} and f_{1} in ℝ^{n} having the same endpoints x_{0} and x_{1} are homotopic via the homotopy f_{t} (s) = (1 - t) f_{0} (s) + t f_{1} (s). Continuity of the homotopy f_{t} as a map I × I → ℝ^{n} follows from continuity of f_{0} and f_{1} since the algebraic operations of vector addition and scalar multiplication in the formula for f_{t} are continuous.

Formalized. Mathlib/Topology/Homotopy/Affine.lean::ContinuousMap.Homotopy.affine — The affine homotopy is the straight-line homotopy between maps into a convex set, giving the stated linear path homotopy in Euclidean space.

Searched: P=db584cd6d46c92f209a44c0f1c829460d327499d; Apache-2.0; Lean 4.33.0

Definition — covering space
