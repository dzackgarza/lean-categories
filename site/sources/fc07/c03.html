Construction — dual cochain complex of a chain complex

For a chain complex C, set C_n^=Hom(C_n,G) and define the coboundary δ=∂*:C_{n−1}→C_n^*; since ∂²=0, δ²=0 and this is the dual cochain complex.

Lemma 3.1

FC07-C03-U004 · §3.1 Cohomology Groups; source L3841 · in Mathlib

Let F,F′ be free resolutions of abelian groups H,H′. Every homomorphism H→H′ extends to a chain map F→F′, any two extensions are chain homotopic, and consequently the cohomology groups obtained by dualizing a free resolution are canonically independent of the chosen resolution.

Formalized. Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Abelian/Projective/Resolution.lean::CategoryTheory.ProjectiveResolution.lift, CategoryTheory.ProjectiveResolution.liftHomotopy, CategoryTheory.ProjectiveResolution.homotopyEquiv; Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Abelian/Ext.lean::CategoryTheory.ProjectiveResolution.isoExt — Free resolutions of abelian groups are projective resolutions. lift extends a morphism to a chain map, liftHomotopy makes any two compatible extensions homotopic, and homotopyEquiv identifies any two projective resolutions. isoExt identifies the cohomology of the dualized resolution with Ext, giving the canonical resolution-independent cohomology asserted in the final clause.

Searched: P=db584cd6d46c92f209a44c0f1c829460d327499d; Apache-2.0; Lean 4.33.0
