Definition — deformation retraction

FC07-C00-U001 · Homotopy and Homotopy Type; source L218 · in another Lean project

A deformation retraction of X onto A is a continuous family f_t:X→X with f_0=id_X, f_1(X)=A, and f_t|_A=id_A for all t.

Formalized. deancureton/sphere-six-complex::SphereSixComplex/Topology/EstablishedStrongDeformationRetracts.lean::SphereSixComplex.StrongDeformationRetraction — The structure records a self-retraction with image in the specified subspace, identity on that subspace, and a homotopy from the identity to the retraction fixed pointwise on the subspace. Together retract_mem and retract_fixed give image exactly the subspace, matching Hatcher’s deformation-retraction convention.

Searched: R=895c0a0661501581c34d42fea3c553d4f0ee43b1; Apache-2.0; Lean 4.34.0-rc1

Definition/construction — mapping cylinder

FC07-C00-U002 · Homotopy and Homotopy Type; source L226 · after FC07-C00-U001 · in another Lean project

For f:X→Y, the mapping cylinder M_f is (X×I)⊔Y modulo (x,1)∼f(x); it deformation retracts onto Y.

Formalized. deancureton/sphere-six-complex::SphereSixComplex/Topology/MappingCylinderGluing.lean::TopCat.MappingCylinder, TopCat.mappingCylinderBase, TopCat.cylinderZeroStrongDeformationRetract; SphereSixComplex/Topology/PushoutHomotopy.lean::TopCat.StrongDeformationRetractData.pushoutRetraction, pushoutHomotopy, inr_pushoutRetraction, inr_pushoutPaths — The mapping cylinder is the topological pushout of a cylinder endpoint and f; interval reversal identifies its zero-end convention with Hatcher’s one-end convention. The cited pushout data gives a retraction onto the target copy and a homotopy to the identity whose target branch stays fixed, so the target is a deformation retract as required.

Searched: R=895c0a0661501581c34d42fea3c553d4f0ee43b1; Apache-2.0; Lean 4.34.0-rc1

Definition — homotopy and homotopic maps

FC07-C00-U003 · Homotopy and Homotopy Type; source L236 · in Mathlib

A homotopy from f_0 to f_1 is a continuous F:X×I→Y, written F(x,t)=f_t(x); maps joined by such a family are homotopic, f_0≃f_1.

Formalized. Mathlib/Topology/Homotopy/Basic.lean::ContinuousMap.Homotopy, ContinuousMap.Homotopic — Pinned Mathlib defines a homotopy as a continuous map on I × X with prescribed endpoint maps and defines Homotopic by existence of such a homotopy, exactly the source unit up to the order of product coordinates.

Definition — homotopy equivalence and homotopy type

FC07-C00-U006 · Homotopy and Homotopy Type; source L244 · after FC07-C00-U003 · in Mathlib

A map f:X→Y is a homotopy equivalence if some g:Y→X satisfies fg≃id_Y and gf≃id_X; then X and Y have the same homotopy type.

Formalized. Mathlib/Topology/Homotopy/Equiv.lean::ContinuousMap.HomotopyEquiv (X ≃ₕ Y) — The structure consists of continuous maps in both directions whose two composites are homotopic to the appropriate identities. Existence of this structure is precisely equality of homotopy type in the source sense.

Definition — contractible and nullhomotopic

FC07-C00-U007 · Homotopy and Homotopy Type; source L248 · after FC07-C00-U006 · in Mathlib

A space is contractible when it has the homotopy type of a point, equivalently when its identity map is homotopic to a constant map (nullhomotopic).
