Definition — homotopy group πₙ

FC07-C04-U001 · §4.1 Definitions and Basic Constructions; source L6781 · after FC07-C00-U005 · in Mathlib

For n≥1, π_n(X,x_0) is the set of homotopy classes rel boundary of maps (In,∂In)→(X,x_0), with group operation by concatenation in one coordinate.

Formalized.
