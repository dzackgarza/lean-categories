### Definition: valuation ring {#fc13-c04-u001}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC13-C04-U001` · §10, pp. 71–72 · [`mathlib`]{.route-mathlib}
:::

A domain R with fraction field K is a valuation ring if for every x∈K, x∈R or x⁻¹∈R. Its ideals are totally ordered by inclusion, so R is local; the field itself is the trivial valuation ring.

::: {.unit-lean}
**In Lean.** [`Mathlib/RingTheory/Valuation/ValuationRing.lean::ValuationRing, iff_ideal_total, isInteger_or_isInteger, isLocalRing`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/RingTheory/Valuation/ValuationRing.html) — Mathlib’s valuation-ring class is equivalent to total divisibility/total ordering of ideals; in a fraction field every x or x⁻¹ is integral over the ring interface, and valuation rings are local. A field carries the trivial valuation-ring instance.
:::
