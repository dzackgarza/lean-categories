### Definition: derivations and k-derivations {#fc13-c09-u001}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC13-C09-U001` · §25, pp. 190–191 · [`mathlib`]{.route-mathlib}
:::

For an A-module M, Der(A,M) consists of additive maps D:A→M satisfying Leibniz. For a k-algebra A, Der_k(A,M) is the A-submodule of derivations vanishing on k; Der_k(A)=Der_k(A,A).

::: {.unit-lean}
**In Lean.** [`Mathlib/RingTheory/Derivation/Basic.lean::Derivation, leibniz, map_algebraMap`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/RingTheory/Derivation/Basic.html) — Mathlib `Derivation R A M` is an R-linear additive map satisfying Leibniz; `map_algebraMap` is the vanishing-on-base condition, exactly Matsumura’s Der_k(A,M).
:::

### Definition: 0-smooth, 0-unramified and 0-etale {#fc13-c09-u007}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC13-C09-U023` · §26, p. 201 · [`mathlib`]{.route-mathlib}
:::

For characteristic p, k^{p^{-n}}={α:α^{p^n}∈k} inside a fixed algebraic closure; their union is the smallest perfect field containing k.

::: {.unit-lean}
**In Lean.** [`Mathlib/FieldTheory/PurelyInseparable/PerfectClosure.lean::perfectClosure, mem_perfectClosure_iff_pow_mem, perfectClosure.perfectField`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/FieldTheory/PurelyInseparable/PerfectClosure.html#perfectClosure.perfectField); [`Mathlib/FieldTheory/Perfect.lean::bijective_iterateFrobenius`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/FieldTheory/Perfect.html) — Inside a perfect ambient field (in particular an algebraic closure), `perfectClosure k E` consists exactly of elements whose p^n-th power lies in k and is itself perfect. Frobenius bijectivity in every perfect intermediate field proves this relative perfect closure is contained in every perfect field containing k, giving the source minimality statement; this assembly was compile-checked against P.
:::
