Construction: quotient-ring ideal correspondence

FC13-C01-U001 · §1, p. 1 · in Mathlib

For I⊲A, ideals of A/I correspond bijectively to ideals J⊇I in A; under J↦J/I, (A/I)/(J/I)≅A/J, and the image/preimage formulas are f(I′)=(I+I′)/I and f⁻¹(f(I′))=I+I′.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Maps.lean::relIsoOfSurjective; Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Quotient/Operations.lean::comap_map_quotientMk, quotQuotEquivQuotOfLE — The surjective-map interval isomorphism gives ideals of A/I versus ideals above I; comap_map_quotientMk gives I+I′ on contraction after image, and the double-quotient equivalence is the third isomorphism theorem.

Definition: proper ideal and unit

FC13-C01-U002 · §1, p. 1 · in Mathlib

A proper ideal is an ideal distinct from A=(1). An element a is a unit iff it has a multiplicative inverse, equivalently (a)=(1).

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Span.lean::span_singleton_eq_top; Mathlib/Algebra/Group/Units/Defs.lean::isUnit_iff_exists_inv — Proper is I ≠ ⊤; principal ideal (a)=⊤ is equivalent to IsUnit a, and isUnit_iff_exists_inv gives the inverse criterion in the commutative-ring setting.

Lemma: unit plus nilpotent

FC13-C01-U003 · §1, p. 1 · after FC13-C01-U002 · in Mathlib

If a is a unit and x is nilpotent, then a+x is a unit.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Nilpotent/Basic.lean::isUnit_add_left_of_commute — IsNilpotent.isUnit_add_left_of_commute specializes in a commutative ring to a unit plus a nilpotent being a unit.

Convention: zero ring

FC13-C01-U004 · §1, pp. 1–2 · in Mathlib

The zero ring A=0 is allowed; it is the ring with 1=0 and hence one element.

Formalized. Mathlib/Algebra/GroupWithZero/Basic.lean::subsingleton_iff_zero_eq_one, subsingleton_of_zero_eq_one — Mathlib permits subsingleton rings; 0=1 is equivalent to subsingleton, hence to the one-element zero ring.

Definition: integral domain and field

FC13-C01-U005 · §1, p. 2 · after FC13-C01-U002, FC13-C01-U004 · in Mathlib

An integral domain is a nonzero ring with no nonzero zero-divisors. A field is an integral domain in which every nonzero element is a unit; equivalently it has exactly the ideals (0),(1).

Formalized. Mathlib/Algebra/Ring/Defs.lean::class IsDomain; Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Basic.lean::isField_iff_isSimpleOrder_ideal — IsDomain includes nontriviality and cancellation/no zero divisors; the field theorem identifies fields with rings whose ideal lattice has only bottom and top.

Definition: maximal ideal

FC13-C01-U006 · §1, p. 2 · after FC13-C01-U002, FC13-C01-U005 · in Mathlib

A maximal ideal is maximal among proper ideals; m is maximal iff A/m is a field.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Quotient/Basic.lean::maximal_ideal_iff_isField_quotient — The theorem is exactly m maximal iff A ⧸ m is a field; maximality itself is the maximal proper-ideal predicate.

Theorem: Theorem 1.1

FC13-C01-U007 · §1, p. 2 · after FC13-C01-U002, FC13-C01-U006 · in Mathlib

Every proper ideal I is contained in a maximal ideal.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Maximal.lean::exists_le_maximal — Ideal.exists_le_maximal has hypothesis I ≠ ⊤ and returns a maximal ideal containing I.

Definition: prime ideal

FC13-C01-U008 · §1, p. 2 · after FC13-C01-U005, FC13-C01-U006 · in Mathlib

P is prime iff A/P is an integral domain, equivalently P≠A and xy∈P implies x∈P or y∈P. Every maximal ideal is prime.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Prime.lean::isPrime_iff; Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Quotient/Basic.lean::isDomain_iff_prime; Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Maximal.lean::IsMaximal.isPrime — These give the elementwise prime criterion, quotient-domain equivalence, and maximal-implies-prime statement with the source hypotheses.

Lemma: prime ideal absorbs ideal products

FC13-C01-U009 · §1, p. 2 · after FC13-C01-U008 · in Mathlib

If I,J are ideals and IJ⊆P for a prime ideal P, then I⊆P or J⊆P.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Operations.lean::IsPrime.mul_le — Ideal.IsPrime.mul_le says I*J ≤ P forces I ≤ P ∨ J ≤ P.

Definition: multiplicative subset

FC13-C01-U010 · §1, p. 2 · in Mathlib

A subset S⊆A is multiplicative if 1∈S and x,y∈S imply xy∈S.

Formalized. Mathlib/Algebra/Group/Submonoid/Defs.lean::structure Submonoid — Submonoid records 1 ∈ S and closure under multiplication, exactly the source multiplicative-set data.

Theorem: Theorem 1.2

FC13-C01-U011 · §1, p. 2 · after FC13-C01-U007, FC13-C01-U010 · in Mathlib

If S is multiplicative and I∩S=∅, there exists a prime ideal P⊇I with P∩S=∅.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Maximal.lean::exists_le_prime_disjoint — For a submonoid S, disjointness from I yields a prime P ≥ I disjoint from S, exactly Theorem 1.2.

Definition: radical of an ideal

FC13-C01-U012 · §1, p. 3 · after FC13-C01-U001 · in Mathlib

√I={a∈A | aⁿ∈I for some n>0}; it is an ideal.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Operations.lean::def radical, mem_radical_iff — Ideal.radical is an ideal with membership ∃ n, r^n ∈ I; allowing n=0 is harmless precisely when I=⊤, so it matches the positive-power source definition.

Theorem: radical as intersection of primes

FC13-C01-U013 · §1, p. 3 · after FC13-C01-U008, FC13-C01-U011, FC13-C01-U012 · in Mathlib

√I is the intersection of all prime ideals containing I.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/MinimalPrime/Basic.lean::radical_eq_sInf — Ideal.radical_eq_sInf identifies the radical with the infimum/intersection of prime ideals containing the ideal.

Definition: nilradical, reduced ring, reduction

FC13-C01-U014 · §1, p. 3 · after FC13-C01-U012, FC13-C01-U013 · in Mathlib

nil(A)=√(0) is the ideal of nilpotents and the intersection of all prime ideals. A is reduced iff nil(A)=0; A_red=A/nil(A) is reduced.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Nilpotent/Lemmas.lean::nilradical_eq_sInf, nilradical_eq_bot_iff; Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Quotient/Nilpotent.lean::isRadical_iff_quotient_reduced — Mathlib identifies the nilradical with the intersection of primes and reducedness with nilradical zero; quotienting by a radical ideal is reduced, applied to the nilradical.

Definition: Jacobson radical

FC13-C01-U015 · §1, p. 3 · after FC13-C01-U006 · in Mathlib

rad(A) is the intersection of all maximal ideals of a nonzero ring A.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Jacobson/Ideal.lean::def jacobson, jacobson_bot — For I=⊥, Ideal.jacobson is the intersection of maximal ideals above zero, i.e. the ring Jacobson radical.

Criterion: Jacobson-radical unit criterion

FC13-C01-U016 · §1, p. 3 · after FC13-C01-U002, FC13-C01-U007, FC13-C01-U015 · in Mathlib

x∈rad(A) iff 1+ax is a unit for every a∈A.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Jacobson/Ideal.lean::mem_jacobson_bot — mem_jacobson_bot states membership iff x*y+1 is a unit for every y; commutativity gives the source 1+ax form.

Definition: local and semilocal rings; residue field

FC13-C01-U017 · §1, p. 3 · after FC13-C01-U006 · in Mathlib

A local ring has one maximal ideal; a nonzero semilocal ring has finitely many. For a local ring (A,m), k=A/m is its residue field; (A,m,k) records this data.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/LocalRing/Defs.lean::class IsLocalRing; Mathlib/RingTheory/LocalProperties/Semilocal.lean::Finite (MaximalSpectrum R); Mathlib/RingTheory/LocalRing/ResidueField/Defs.lean::def ResidueField, def residue — IsLocalRing is the unique-maximal/nonunit local structure; Mathlib encodes semilocality by finiteness of MaximalSpectrum, and ResidueField R is the quotient by the maximal ideal.

Criterion: local-ring nonunit criterion

FC13-C01-U018 · §1, p. 3 · after FC13-C01-U002, FC13-C01-U017 · in Mathlib

For a local ring (A,m), A∖m is the unit group. Conversely, a nonzero ring whose nonunits form an ideal is local.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/LocalRing/MaximalIdeal/Basic.lean::mem_maximalIdeal; Mathlib/RingTheory/LocalRing/Basic.lean::of_nonunits_add, nonunitsAddSubmonoid — mem_maximalIdeal identifies the maximal ideal with nonunits. Conversely closure of nonunits under addition (multiplicative absorption is automatic) constructs IsLocalRing; nontriviality is the source nonzero hypothesis.

Definition: coprime ideals

FC13-C01-U019 · §1, p. 3 · in Mathlib

Ideals I,I′ are coprime if I+I′=(1). For coprime ideals, II′=I∩I′; if I is coprime to I′ and I″ then it is coprime to I′I″.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Operations.lean::isCoprime_iff_sup_eq, mul_eq_inf_of_isCoprime, isCoprime_biInf — IsCoprime is sum/top; coprime product equals intersection, and isCoprime_biInf plus that equality gives coprimality with a product from coprimality with each factor.

Theorem: Theorem 1.3

FC13-C01-U020 · §1, pp. 3–4 · after FC13-C01-U019 · in Mathlib

For pairwise coprime ideals I₁,…,Iₙ, their product equals their intersection. In particular, for the finitely many maximal ideals of a semilocal ring, ∏mᵢ=⋂mᵢ.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Operations.lean::prod_eq_iInf_of_pairwise_isCoprime, isCoprime_of_isMaximal — The finite product equals finite intersection for pairwise coprime ideals. Distinct maximal ideals are coprime; semilocality supplies finiteness of the maximal spectrum, giving the stated specialization.

Theorem: Theorem 1.4 (Chinese remainder theorem)

FC13-C01-U021 · §1, p. 4 · after FC13-C01-U019, FC13-C01-U020 · in Mathlib

For pairwise coprime ideals I₁,…,Iₙ, A/(I₁⋯Iₙ)≅∏ᵢA/Iᵢ.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Quotient/Operations.lean::quotientInfRingEquivPiQuotient — This is the finite CRT for pairwise coprime ideals; composing with product=intersection gives the source quotient by the product.

Example: formal power-series units and local rings

FC13-C01-U022 · §1, p. 4 · after FC13-C01-U002, FC13-C01-U015, FC13-C01-U017 · not formalized

In AX₁,…,Xₙ, a series is a unit iff its constant term is a unit. Hence (X₁,…,Xₙ)⊆rad(AX₁,…,Xₙ); if k is a field then kX₁,…,Xₙ is local with maximal ideal (X₁,…,Xₙ).

Strict bundle semantics therefore rejects the partial match.
