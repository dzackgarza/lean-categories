Definition: minimal free resolution over a local ring

FC13-C07-U001 · §19, pp. 153–154 · not formalized

A free resolution of a finite module over a local ring is minimal when every differential has image inside the maximal ideal times the next free module. Minimal resolutions exist, and their free ranks are invariants detected by Tor with the residue field.

Not formalized. Projective dimension and Tor infrastructure exist, but no checked pinned/current/open declaration constructs Matsumura’s minimal free resolution over a local ring with differentials in the maximal ideal and identifies all Betti ranks with Tor against the residue field.

Definition: global dimension

FC13-C07-U006 · §19, p. 155 · after FC13-C07-U005 · not formalized

gl.dim A is the supremum of projective dimensions of all A-modules; by Lemma 2 it is the maximum over finite modules, and for Noetherian local (A,m,k), gl.dim A=projdim_A k.

Not formalized. The project already defines globalDimension, and PR #29802 proves the supremum-over-finite-modules result. Its residue-field equality is proved under regular-local hypotheses, whereas Matsumura’s U006 states gl.dim A = pd_A(k) for an arbitrary Noetherian local ring. Strict bundle semantics therefore leaves U006 unmatched.
