Definition: derivations and k-derivations

FC13-C09-U001 · §25, pp. 190–191 · in Mathlib

For an A-module M, Der(A,M) consists of additive maps D:A→M satisfying Leibniz. For a k-algebra A, Der_k(A,M) is the A-submodule of derivations vanishing on k; Der_k(A)=Der_k(A,A).

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Derivation/Basic.lean::Derivation, leibniz, map_algebraMap — Mathlib Derivation R A M is an R-linear additive map satisfying Leibniz; map_algebraMap is the vanishing-on-base condition, exactly Matsumura’s Der_k(A,M).

Searched: P=db584cd6d46c92f209a44c0f1c829460d327499d

Construction: Lie bracket and p-th powers of derivations

FC13-C09-U002 · §25, pp. 190–191 · after FC13-C09-U001 · not formalized

Der_k(A) is a Lie algebra under [D,D′]=DD′−D′D. In characteristic p, D(a^p)=0 for every derivation and D^p is again a derivation.

Not formalized. Derivation/Lie.lean gives the commutator Lie algebra and leibniz_pow gives D(a^p)=0 in characteristic p, but no checked pinned/current/open declaration makes D^p an ordinary derivation or packages the whole characteristic-p row.

Definition: square-zero and trivial extensions

FC13-C09-U003 · §25, p. 191 · in Mathlib

If N⊲B has N²=0 and A=B/N, then B is an extension of the k-algebra A by the A-module N. It is split when A→B admits a k-algebra section; the canonical trivial extension A*N has underlying module A⊕N and product (a,x)(a′,x′)=(aa′,ax′+a′x).

Formalized. Mathlib/Algebra/TrivSqZeroExt/Basic.lean::TrivSqZeroExt, fstHom, inlAlgHom, inr_mul_inr — TrivSqZeroExt A N is A⊕N with the source multiplication, projection, canonical section, and square-zero N-summand.

Searched: P=db584cd6d46c92f209a44c0f1c829460d327499d

Lemma: difference of liftings is a derivation

FC13-C09-U004 · §25, p. 191 · after FC13-C09-U003 · in Mathlib

For a square-zero extension B→A and two k-algebra liftings h,h′ of the same map C→A, h−h′ is a k-derivation C→N=ker(B→A); conversely h+D is another lifting for every such derivation.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Derivation/ToSquareZero.lean::diffToIdealOfQuotientCompEq, derivationToSquareZeroOfLift, liftOfDerivationToSquareZero, derivationToSquareZeroEquivLift — derivationToSquareZeroEquivLift identifies derivations into the square-zero kernel with lifts of a fixed quotient map; its forward/backward maps are exactly h-h′ and h+D.

Searched: P=db584cd6d46c92f209a44c0f1c829460d327499d

Construction: Kähler differentials and universal derivation

FC13-C09-U005 · §25, pp. 191–192 · after FC13-C09-U004 · in Mathlib

For a k-algebra A, with I=ker(A⊗kA→A), define Ω{A/k}=I/I² and d(a)=1⊗a−a⊗1 mod I². Then d is universal: Der_k(A,M)≅Hom_A(Ω_{A/k},M) naturally in M.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Kaehler/Basic.lean::KaehlerDifferential, D, linearMapEquivDerivation — Pinned Mathlib defines Ω as the cotangent quotient of ker(S⊗_R S→S), defines the universal derivation D, and represents derivations by S-linear maps out of Ω.

Searched: P=db584cd6d46c92f209a44c0f1c829460d327499d

Example: differentials of generated and polynomial algebras

FC13-C09-U006 · §25, pp. 192–193 · after FC13-C09-U005 · in Mathlib

If A is generated over k by U then Ω_{A/k} is generated by {du:u∈U}. For A=k[X_1,…,X_n], Ω_{A/k} is free with basis dX_1,…,dX_n, and d f=Σ_i(∂f/∂X_i)dX_i.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Kaehler/Basic.lean::span_range_derivation, finite; Mathlib/RingTheory/Kaehler/Polynomial.lean::mvPolynomialBasis, mvPolynomialBasis_repr_apply, polynomial_D_apply — mvPolynomialBasis gives the free basis dX_i and mvPolynomialBasis_repr_apply identifies coefficients with partial derivatives. The generic spanning proof for Kähler differentials uses adjoin induction on algebra generators, exactly the source generator argument.

Searched: P=db584cd6d46c92f209a44c0f1c829460d327499d

Definition: 0-smooth, 0-unramified and 0-etale

FC13-C09-U007 · §25, p. 193 · after FC13-C09-U004, FC13-C09-U005 · in Mathlib

A k-algebra A is 0-smooth if maps A→C/N lift across every square-zero ideal N; it is 0-unramified if such liftings are unique, and 0-etale if both existence and uniqueness hold. 0-unramified is equivalent to Ω_{A/k}=0.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Smooth/Basic.lean::FormallySmooth, iff_comp_surjective; Mathlib/RingTheory/Unramified/Basic.lean::FormallyUnramified, iff_comp_injective, subsingleton_kaehlerDifferential; Mathlib/RingTheory/Etale/Basic.lean::FormallyEtale, iff_comp_bijective, iff_formallyUnramified_and_formallySmooth — The three pinned classes are exactly the square-zero existence, uniqueness, and bijective lifting conditions; formal unramifiedness is represented by Ω=0, matching Matsumura’s 0-terminology.

Searched: P=db584cd6d46c92f209a44c0f1c829460d327499d

Theorem: Theorem 25.1 (first fundamental exact sequence)

FC13-C09-U009 · §25, pp. 193–194 · after FC13-C09-U005, FC13-C09-U007 · in Mathlib

For k→A→B there is an exact sequence Ω_{A/k}⊗A B→Ω{B/k}→Ω_{B/A}→0. If B is 0-smooth over A, adding 0 on the left gives a split exact sequence.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Kaehler/Basic.lean::exact_mapBaseChange_map, map_surjective; Mathlib/RingTheory/Kaehler/JacobiZariski.lean::H1Cotangent.exact_map_δ, H1Cotangent.exact_δ_mapBaseChange; Mathlib/RingTheory/Smooth/Basic.lean::projective_kaehlerDifferential, subsingleton_h1Cotangent — The pinned Jacobi–Zariski sequence is Ω_A/k⊗_A B→Ω_B/k→Ω_B/A→0. If B/A is formally smooth, H₁(B/A)=0 makes the left map injective and projectivity of Ω_B/A splits the right surjection, giving the source split exact sequence.

Searched: P=db584cd6d46c92f209a44c0f1c829460d327499d

Theorem: Theorem 25.2 (second fundamental exact sequence)

FC13-C09-U010 · §25, pp. 194–195 · after FC13-C09-U009 · in Mathlib

For k→A→B=A/m, there is an exact sequence m/m²→Ω_{A/k}⊗A B→Ω{B/k}→0. If B is 0-smooth over k, the sequence is split exact.

Formalized. Formal smoothness of B/k kills H₁(B/k), so exactness makes the conormal map injective; projectivity of Ω_B/k splits the right surjection. This assembly was compile-checked against P.

Example: Jacobian presentation of Ω for quotient algebras

If B=k[X_1,…,X_n]/(f_1,…,f_m), then Ω_{B/k} is the quotient of the free B-module on dX_j by the submodule generated by df_i=Σ_j(∂f_i/∂X_j)dX_j.

Theorem: Theorem 25.3

FC13-C09-U012 · §25, pp. 195–196 · after FC13-C09-U007, FC13-C09-U009 · in Mathlib

If L/K is separable algebraic, then L is 0-etale over K. For any subfield k⊆K, Ω_{L/k}≅Ω_{K/k}⊗_K L.

Theorem: Theorem 25.4

FC13-C09-U013 · §25, pp. 196–197 · after FC13-C09-U002 · not formalized

For a field K of characteristic p and nonzero D∈Der(K), the operators 1,D,…,D^{p−1} are K-linearly independent; a K-linear combination of them can be a derivation only with zero constant and all coefficients except that of D equal to zero.

Not formalized. Pinned/current/open searches found ordinary derivations and their Lie bracket but no source-shaped theorem for the linear independence of 1,D,…,D^{p-1} or Hochschild’s (aD)^p formula.

Theorem: Theorem 25.5 (Hochschild formula)

FC13-C09-U014 · §25, p. 197 · after FC13-C09-U013 · not formalized

In characteristic p, for a∈A and D∈Der(A), (aD)p=apDp+(aD){p−1}(a)D.

Not formalized. Pinned/current/open searches found ordinary derivations and their Lie bracket but no source-shaped theorem for the linear independence of 1,D,…,D^{p-1} or Hochschild’s (aD)^p formula.

Definition: separable k-algebra

FC13-C09-U015 · §26, p. 198 · after FC13-C01-U014 · not formalized

A k-algebra A is separable over the field k if A⊗_k k′ is reduced for every field extension k′/k. The property passes to subalgebras, is finitely generated-local on A, and is stable under field extension.

Not formalized. Algebra.IsGeometricallyReduced matches the source definition over a field and supports subalgebras/finitely-generated detection, but its pinned file explicitly lists arbitrary field-extension stability as TODO. The source row includes that clause, so strict bundle semantics leaves it unmatched.

Criterion: discriminant criterion for finite separable algebras

FC13-C09-U016 · §26, pp. 198–199 · after FC13-C09-U015 · not formalized
