### Definition: M-regular element and M-sequence {#fc13-c06-u001}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC13-C06-U001` · §16, p. 123 · [`mathlib`]{.route-mathlib}
:::

An element a∈A is M-regular if ax≠0 for every nonzero x∈M. A sequence a_1,…,a_n is M-regular when each a_i is regular on M/(a_1,…,a_{i-1})M and M≠(a_1,…,a_n)M.

::: {.unit-lean}
**In Lean.** [`Mathlib/RingTheory/Regular/IsSMulRegular.lean::IsSMulRegular`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/RingTheory/Regular/IsSMulRegular.html); [`Mathlib/RingTheory/Regular/RegularSequence.lean::IsRegular, isRegular_iff`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/RingTheory/Regular/RegularSequence.html) — `IsSMulRegular M a` is injectivity of multiplication by a; `Sequence.IsRegular M rs` is exactly successive regularity on the quotient by earlier terms together with the final nontriviality condition.
:::
