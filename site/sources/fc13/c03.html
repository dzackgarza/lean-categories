Definition: flat and faithfully flat module

FC13-C03-U001 · §7, p. 45 · in Mathlib

An A-module M is flat if tensoring every exact sequence with M preserves exactness. It is faithfully flat if a sequence is exact exactly when its tensor with M is exact; it suffices to test short exact sequences, or injectivity at the left end.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/Basic.lean::Module.Flat, iff_rTensor_exact, iff_lTensor_exact; Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/FaithfullyFlat/Basic.lean::Module.FaithfullyFlat, rTensor_exact_iff_exact, lTensor_exact_iff_exact — Flatness is exactly preservation of tensor exactness; faithful flatness extends flatness and reflects exactness, with the pinned iff theorems giving Matsumura’s preservation/reflection formulation.

Definition: flat ring homomorphism / flat algebra

FC13-C03-U002 · §7, pp. 45–46 · after FC13-C03-U001 · in Mathlib

A ring map A→B is flat when B is flat as an A-module; B is then a flat A-algebra.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/Basic.lean::Module.Flat — A flat A-algebra B is represented exactly by the instance Module.Flat A B for the algebra-map module structure.

Example: localisations are flat

Theorem: transitivity of flatness and faithful flatness

For an A-algebra B and B-module M: B A-flat and M B-flat imply M A-flat; faithful-flat analogues hold; if M is B-faithfully-flat and A-flat then B is A-flat; and if M is faithfully flat over both A and B then B is faithfully flat over A.

FC13-C03-U005 · §7, p. 46 · after FC13-C03-U001 · in Mathlib

Theorem: Theorem 7.1

FC13-C03-U006 · §7, pp. 46–47 · after FC13-C02-U011, FC13-C03-U001 · in Mathlib

For A→B and a B-module M, M is flat over A iff M_P is flat over A_p for every prime P of B, where p=P∩A; it suffices to test maximal ideals P.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/Localization.lean::flat_iff_of_isLocalization, flat_of_isLocalized_maximal; Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/AtPrime/Basic.lean::Localization.AtPrime — Localizing at P and its contraction p preserves/refines flatness via flat_iff_of_isLocalization; flat_of_isLocalized_maximal recovers global flatness from maximal localizations. Prime testing follows by the same AtPrime equivalence.

Theorem: Theorem 7.2

FC13-C03-U007 · §7, p. 47 · after FC13-C03-U001 · in Mathlib

For an A-module M, faithful flatness is equivalent to: M is flat and N⊗_A M≠0 for every nonzero N; equivalently, M is flat and mM≠M for every maximal ideal m.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/FaithfullyFlat/Basic.lean::iff_flat_and_proper_ideal, nontrivial_tensorProduct_iff_left, nontrivial_tensorProduct_iff_right — FaithfullyFlat is equivalent to flatness plus every proper ideal acting nontrivially, hence in particular the maximal-ideal test; the tensor nontriviality iff theorems are exactly the nonzero-module criterion.

Definition: fibre of Spec B→Spec A

FC13-C03-U008 · §7, pp. 47–48 · after FC13-C02-U015, FC13-C02-U024 · in Mathlib

For A→B and p∈Spec A, the fibre over p is Spec(B⊗_A κ(p)), canonically homeomorphic to {P∈Spec B | P∩A=p}.

Theorem: Theorem 7.5

FC13-C03-U014 · §7, p. 49 · after FC13-C03-U001 · in Mathlib

If A→B is faithfully flat, then M→M⊗_A B, m↦m⊗1, is injective for every A-module M; in particular A→B is injective, and IB∩A=I for every ideal I.

Formalized. Theorem: Theorem 7.6 (equational criterion)

FC13-C03-U015 · §7, pp. 49–50 · after FC13-C03-U001 · in Mathlib

If M is flat, every finite system Σ_j a_ij x_j=0 in M is obtained as an M-linear combination of solutions over A: x_j=Σ_k b_jk y_k with Σ_j a_ij b_jk=0. Conversely it suffices for this conclusion to hold for a single equation.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/EquationalCriterion.lean::tfae_equational_criterion — The equational-criterion development formalizes factorization of finite relations through relations over the base and proves the converse flatness criterion.

Theorem: Theorem 7.7 (ideal criterion for flatness)

FC13-C03-U016 · §7, pp. 50–51 · after FC13-C03-U001 · in Mathlib

M is flat iff for every finitely generated ideal I, the canonical map I⊗_A M→M is injective; then I⊗M≅IM.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/Tensor.lean::iff_lTensor_injective, iff_rTensor_injective — Pinned flatness is equivalent to injectivity of tensoring finitely generated ideals; the image of the multiplication map is the product submodule, yielding I⊗M ≃ IM.

Theorem: Theorem 7.8 (Tor criterion)

For an A-module M, the following are equivalent: M is flat; Tor_i^A(M,N)=0 for every A-module N and every i>0; Tor_1^A(M,A/I)=0 for every finitely generated ideal I.
