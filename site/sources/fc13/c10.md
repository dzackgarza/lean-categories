### Definition: I-smooth, I-unramified and I-etale {#fc13-c10-u001}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC13-C10-U001` · §28, pp. 213–214 · after [`FC13-C09-U007`](c09.html#fc13-c09-u007) · [not formalized]{.route-unmatched}
:::

For an A-algebra B with I-adic topology, I-smoothness means continuous maps B→C/N lift across square-zero N; I-unramified means at most one lift; I-etale means both. For I=0 these are the 0-smooth/unramified/etale notions of §25.

::: {.unit-lean}
**Not formalized.** `Smooth/AdicCompletion.lean` proves that ordinary formally smooth algebras lift maps into adically complete targets, but Mathlib has no source-level I-smooth/I-unramified/I-étale topology-dependent notion. Consequently this unit cannot be represented without first formalizing Matsumura’s adic interface.
