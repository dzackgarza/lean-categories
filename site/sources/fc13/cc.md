### Definition: alternating multilinear map {#fc13-cc-u001}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC13-CC-U001` · Appendix C, p. 283
:::

An r-multilinear map M^r→N is alternating if it vanishes whenever two arguments coincide; swapping two arguments changes the sign.

### Definition/construction: exterior power {#fc13-cc-u002}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC13-CC-U002` · Appendix C, p. 283 · after [`FC13-CA-U004`](ca.html#fc13-ca-u004), [`FC13-CC-U001`](#fc13-cc-u001)
:::

The rth exterior power Λ^rM represents alternating r-multilinear maps. It is the quotient of M^{⊗r} by tensors with repeated factors; x_1∧⋯∧x_r denotes the universal alternating image.

### Theorem: exterior powers of finite free modules {#fc13-cc-u003}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC13-CC-U003` · Appendix C, p. 283 · after [`FC13-CC-U002`](#fc13-cc-u002)
:::

If M is free of rank n with basis e_1,…,e_n, then Λ^rM=0 for r>n and for r≤n it is free of rank binom(n,r) with basis e_{i_1}∧⋯∧e_{i_r}, i_1<⋯<i_r.

### Example: Λ² of an ideal can be nonzero {#fc13-cc-u004}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC13-CC-U004` · Appendix C, pp. 283–284 · after [`FC13-CC-U002`](#fc13-cc-u002)
:::

Although Λ²A=0, Λ²I need not vanish for an ideal I⊆A; for A=k[x,y] and I=(x,y), an alternating pairing I×I→A/I with value 1 on (x,y) shows Λ²I≠0.

### Theorem: exterior powers commute with scalar extension {#fc13-cc-u005}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC13-CC-U005` · Appendix C, p. 284 · after [`FC13-CA-U013`](ca.html#fc13-ca-u013), [`FC13-CC-U002`](#fc13-cc-u002)
:::

For an A-algebra B and A-module M, (Λ^r_A M)⊗_A B≅Λ^r_B(M⊗_A B).

### Theorem: Theorem C1 {#fc13-cc-u006}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC13-CC-U006` · Appendix C, p. 284 · after [`FC13-C04-U004`](c04.html#fc13-c04-u004), [`FC13-CC-U003`](#fc13-cc-u003)
:::

Let A be a domain, I_1,…,I_r ideals and M=I_1⊕⋯⊕I_r. If T is the torsion of Λ^rM, then (Λ^rM)/T≅I_1⋯I_r. Hence an isomorphism ⊕I_i≅⊕J_i implies I_1⋯I_r≅J_1⋯J_r.

### Remark: direct sums of ideals over Dedekind rings {#fc13-cc-u007}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC13-CC-U007` · Appendix C, p. 284 · after [`FC13-C04-U029`](c04.html#fc13-c04-u029), [`FC13-CC-U006`](#fc13-cc-u006)
:::

For a Dedekind ring, the source records I_1⊕⋯⊕I_r≅A^{r−1}⊕I_1⋯I_r.

### Theorem: Theorem C2 {#fc13-cc-u008}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC13-CC-U008` · Appendix C, pp. 284–285 · after [`FC13-CC-U002`](#fc13-cc-u002)
:::

For A-modules M,N, Λ^r(M⊕N)≅⊕_{s+t=r}(Λ^sM)⊗_A(Λ^tN).

### Definition: skew-commutative graded algebra {#fc13-cc-u009}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC13-CC-U009` · Appendix C, p. 285
:::

A graded A-algebra E is skew-commutative when E_pE_q⊆E_{p+q}, xy=(−1)^{pq}yx for homogeneous x,y, and every odd-degree homogeneous element squares to zero.

### Definition: skew derivation {#fc13-cc-u010}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC13-CC-U010` · Appendix C, p. 285 · after [`FC13-CC-U009`](#fc13-cc-u009)
:::

A skew derivation d on a skew-commutative graded algebra lowers degree by one and satisfies d(xy)=(dx)y+(−1)^p x(dy) for x of degree p.

### Construction: exterior algebra {#fc13-cc-u011}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC13-CC-U011` · Appendix C, pp. 285–286 · after [`FC13-CC-U002`](#fc13-cc-u002), [`FC13-CC-U009`](#fc13-cc-u009)
:::

The wedge maps Λ^pM×Λ^qM→Λ^{p+q}M define an associative multiplication on ΛM=⊕_{n≥0}Λ^nM, making it a skew-commutative graded A-algebra.

### Theorem: skew derivation generated by a linear functional {#fc13-cc-u012}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC13-CC-U012` · Appendix C, p. 286 · after [`FC13-CC-U010`](#fc13-cc-u010), [`FC13-CC-U011`](#fc13-cc-u011)
:::

Every A-linear map α:M→A extends uniquely to a skew derivation d of ΛM, with d(x_1∧⋯∧x_p)=Σ_i(−1)^{i−1}α(x_i)x_1∧⋯∧x̂_i∧⋯∧x_p.

### Construction: Koszul complex as an exterior algebra {#fc13-cc-u013}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC13-CC-U013` · Appendix C, p. 286 · after [`FC13-C06-U006`](c06.html#fc13-c06-u006), [`FC13-CC-U012`](#fc13-cc-u012)
:::

For free M=Ae_1⊕⋯⊕Ae_n and scalars c_i, the skew derivation with d(e_i)=c_i turns ΛM into the Koszul complex K(c_1,…,c_n).
