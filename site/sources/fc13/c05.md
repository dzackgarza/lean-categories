### Definition: graded rings, graded modules and homogeneous elements {#fc13-c05-u001}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC13-C05-U001` · §13, pp. 92–93 · [`mathlib`]{.route-mathlib}
:::

For an abelian semigroup G with identity, a G-graded ring is a direct sum R=⊕_{i∈G}R_i with R_iR_j⊆R_{i+j}; a graded module is M=⊕M_i with R_iM_j⊆M_{i+j}. Elements in a single M_i are homogeneous of degree i, and every element has unique homogeneous terms.

::: {.unit-lean}
**In Lean.** [`Mathlib/RingTheory/GradedAlgebra/Basic.lean::GradedRing`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/RingTheory/GradedAlgebra/Basic.html); [`Mathlib/Algebra/DirectSum/Decomposition.lean::Decomposition`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/Algebra/DirectSum/Decomposition.html); [`Mathlib/Algebra/GradedMulAction.lean::SetLike.GradedSMul`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/Algebra/GradedMulAction.html#SetLike.GradedSMul) — `GradedRing` is a direct-sum decomposition with graded multiplication; a module decomposition together with `GradedSMul` gives the graded module condition, and [`DirectSum.Decomposition`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/find/?pattern=DirectSum.Decomposition#doc) supplies unique homogeneous components.
:::
