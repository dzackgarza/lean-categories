Definition: M-regular element and M-sequence

FC13-C06-U001 · §16, p. 123 · in Mathlib

An element a∈A is M-regular if ax≠0 for every nonzero x∈M. A sequence a_1,…,a_n is M-regular when each a_i is regular on M/(a_1,…,a_{i-1})M and M≠(a_1,…,a_n)M.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Regular/IsSMulRegular.lean::IsSMulRegular; Mathlib/RingTheory/Regular/RegularSequence.lean::IsRegular, isRegular_iff — IsSMulRegular M a is injectivity of multiplication by a; Sequence.IsRegular M rs is exactly successive regularity on the quotient by earlier terms together with the final nontriviality condition.

For Noetherian A and M≠0, if the successive quotients by a_1,…,a_n are I-adically separated, then an M-quasi-regular sequence is M-regular. The hypothesis holds for finite M with I⊆rad A and in the stated positive-degree graded case.

Corollary: permutation invariance under separatedness hypotheses

FC13-C06-U008 · §16, p. 127 · after FC13-C06-U001, FC13-C06-U007 · not formalized

Under either hypothesis of Theorem 16.3, every permutation of an M-sequence is again an M-sequence.

Not formalized. Construction: Koszul complex

FC13-C06-U010 · §16, pp. 127–128 · not formalized

For x_1,…,x_n∈A, K_•(x) has K_p free on p-element subsets and differential d(e_{i_1…i_p})=Σ_r(-1)^{r-1}x_{i_r}e_{i_1…hat{i_r}…i_p}. Put K_•(x,M)=K_•(x)⊗M; tensor products identify K(x_1,…,x_n) with the tensor of the one-variable complexes, hence it is permutation invariant up to isomorphism.

Not formalized. Open Mathlib PR #34913 defines the ring-valued Koszul complex koszulComplex.ofList, exterior-power differential, base change, and append decomposition. Matsumura U010 also includes K(x,M)=K(x)⊗M for an arbitrary A-module M and its tensor-product/permutation package; no compatible declaration for that coefficient-module bundle was found, so the whole row remains unmatched.

Construction: Koszul homology

FC13-C06-U011 · §16, p. 128 · after FC13-C06-U010 · not formalized

Theorem: Theorem 16.4

FC13-C06-U012 · §16, p. 128 · after FC13-C06-U010 · not formalized

For a complex C_• and x∈A there is a short exact sequence of complexes 0→C_•→C_•(x)→C_•[1]→0, with the resulting homology long exact sequence; multiplication by x annihilates H_p(C_•(x)).

Corollary: Koszul homology is annihilated by the sequence ideal

FC13-C06-U013 · §16, p. 128 · after FC13-C06-U012 · not formalized

Theorem: Theorem 16.5

FC13-C06-U014 · §16, pp. 128–129 · after FC13-C06-U001, FC13-C06-U011, FC13-C06-U012 · not formalized

If x is an M-sequence then H_p(x,M)=0 for p>0 and H_0=M/(x)M. Conversely, for local finite modules with x_i in the maximal ideal, or in the stated positive graded case, vanishing of H_1(x,M) with M≠0 implies that x is an M-sequence.

Not formalized. PR #34913 proves koszulComplex.exactAt_of_isRegular and zeroHomologyOfListLinearEquiv, giving acyclicity/H₀ for a regular sequence in the ring. The source theorem is for an M-sequence on an arbitrary finite module and also includes local/graded converses from H₁=0. Those missing clauses keep the row unmatched.

Definition: I-depth and local depth

FC13-C06-U018 · §16, pp. 130–131 · after FC13-C06-U017 · in another Lean project

The common length in Theorem 16.7 is depth(I,M)=inf{i | Ext_A^i(A/I,M)≠0}; if IM=M it is ∞. In a Noetherian local ring, depth M means depth(m,M)=inf{i | Ext_A^i(k,M)≠0}.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Depth/Basic.lean::Ideal.depth, moduleDepth, IsLocalRing.depth — PR #26218 defines ideal depth via Ext and local depth via the maximal ideal/residue field, matching the source conventions including the top/infinite case.

Theorem: Theorem 16.8 (depth sensitivity of Koszul homology)

FC13-C06-U019 · §16, p. 131 · after FC13-C06-U011, FC13-C06-U018 · not formalized

If I=(y_1,…,y_n), M finite and IM≠M, and q=sup{i | H_i(y,M)≠0}, then depth(I,M)=n-q. Thus y is an M-sequence iff depth(I,M)=n.
