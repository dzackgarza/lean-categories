### Definition: flat and faithfully flat module {#fc13-c03-u001}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC13-C03-U001` · §7, p. 45 · [`mathlib`]{.route-mathlib}
:::

An A-module M is flat if tensoring every exact sequence with M preserves exactness. It is faithfully flat if a sequence is exact exactly when its tensor with M is exact; it suffices to test short exact sequences, or injectivity at the left end.

::: {.unit-lean}
**In Lean.** [`Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/Basic.lean::Module.Flat, iff_rTensor_exact, iff_lTensor_exact`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/Basic.html#Module.Flat); [`Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/FaithfullyFlat/Basic.lean::Module.FaithfullyFlat, rTensor_exact_iff_exact, lTensor_exact_iff_exact`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/FaithfullyFlat/Basic.html#Module.FaithfullyFlat) — Flatness is exactly preservation of tensor exactness; faithful flatness extends flatness and reflects exactness, with the pinned iff theorems giving Matsumura’s preservation/reflection formulation.
:::
