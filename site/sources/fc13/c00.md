### Convention: rings and homomorphisms {#fc13-c00-u001}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC13-C00-U001` · Conventions and terminology, PDF p. 9 · [in Mathlib]{.route-mathlib}
:::

Throughout, a ring is commutative with identity; ring homomorphisms preserve 1, and subrings share the same identity.

::: {.unit-lean}
**Formalized.** [`Mathlib/Algebra/Ring/Defs.lean::CommRing`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/Algebra/Ring/Defs.html); [`Mathlib/Algebra/Ring/Hom/Defs.lean::RingHom`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/Algebra/Ring/Hom/Defs.html); [`Mathlib/Algebra/Ring/Subring/Defs.lean::Subring`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/Algebra/Ring/Subring/Defs.html) — `CommRing` is the commutative ring structure with identity; `RingHom` is the bundled unital ring homomorphism (through its multiplicative-hom component); and the pinned `Subring` definition explicitly shares the ambient 0 and 1. Together these are exactly Matsumura’s standing convention.
:::

### Notation: extension and contraction of ideals {#fc13-c00-u002}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC13-C00-U002` · Conventions and terminology, PDF p. 9 · [in lean-categories]{.route-project-existing}
:::

For f:A→B and J⊲B, write A∩J=f⁻¹(J); for I⊲A, write IB=f(I)B.

::: {.unit-lean}
**Formalized.** [`LeanCategories/Algebra/Ideals.lean::LeanCategories.Algebra.idealMapOrderHom`](https://github.com/dzackgarza/lean-categories/blob/main/LeanCategories/Algebra/Ideals.lean), [`LeanCategories.Algebra.idealComapOrderHom`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/find/?pattern=LeanCategories.Algebra.idealComapOrderHom#doc), [`LeanCategories.Algebra.idealMapFunctor_obj`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/find/?pattern=LeanCategories.Algebra.idealMapFunctor_obj#doc), [`LeanCategories.Algebra.idealComapFunctor_obj`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/find/?pattern=LeanCategories.Algebra.idealComapFunctor_obj#doc) — At project HEAD, extension along `f : A →+* B` is definitionally `Ideal.map f I` and contraction is `Ideal.comap f J`; the object theorems expose those equalities. Pinned Mathlib defines [`Ideal.map`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/find/?pattern=Ideal.map#doc) as the ideal span of the image and [`Ideal.comap`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/find/?pattern=Ideal.comap#doc) as the preimage, so this project-owned pair is exactly the source `IB=f(I)B` / `f⁻¹(J)` notation semantics.
:::

### Notation: generated ideals {#fc13-c00-u003}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC13-C00-U003` · Conventions and terminology, PDF p. 9 · [in Mathlib]{.route-mathlib}
:::

The ideal generated by a₁,…,aₙ may be written ΣaᵢA or (a₁,…,aₙ).

::: {.unit-lean}
**Formalized.** [`Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Span.lean::Ideal.span`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Span.html#Ideal.span), [`Ideal.span_insert`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/find/?pattern=Ideal.span_insert#doc) — `Ideal.span s` is explicitly the ideal generated by a subset `s`; [`Ideal.span_insert`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/find/?pattern=Ideal.span_insert#doc) identifies adjoining one generator with the join of the corresponding principal ideal and the previous span. Iterating over a finite generator set gives exactly the source ideal `(a₁,…,aₙ)=Σ aᵢA`.
:::

### Convention: inclusion and chains {#fc13-c00-u004}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC13-C00-U004` · Conventions and terminology, PDF p. 9 · [in Mathlib]{.route-mathlib}
:::

The inclusion symbol permits equality; an “ascending chain” M₁⊂M₂⊂⋯ is intended to be strictly increasing unless stated otherwise.

::: {.unit-lean}
**Formalized.** [`Mathlib/Data/Set/Defs.lean::Set.Subset`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/Data/Set/Defs.html#Set.Subset); [`Mathlib/Order/Monotone/Defs.lean::StrictMono`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/Order/Monotone/Defs.html) — [`Set.Subset`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/find/?pattern=Set.Subset#doc) (and the induced subobject order `≤`) is non-strict and therefore permits equality, while `StrictMono f` requires `a<b → f a<f b`. Thus a module/ideal chain represented by a strictly monotone indexed family has exactly the source “ascending chain means strictly increasing” convention.
:::

### Convention: positive characteristic {#fc13-c00-u005}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC13-C00-U005` · Conventions and terminology, PDF p. 9 · [in Mathlib]{.route-mathlib}
:::

Writing char R=p means p>0 is a prime number.

::: {.unit-lean}
**Formalized.** [`Mathlib/Algebra/CharP/Defs.lean::CharP`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/Algebra/CharP/Defs.html); [`Mathlib/Data/Nat/Prime/Defs.lean::Nat.Prime`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/Data/Nat/Prime/Defs.html#Nat.Prime) — `CharP R p` records that the characteristic is `p`; the additional hypothesis `Nat.Prime p` says `p≥2`, hence positive and prime. The conjunction, rather than `CharP` alone, exactly represents Matsumura’s convention when the symbol `p` is used for positive characteristic.
:::
