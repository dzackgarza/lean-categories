Definition: localisation / ring of fractions

FC13-C02-U001 · §4, pp. 20–21 · after FC13-C01-U010 · in Mathlib

For a multiplicative set S⊆A, a localisation A_S is characterized by a canonical ring map A→A_S taking S to units and initial among ring maps with that property; it is unique up to unique isomorphism.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/Defs.lean::IsLocalization, isLocalization_iff, lift, lift_unique; Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/Basic.lean::algEquiv — IsLocalization is the source localization predicate; lift/lift_unique give the initial mapping property, and algEquiv gives uniqueness up to the canonical algebra isomorphism.

Construction: fractions construction of AS

FC13-C02-U002 · §4, p. 21 · after FC13-C02-U001 · in Mathlib

On A×S set (a,s)∼(b,s′) iff t(s′a−sb)=0 for some t∈S. The equivalence classes a/s form A_S with the usual fraction addition and multiplication, and a↦a/1 is the canonical map.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/Defs.lean::Localization M S, mk_eq_of_mk', mk — The canonical Localization S is the quotient-type fraction construction; its mk/mk' API realizes the source numerator-denominator classes and ring operations.

Theorem: kernel and injectivity of localisation

FC13-C02-U003 · §4, p. 21 · after FC13-C02-U002 · in Mathlib

The kernel of A→A_S is {a∈A | sa=0 for some s∈S}; hence the canonical map is injective iff S contains no zero-divisors.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/Defs.lean::map_eq_zero_iff, injective, nonZeroDivisors — map_eq_zero_iff gives the annihilation-by-a-denominator kernel. IsLocalization.injective gives injectivity when every denominator is a non-zero-divisor; conversely injectivity plus invertibility of denominators forces the same regularity condition.

Definition: total ring and field of fractions

FC13-C02-U004 · §4, p. 21 · after FC13-C01-U005, FC13-C02-U003 · in Mathlib

Localising at the multiplicative set of all non-zero-divisors gives the total ring of fractions. For an integral domain this is its field of fractions.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/FractionRing.lean::IsFractionRing, FractionRing — IsFractionRing R K is localization at nonZeroDivisors R; for domains FractionRing R is the field-of-fractions specialization.

Definition: extension and contraction of ideals

FC13-C02-U005 · §4, pp. 21–22 · after FC13-C00-U002 · in Mathlib

For f:A→B, the extension of I⊲A is I^e=IB=f(I)B and the contraction of J⊲B is J^c=f⁻¹(J). One has I{ece}=Ie and J{cec}=Jc, giving a bijection between extended ideals and contracted ideals.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Maps.lean::Ideal.map, Ideal.comap, map_comap_map, comap_map_comap — Ideal.map/Ideal.comap are extension and contraction. map_comap_map and comap_map_comap are exactly the ECE/CEC identities, hence identify the images of the two operators.

Lemma: contraction preserves prime and primary ideals

FC13-C02-U006 · §4, pp. 21–22 · after FC13-C01-U008, FC13-C02-U005 · in Mathlib

The contraction of a prime ideal is prime. The contraction of a primary ideal is primary.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Maps.lean::comap_isPrime; Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/IsPrimary.lean::IsPrimary.comap — Prime ideals contract to prime ideals and primary ideals contract to primary ideals along arbitrary ring homomorphisms.

Definition: primary ideal

FC13-C02-U007 · §4, p. 21 · after FC13-C01-U012 · in Mathlib

A proper ideal J is primary if xy∈J and x∉J imply y^n∈J for some n>0; equivalently every zero-divisor of B/J is nilpotent.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/IsPrimary.lean::isPrimary_iff; Mathlib/RingTheory/IsPrimary.lean::isPrimary_iff_zero_divisor_quotient_imp_nilpotent_smul — The elementwise power criterion is the source primary definition, and the quotient theorem identifies it with nilpotence of every zero-divisor on the quotient.

Theorem: Theorem 4.1

FC13-C02-U008 · §4, p. 22 · after FC13-C02-U001, FC13-C02-U005, FC13-C02-U006, FC13-C02-U007 · not formalized

Every ideal of A_S is IA_S for an ideal I⊲A. Prime ideals of A_S correspond exactly to prime ideals p⊲A disjoint from S by p↦pA_S; the same extension/contraction statement holds for primary ideals.

Not formalized. It does not prove that extension of such a primary ideal is primary, so the full prime-and-primary Theorem 4.1 bundle is not credited. Open/current/external search found no exact owner.

Example: principal localisation Aa

FC13-C02-U010 · §4, p. 22 · after FC13-C02-U008 · in Mathlib

Example: local ring Ap

Definition: saturation of a multiplicative set

FC13-C02-U013 · §4, p. 23 · after FC13-C02-U001 · in Mathlib

The saturation S~= {a∈A | ab∈S for some b∈A} is multiplicative; A_S=A_{S~}, and S~ is maximal among T with A_T=A_S. Equivalently a∈S~ iff a/1 is a unit in A_S.

Theorem: Theorem 4.2

FC13-C02-U014 · §4, p. 23 · after FC13-C01-U001, FC13-C02-U001 · in Mathlib

Localisation commutes with quotients: for I⊲A and the image S̄⊆A/I, A_S/IA_S ≅ (A/I)_{S̄}.

Definition: residue field κ(p)
