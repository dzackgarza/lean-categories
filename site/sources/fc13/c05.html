Definition: graded rings, graded modules and homogeneous elements

FC13-C05-U001 · §13, pp. 92–93 · in Mathlib

For an abelian semigroup G with identity, a G-graded ring is a direct sum R=⊕{i∈G}R_i with R_iR_j⊆R{i+j}; a graded module is M=⊕M_i with R_iM_j⊆M_{i+j}. Elements in a single M_i are homogeneous of degree i, and every element has unique homogeneous terms.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/GradedAlgebra/Basic.lean::GradedRing; Mathlib/Algebra/DirectSum/Decomposition.lean::Decomposition; Mathlib/Algebra/GradedMulAction.lean::SetLike.GradedSMul — GradedRing is a direct-sum decomposition with graded multiplication; a module decomposition together with GradedSMul gives the graded module condition, and DirectSum.Decomposition supplies unique homogeneous components.

Searched: P=db584cd6d46c92f209a44c0f1c829460d327499d

Definition: homogeneous submodule

FC13-C05-U002 · §13, p. 92 · after FC13-C05-U001 · in Mathlib

A submodule N of a graded module M is homogeneous if generated by homogeneous elements; equivalently every homogeneous term of every x∈N lies in N, equivalently N=⊕(N∩M_i).

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/GradedAlgebra/Homogeneous/Submodule.lean::HomogeneousSubmodule, Submodule.IsHomogeneous.mem_iff — HomogeneousSubmodule bundles a submodule generated/componentwise stable under homogeneous pieces; mem_iff is exactly the criterion that every homogeneous component of x lies in N.

Construction: graded quotients and the irrelevant ideal

FC13-C05-U003 · §13, p. 93 · after FC13-C05-U001, FC13-C05-U002 · not formalized

For homogeneous N, M/N inherits the grading (M/N)i=M_i/(N∩M_i). For an N-graded ring, R+=⊕{n>0}R_n is an ideal and R/R+≅R_0.

Definition: filtration and associated graded ring

FC13-C05-U005 · §13, p. 93 · after FC13-C03-U031, FC13-C03-U033 · not formalized

A filtration A=J_0⊇J_1⊇⋯ with J_iJ_j⊆J_{i+j} defines gr(A)=⊕{n≥0}J_n/J{n+1}; a completion for the filtration has canonically the same associated graded ring.

Not formalized. Open PRs #33218–#33220 define generic associated graded rings/modules for filtrations, but no checked compatible theorem identifies a filtration completion with the same associated graded ring. Whole-bundle semantics therefore does not credit the definition-only PR.

Construction: I-adic associated graded ring

FC13-C05-U006 · §13, p. 93 · after FC13-C05-U005 · not formalized

For I⊲A, gr_I(A)=⊕_{n≥0}In/I{n+1}; it is generated over A/I by I/I², and if I=(x_1,…,x_r) it is a graded quotient of (A/I)[X_1,…,X_r].

Not formalized. The adic filtration and open associated-graded PRs supply ingredients, but no pinned/current/open declaration proves the complete bundle gr_I(A)=⊕I^n/I^{n+1}, generation over A/I by I/I², and the graded polynomial quotient for finite generators.

Theorem: Theorem 13.1

FC13-C05-U007 · §13, pp. 93–94 · after FC13-C01-U052, FC13-C05-U001, FC13-C05-U003 · in another Lean project

An N-graded ring R is Noetherian iff R_0 is Noetherian and R is finitely generated as an R_0-algebra by homogeneous elements of positive degree.

Formalized. The reverse direction is standard finite-type Noetherianity. PR head is Apache-2.0 Mathlib on Lean 4.9.0-rc3, so it is a reference to port, not an import route.

Theorem: Theorem 13.2

FC13-C05-U010 · §13, pp. 94–95 · after FC13-C05-U007, FC13-C05-U009 · in another Lean project

If R is Noetherian graded with R_0 Artinian, M finite graded, and R=R_0[x_1,…,x_r] with deg x_i=d_i, then P(M,t)=f(t)/∏_i(1-t^{d_i}) for some f(t)∈Z[t].

For a⊲A, set a′=aA[t,u]∩R(A,I). Distinct ideals remain distinct; primes and primary ideals remain prime/primary; primary decompositions of (0) induce primary decompositions in R, and minimal primes correspond.
