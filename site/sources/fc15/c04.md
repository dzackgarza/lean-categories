### Definition: affine group and translations {#fc15-c04-u001}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC15-C04-U001` · §4.1; p. 87
:::

Aff(V)=GL(V)⋉V is the affine group. Translation by λ is t(λ):μ↦μ+λ, and g t(λ) g^{-1}=t(gλ).

### Definition: affine root hyperplanes and affine reflections {#fc15-c04-u002}

For root α and k∈Z, H_{α,k}={λ | (λ,α)=k}; the corresponding affine reflection is s_{α,k}(λ)=λ−((λ,α)−k)α∨=t(kα∨)s_α.

### Proposition: affine hyperplanes under Weyl transformations and translations {#fc15-c04-u003}

### Definition: separating-hyperplane set C(w) and n(w) {#fc15-c04-u016}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC15-C04-U016` · §4.4; p. 91 · after [`FC15-C04-U010`](#fc15-c04-u010)
:::

C(w) is the finite set of affine root hyperplanes separating A_0 from wA_0; n(w)=|C(w)|. One has n(1)=0, n(s)=1 for s∈S_a, and n(w^{-1})=n(w).

### Proposition: change of n(w) under a simple affine reflection {#fc15-c04-u017}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC15-C04-U017` · §4.4; pp. 91–92 · after [`FC15-C04-U016`](#fc15-c04-u016)
:::

For w∈W_a,s∈S_a, the wall H_s belongs to exactly one of C(w^{-1}),C(sw^{-1}); reflection in H_s pairs all other separating hyperplanes. Consequently n(ws)=n(w)±1 with the sign determined by membership of H_s in C(w^{-1}).

### Theorem/recorded result: general discrete affine reflection groups are Coxeter groups {#fc15-c04-u029}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC15-C04-U029` · §4.10; pp. 99–100 · after [`FC15-C04-U001`](#fc15-c04-u001)
:::

For a locally finite/proper discrete affine reflection arrangement, the group G generated by wall reflections acts simply transitively on chambers, is generated by the finitely many wall reflections of a fixed chamber, and with these generators is a Coxeter system.

### Theorem/recorded classification: irreducible infinite discrete affine reflection groups are affine Weyl groups {#fc15-c04-u030}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC15-C04-U030` · §4.10; p. 100 · after [`FC15-C02-U018`](c02.html#fc15-c02-u018), [`FC15-C04-U029`](#fc15-c04-u029)
:::

An infinite irreducible discrete group generated by affine reflections contains a full-rank translation lattice equal to the root lattice of a crystallographic root system; the stabilizer of a special point is its finite Weyl group. Hence every such group is an affine Weyl group.
