### Proposition: decomposition into irreducible Coxeter systems {#fc15-c06-u002}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC15-C06-U002` · §6.1; pp. 129–130 · after [`FC15-C05-U015`](c05.html#fc15-c05-u015), [`FC15-C06-U001`](#fc15-c06-u001)

If the Coxeter graph has connected components with vertex sets S_1,…,S_r, then W is the direct product W_{S_1}×⋯×W_{S_r}, with each factor irreducible.

### Proposition: invariant subspaces in an irreducible Coxeter representation {#fc15-c06-u006}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC15-C06-U006` · §6.3; pp. 131–132 · after [`FC15-C06-U001`](#fc15-c06-u001), [`FC15-C06-U005`](#fc15-c06-u005)

For irreducible (W,S): every proper W-stable subspace is contained in rad B; if B is degenerate the geometric module is not completely reducible; if B is nondegenerate it is irreducible; and its commuting endomorphism algebra consists only of scalars.

### Lemma: finite real group representations {#fc15-c06-u007}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC15-C06-U007` · §6.4; p. 132 · after [`FC15-C03-U003`](c03.html#fc15-c03-u003)
:::

For a finite group representation on finite-dimensional real E: averaging gives a positive-definite invariant symmetric form; the module is completely reducible; and if the commutant is scalar, any two nondegenerate invariant symmetric forms are scalar multiples.

### Theorem: characterization of finite Coxeter groups {#fc15-c06-u008}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC15-C06-U008` · §6.4; pp. 132–133 · after [`FC15-C02-U009`](c02.html#fc15-c02-u009), [`FC15-C05-U009`](c05.html#fc15-c05-u009), [`FC15-C06-U004`](#fc15-c06-u004), [`FC15-C06-U006`](#fc15-c06-u006), [`FC15-C06-U007`](#fc15-c06-u007)
:::

The following are equivalent: W is finite; the geometric bilinear form B is positive definite; W in its geometric representation is a finite reflection group in the sense of Part I.

### Construction/comparison: affine Coxeter representation as an affine Weyl action {#fc15-c06-u009}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC15-C06-U009` · §6.5; pp. 133–135 · after [`FC15-C02-U010`](c02.html#fc15-c02-u010), [`FC15-C04-U011`](c04.html#fc15-c04-u011), [`FC15-C05-U009`](c05.html#fc15-c05-u009), [`FC15-C06-U005`](#fc15-c06-u005)
:::

For an irreducible positive-semidefinite non-positive-definite Coxeter graph, rad B is one-dimensional, fixed by W. In the dual, the affine hyperplane (f,λ)=1 carries a Euclidean structure on which the simple generators act as affine reflections, recovering the affine-Weyl configuration and fundamental alcove of Chapter 4.

### Definition: crystallographic Coxeter group relative to the geometric representation {#fc15-c06-u010}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC15-C06-U010` · §6.6; p. 135 · after [`FC15-C05-U009`](c05.html#fc15-c05-u009)
:::

W is crystallographic relative to σ if the geometric representation stabilizes a lattice in V.

### Necessary condition: edge labels and circuit parity for crystallographic Coxeter groups {#fc15-c06-u011}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC15-C06-U011` · §6.6; pp. 135–136 · after [`FC15-C06-U010`](#fc15-c06-u010)
:::

Crystallographicity forces m(s,t)∈{2,3,4,6,∞}; moreover every circuit in the Coxeter graph must contain an even number of label-4 edges and an even number of label-6 edges.

### Construction: root-rescaling lattice for crystallographic Coxeter groups {#fc15-c06-u012}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC15-C06-U012` · §6.6; p. 136 · after [`FC15-C05-U007`](c05.html#fc15-c05-u007), [`FC15-C06-U011`](#fc15-c06-u011)
:::

Scalars c_s can be chosen so λ_s=c_sα_s span a W-stable lattice when along labels 3,4,6,∞ the ratios are respectively 1,√2 or 1/√2,√3 or 1/√3,1. Circuit parity is exactly the consistency condition.

### Proposition: criterion for crystallographicity of a Coxeter group {#fc15-c06-u013}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC15-C06-U013` · §6.6; pp. 136–137 · after [`FC15-C06-U011`](#fc15-c06-u011), [`FC15-C06-U012`](#fc15-c06-u012)
:::

If the Coxeter graph is a tree, W is crystallographic relative to σ iff all labels lie in {2,3,4,6,∞}. With circuits, this label condition plus even parity of label-4 and label-6 edges on every circuit is necessary and sufficient.

### Remark/comparison: Kac–Moody crystallographic convention {#fc15-c06-u014}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC15-C06-U014` · §6.6; p. 137 · after [`FC15-C06-U013`](#fc15-c06-u013)
:::

The common literature convention calls a Coxeter group crystallographic whenever all labels lie in {2,3,4,6,∞}; these are exactly Weyl groups of Kac–Moody Lie algebras from generalized Cartan matrices, where m_ij=2,3,4,6,∞ according as a_ij a_ji=0,1,2,3,≥4.

### Classification/result: rank-three Coxeter signatures {#fc15-c06-u015}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC15-C06-U015` · §6.7; pp. 137–138 · after [`FC15-C02-U015`](c02.html#fc15-c02-u015), [`FC15-C06-U008`](#fc15-c06-u008)
:::

For connected rank-three graphs, finite types give positive-definite B, affine types positive semidefinite B, and all other cases signature (2,1). For triangular labels m,n,p, the sum 1/m+1/n+1/p is >1,=1,<1 exactly in the spherical, Euclidean, hyperbolic cases.

### Definition: hyperbolic Coxeter system {#fc15-c06-u016}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC15-C06-U016` · §6.8; pp. 138–139 · after [`FC15-C05-U034`](c05.html#fc15-c05-u034), [`FC15-C06-U001`](#fc15-c06-u001)
:::

An irreducible Coxeter system of rank n is hyperbolic when B has signature (n−1,1) and B(λ,λ)<0 for every λ in the open Tits chamber C.

### Lemma: hyperplane restriction criterion in signature (n−1,1) {#fc15-c06-u017}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC15-C06-U017` · §6.8; p. 139 · after [`FC15-C06-U016`](#fc15-c06-u016)
:::

For nonzero λ in a real quadratic space of signature (n−1,1), the restriction of B to λ^⊥ is of positive type iff B(λ,λ)≤0 (with positive definiteness when <0).

### Proposition: graph criterion for hyperbolic Coxeter systems {#fc15-c06-u018}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC15-C06-U018` · §6.8; pp. 139–140 · after [`FC15-C02-U007`](c02.html#fc15-c02-u007), [`FC15-C06-U017`](#fc15-c06-u017)
:::

Irreducible (W,S) is hyperbolic iff B is nondegenerate but not positive definite and deleting any one vertex from the Coxeter graph leaves a graph of positive type.

### Theorem/recorded result: finite covolume criterion for Coxeter lattices {#fc15-c06-u019}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC15-C06-U019` · §6.8; p. 140 · after [`FC15-C06-U003`](#fc15-c06-u003), [`FC15-C06-U008`](#fc15-c06-u008), [`FC15-C06-U016`](#fc15-c06-u016), [`FC15-C06-U018`](#fc15-c06-u018)
:::

For nondegenerate B, the discrete group W≤O(V) has finite Haar covolume iff B is positive definite or W is hyperbolic in the above sense.

### Definition: uniform/compact hyperbolic Coxeter group {#fc15-c06-u020}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC15-C06-U020` · §6.8; p. 140 · after [`FC15-C06-U019`](#fc15-c06-u019)
:::

A hyperbolic Coxeter lattice is uniform/co-compact when O(V)/W is compact.

### Theorem/recorded result: compact-hyperbolic graph criterion {#fc15-c06-u021}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC15-C06-U021` · §6.8; p. 140 · after [`FC15-C06-U018`](#fc15-c06-u018), [`FC15-C06-U020`](#fc15-c06-u020)
:::

W is compact hyperbolic iff B is nondegenerate but not positive definite and deleting any one Coxeter-graph vertex yields a positive-definite graph.

### Classification/result: rank bounds for hyperbolic Coxeter groups {#fc15-c06-u022}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC15-C06-U022` · §6.9; pp. 141–144 · after [`FC15-C06-U018`](#fc15-c06-u018), [`FC15-C06-U021`](#fc15-c06-u021)
:::

Hyperbolic Coxeter systems of simplex type exist only in ranks 3 through 10 and only finitely many in each rank 4–10; compact hyperbolic systems exist only in ranks 3,4,5. Figures 2–3 list the rank≥4 cases.
