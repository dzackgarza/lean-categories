Definition: Element integral over a subring

FC04-C05-U001 · Integral dependence; pp. 59–60; L2284–2290 · after FC04-C01-U005, FC04-C01-U001 · in Mathlib

For A⊂B, x∈B is integral over A if it satisfies a monic polynomial xn+a₁x{n−1}+⋯+a_n=0 with a_i∈A.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/IntegralClosure/IsIntegral/Defs.lean::RingHom.IsIntegralElem; IsIntegral — Integrality is existence of a monic polynomial over the base whose evaluation at the element is zero, exactly the source definition.

Example 5.0: Rational numbers integral over Z

FC04-C05-U002 · Integral dependence; pp. 59–60; L2292–2298 · after FC04-C05-U001 · in Mathlib

A rational r/s in lowest terms integral over Z must have s=±1; hence the elements of Q integral over Z are exactly Z.

Formalized. Mathlib/NumberTheory/Niven.lean::IsIntegral.exists_int_iff_exists_rat; Rat.den_eq_one_iff; Rat.den_div_intCast_eq_one_iff — Specializing the concrete rational/integer theorem to x=q:ℚ gives integral rational ⇒ integer; integer casts are integral. The denominator lemmas recover the source reduced-fraction conclusion that the denominator is a unit, hence ±1.

Proposition 5.1: Equivalent criteria for integrality

FC04-C05-U003 · Integral dependence; pp. 59–60; L2300–2320 · after FC04-C02-U020, FC04-C02-U022, FC04-C02-U029, FC04-C05-U001 · not formalized

x is integral over A iff A[x] is finite as an A-module; equivalently it lies in a finite A-subring and there exists a faithful A[x]-module finite over A. The extraction omits displayed clause (iii), but lines 2316–2318 identify it as the intermediate finite A-subring criterion.

Not formalized. partial: Algebra.finite_adjoin_simple_of_isIntegral; Algebra.IsIntegral.of_finite; IsIntegral.of_mem_of_fg; LinearMap.exists_monic_and_aeval_eq_zero — P/H realize the finite-adjoin and finite-intermediate-subalgebra criteria, but no checked theorem gives the source converse from existence of a faithful A[x]-module finite over arbitrary commutative A. Corollary 5.3: Integral elements form a subring

FC04-C05-U005 · Integral dependence; pp. 59–60; L2326–2328 · after FC04-C05-U003, FC04-C05-U004 · in Mathlib

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/IntegralClosure/Algebra/Basic.lean::integralClosure; mem_integralClosure_iff — integralClosure A B is the subalgebra whose carrier is exactly the elements integral over A; its structure supplies closure under ring operations and contains the base image.

Definition: Integral closure of A in B

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/IntegralClosure/Algebra/Basic.lean::integralClosure; mem_integralClosure_iff — Exactly the subring/subalgebra of ambient elements integral over the base.

Definition: A integrally closed in B

FC04-C05-U007 · Integral dependence; pp. 59–60; L2330 · after FC04-C05-U006 · in Mathlib

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/IntegralClosure/IntegrallyClosed.lean::IsIntegrallyClosedIn.integralClosure_eq_bot_iff; isIntegrallyClosedIn_iff — For an injective inclusion, being integrally closed in the ambient ring is equivalent to the integral closure being the embedded base ring (⊥ as a subalgebra).

Definition: B integral over A

FC04-C05-U008 · Integral dependence; pp. 59–60; L2330 · after FC04-C05-U001, FC04-C05-U006 · in Mathlib

Formalized. Algebra.IsIntegral; Mathlib/RingTheory/IntegralClosure/IsIntegralClosure/Basic.lean::integralClosure_eq_top_iff — Every target element is integral over the source iff the integral closure is top, exactly “B integral over A”.

Definition: Integral ring homomorphism / integral A-algebra

Comparison: Finite type + integral = finite

FC04-C05-U010 · Integral dependence; pp. 59–60; L2332–2336 · after FC04-C02-U066, FC04-C02-U067, FC04-C05-U004, FC04-C05-U009 · in Mathlib

Corollary 5.4: Transitivity of integral dependence

Corollary 5.5: Integral closure is integrally closed in the ambient ring

Proposition 5.7: Field criterion for integral domain extensions

Corollary 5.9: Incomparability over one contracted prime

Theorem 5.10: Lying over

Theorem 5.11: Going-up theorem

Proposition 5.12: Integral closure commutes with localization

Reusable comparison: UFDs and polynomial rings are integrally closed
