Definition: Multiplicatively closed subset

FC04-C03-U001 · Rings and Modules of Fractions; pp. 36–39; L1483 · after FC04-C01-U001 · in Mathlib

S⊂A with 1∈S and closed under multiplication.

Formalized. Submonoid R — A submonoid contains 1 and is closed under multiplication, exactly the source multiplicatively closed subset.

Construction: Ring of fractions S⁻¹A

FC04-C03-U002 · Rings and Modules of Fractions; pp. 36–39; L1483–1493 · after FC04-C03-U001, FC04-C01-U001 · in Mathlib

On A×S set (a,s)~(b,t) iff (at−bs)u=0 for some u∈S; equivalence classes a/s receive the usual fraction addition and multiplication.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/Defs.lean::Localization; Localization.mk — The quotient relation on numerator/denominator pairs is the source cross-multiplication relation up to the standard witness orientation, and the quotient carries the usual ring operations.

Construction: Canonical localization map A→S⁻¹A

FC04-C03-U003 · Rings and Modules of Fractions; pp. 36–39; L1497 · after FC04-C01-U004, FC04-C03-U002 · in Mathlib

f(x)=x/1; it need not be injective.

Formalized. algebraMap R (Localization S); Localization.mk_one; IsLocalization.subsingleton — The canonical map is x ↦ x/1. If 0 ∈ S, every localization is subsingleton, so for any nontrivial base ring the map cannot be injective; this realizes the source warning that injectivity need not hold.

Reusable comparison: Field of fractions as a localization

FC04-C03-U004 · Rings and Modules of Fractions; pp. 36–39; L1499 · after FC04-C01-U013, FC04-C03-U001, FC04-C03-U002 · in Mathlib

If A is a domain and S=A−{0}, then S⁻¹A is the field of fractions of A.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/FractionRing.lean::FractionRing; IsFractionRing; IsLocalization at nonZeroDivisors — For a domain, localization at the nonzero elements is its fraction field.

Definition: Ring of fractions

FC04-C03-U005 · Rings and Modules of Fractions; pp. 36–39; L1501 · after FC04-C03-U002 · in Mathlib

S⁻¹A is called the ring of fractions of A with respect to S.

Formalized. Localization S; IsLocalization S — Mathlib uses the same ring-of-fractions object, parameterized by the localization submonoid.

Proposition 3.1: Universal property of localization

FC04-C03-U006 · Rings and Modules of Fractions; pp. 36–39; L1503–1519 · after FC04-C01-U004, FC04-C01-U015, FC04-C03-U003 · in Mathlib

If g:A→B sends every s∈S to a unit, there is a unique h:S⁻¹A→B with g=h∘f, necessarily h(a/s)=g(a)g(s)⁻¹.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/Basic.lean::IsLocalization.lift; lift_mk'; ringHom_ext — A map sending denominators to units extends uniquely, and lift_mk' gives the formula g(a) g(s)⁻¹.

Characterization: Three structural properties of S⁻¹A

FC04-C03-U007 · Rings and Modules of Fractions; pp. 36–39; L1521–1531 · after FC04-C03-U003, FC04-C03-U006 · in Mathlib

The canonical map sends S to units; kernel elements are annihilated by some s∈S; every localized element is f(a)f(s)⁻¹. These characterize S⁻¹A up to isomorphism.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/Defs.lean::IsLocalization; map_units; map_eq_zero_iff; surj — The typeclass records exactly the three source properties: denominators become units, kernel elements are annihilated by a denominator, and every element is a fraction.

Corollary 3.2: Recognition criterion for a localization

FC04-C03-U008 · Rings and Modules of Fractions; pp. 36–39; L1533–1547 · after FC04-C03-U006, FC04-C03-U007 · in Mathlib

A map g:A→B satisfying the three localization properties identifies B uniquely with S⁻¹A.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/Basic.lean::IsLocalization.algEquiv; Localization.algEquiv; isLocalization_of_algEquiv — Any algebra satisfying the localization structure is uniquely isomorphic over the base ring to the canonical localization.

Construction / definition: Localization A𝔭 at a prime

FC04-C03-U009 · Rings and Modules of Fractions; pp. 36–39; L1549–1551 · after FC04-C01-U020, FC04-C01-U028, FC04-C03-U001, FC04-C03-U002 · in Mathlib

For prime 𝔭 take S=A−𝔭 and write A_𝔭=S⁻¹A; its fractions with numerator in 𝔭 form the unique maximal ideal, so A_𝔭 is local. Passing A→A_𝔭 is localization at 𝔭.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/AtPrime/Basic.lean::Localization.AtPrime; IsLocalization.AtPrime.under_maximalIdeal; AtPrime.map_eq_maximalIdeal; local-ring instance — Localization at the prime complement is local, with unique maximal ideal the extension of the prime.

Notation / construction: Principal localization Af

FC04-C03-U010 · Rings and Modules of Fractions; pp. 36–39; L1556 · after FC04-C03-U001, FC04-C03-U002 · in Mathlib

For S={f^n}_{n≥0}, write A_f=S⁻¹A.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/Away/Basic.lean::Localization.Away; Submonoid.powers — Localization.Away f is localization at the powers of f, exactly A_f.

Reusable example: Localization at 1+𝔞

For any ideal 𝔞, S=1+𝔞 is multiplicatively closed.

Reusable example: Localizations of Z

FC04-C03-U012 · Rings and Modules of Fractions; pp. 36–39; L1567 · after FC04-C03-U009, FC04-C03-U010 · not formalized

Z_(p) consists of rationals with denominator prime to p; Z_f consists of rationals whose denominator is a power of f.

Reusable geometric example: Polynomial localization along a variety

FC04-C03-U013 · Rings and Modules of Fractions; pp. 36–39; L1569 · after FC04-C01-U020, FC04-C03-U009 · not formalized

For A=k[t₁,…,tₙ] and prime 𝔭, A_𝔭 consists of rational functions f/g with g∉𝔭; for infinite k it is interpreted as functions defined at almost all points of V(𝔭).

Not formalized. partial: Localization.AtPrime; RatFunc; polynomial/zero-locus APIs — P/H realize the algebraic localization at a prime but not the source geometric interpretation as rational functions defined at almost all points of V(𝔭) over an infinite field; targeted external Lean search found no complete owner.

Construction: Module of fractions S⁻¹M

FC04-C03-U014 · Rings and Modules of Fractions; pp. 36–39; L1571–1577 · after FC04-C02-U001, FC04-C03-U001, FC04-C03-U002 · in Mathlib

On M×S set (m,s)~(m′,s′) iff t(sm′−s′m)=0 for some t∈S; classes form an S⁻¹A-module. Write M_𝔭 and M_f in the corresponding cases.

Formalized. Mathlib/Algebra/Module/LocalizedModule/Basic.lean::LocalizedModule; IsLocalizedModule; LocalizedModule.mk — The quotient/fraction module and its localization-ring action realize the source S⁻¹M, including prime and principal specializations.

Construction: Localization of a module homomorphism

FC04-C03-U015 · Rings and Modules of Fractions; pp. 36–39; L1579 · after FC04-C02-U003, FC04-C03-U014 · in Mathlib

u:M→N induces S⁻¹u:m/s↦u(m)/s, compatible with composition.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/Module.lean::LocalizedModule.map; map_mk; map_id; map_comp — A linear map localizes by m/s ↦ u(m)/s and is functorial.

Proposition 3.3: Exactness of localization

FC04-C03-U016 · Rings and Modules of Fractions; pp. 36–39; L1581 · after FC04-C02-U035, FC04-C03-U014, FC04-C03-U015 · in Mathlib

If M′→M→M″ is exact at M, its localization is exact at S⁻¹M.

Formalized. Mathlib/Algebra/Module/LocalizedModule/Exact.lean::LocalizedModule.map_exact; IsLocalizedModule.map_exact — Localization preserves exactness at the middle module exactly as Proposition 3.3.

Convention: Localized submodule convention

FC04-C03-U017 · Rings and Modules of Fractions; pp. 36–39; L1583 · after FC04-C03-U016 · in Mathlib

For M′⊂M, exactness makes S⁻¹M′→S⁻¹M injective, so S⁻¹M′ is henceforth regarded as a submodule.

Formalized. U016 plus Submodule.toLocalized; LocalizedModule.map_injective_iff interfaces — Localization of a submodule inclusion remains injective, and the localized submodule API realizes it as a submodule of the localized ambient module.

Corollary 3.4: Localization commutes with finite sums, intersections, quotients

FC04-C03-U018 · Rings and Modules of Fractions; pp. 36–39; L1585–1597 · after FC04-C02-U008, FC04-C02-U013, FC04-C02-U014, FC04-C03-U016, FC04-C03-U017 · in Mathlib

S⁻¹(N+P)=S⁻¹N+S⁻¹P, S⁻¹(N∩P)=S⁻¹N∩S⁻¹P, and S⁻¹(M/N)≅S⁻¹M/S⁻¹N.

Formalized. Submodule.localized'_iSup; localized'_inf; localizedQuotientEquiv — Localization preserves sums and intersections and the quotient localization is canonically linearly equivalent to the quotient by the localized submodule.

Proposition 3.5: Localization as tensor product

FC04-C03-U019 · Rings and Modules of Fractions; pp. 36–39; L1599–1631 · after FC04-C02-U046, FC04-C03-U014 · in Mathlib

There is a unique S⁻¹A-linear isomorphism S⁻¹A⊗_A M→S⁻¹M sending (a/s)⊗m to am/s.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Localization/BaseChange.lean::LocalizedModule.equivTensorProduct; pure-tensor lemmas — This is the canonical S⁻¹A ⊗_A M ≃ S⁻¹M equivalence with the source fraction formula.

Corollary 3.6: Localization ring is flat

FC04-C03-U020 · Rings and Modules of Fractions; pp. 36–39; L1633–1635 · after FC04-C02-U061, FC04-C03-U016, FC04-C03-U019 · in Mathlib

S⁻¹A is flat as an A-module.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/Localization.lean::IsLocalization.flat — Every localization ring is flat over the base ring.

Proposition 3.7: Localization commutes with tensor product

FC04-C03-U021 · Rings and Modules of Fractions; pp. 36–39; L1637–1651 · after FC04-C02-U046, FC04-C03-U019 · in Mathlib

S⁻¹M⊗_{S⁻¹A}S⁻¹N≅S⁻¹(M⊗_A N), with (m/s)⊗(n/t)↦(m⊗n)/(st).

Formalized. IsLocalization.tensorProduct_isLocalizedModule; IsLocalization.moduleTensorEquiv; IsLocalizedModule.linearEquiv — The tensor product of localized modules is itself the localization of the original tensor product, and the canonical equivalences give the stated S⁻¹M ⊗_{S⁻¹A} S⁻¹N ≃ S⁻¹(M⊗_A N) formula.

Definition: Local property

FC04-C03-U022 · Local properties; p. 40; L1655–1657 · after FC04-C03-U009, FC04-C03-U014 · not formalized

A property P of A or an A-module M is local if it holds globally iff it holds after localization at every prime ideal.

Not formalized. partial: Mathlib/RingTheory/LocalProperties/Basic.lean::RingHom.PropertyIsLocal — Mathlib’s PropertyIsLocal is a stronger ring-hom gluing/localization framework, not the source generic predicate on a ring or module defined simply by P(X) ↔︎ ∀ 𝔭, P(X_𝔭). No source-shaped generic definition was found in P/H or indexed external Lean.

Searched: N

Proposition 3.8: Vanishing is local

FC04-C03-U023 · Local properties; p. 40; L1659–1667 · after FC04-C01-U020, FC04-C01-U021, FC04-C03-U014, FC04-C03-U022 · in Mathlib

M=0 iff M_𝔭=0 for every prime 𝔭 iff M_𝔪=0 for every maximal 𝔪.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/LocalProperties/Submodule.lean::Module.eq_zero_of_localization_maximal; Module.subsingleton_of_localization_maximal; support/localization APIs — The maximal-local converse is explicit; forward localization of zero is trivial. Since maximal ideals are prime, this yields both prime and maximal formulations.

Proposition 3.9: Injectivity and surjectivity are local
