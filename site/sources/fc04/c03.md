### Proposition 3.1: Universal property of localization {#fc04-c03-u006}

### Characterization: Three structural properties of S⁻¹A {#fc04-c03-u007}

:::

### Proposition 3.9: Injectivity and surjectivity are local {#fc04-c03-u024}
