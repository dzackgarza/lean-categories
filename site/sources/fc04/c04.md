### Definition: Primary ideal {#fc04-c04-u001}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC04-C04-U001` · Primary Decomposition; pp. 50–54; L1986–1992 · after [`FC04-C01-U006`](c01.html#fc04-c01-u006), [`FC04-C01-U012`](c01.html#fc04-c01-u012), [`FC04-C01-U014`](c01.html#fc04-c01-u014), [`FC04-C01-U007`](c01.html#fc04-c01-u007) · [in Mathlib]{.route-mathlib}
:::

A proper ideal 𝔮 such that xy∈𝔮 implies x∈𝔮 or y^n∈𝔮 for some n>0; equivalently A/𝔮≠0 and every zero-divisor of A/𝔮 is nilpotent.

::: {.unit-lean}
**Formalized.** [`Ideal.IsPrimary`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/find/?pattern=Ideal.IsPrimary#doc); [`Ideal.isPrimary_iff`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/find/?pattern=Ideal.isPrimary_iff#doc); [`Submodule.isPrimary_iff_zero_divisor_quotient_imp_nilpotent_smul`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/find/?pattern=Submodule.isPrimary_iff_zero_divisor_quotient_imp_nilpotent_smul#doc) — The product criterion is the source definition; the quotient theorem gives the equivalent nonzero-quotient/nilpotent-zero-divisor characterization.
:::
