### Definition: Chains of primes, length, and dimension of a ring {#fc04-c08-u005}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC04-C08-U005` · § Artin Rings; pp. 89–90; L3289 · after [`FC04-C01-U020`](c01.html#fc04-c01-u020), [`FC04-C06-U020`](c06.html#fc04-c06-u020) · [in Mathlib]{.route-mathlib}
:::

A chain of prime ideals is a finite strict chain p₀⊂⋯⊂pₙ; its length is n. For A≠0, dim A is the supremum of these lengths (an integer ≥0 or +∞). A field has dimension 0 and Z has dimension 1.

::: {.unit-lean}
**Formalized.** [`Mathlib/RingTheory/KrullDimension/Basic.lean::ringKrullDim`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/RingTheory/KrullDimension/Basic.html); [`Mathlib/Order/KrullDimension.lean::Order.krullDim`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/Order/KrullDimension.html#Order.krullDim); [`Mathlib/RingTheory/KrullDimension/Field.lean::ringKrullDim_eq_zero_of_field`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/RingTheory/KrullDimension/Field.html); [`Mathlib/RingTheory/KrullDimension/PID.lean::IsPrincipalIdealRing.ringKrullDim_eq_one`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/RingTheory/KrullDimension/PID.html#IsPrincipalIdealRing.ringKrullDim_eq_one) — `ringKrullDim` is the supremum of strict prime-chain lengths (with bottom for the zero ring and top for infinity). Fields have dimension `0`; applying the PID theorem with [`Int.not_isField`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/find/?pattern=Int.not_isField#doc) gives `dim ℤ=1`.
:::

### Comparison / example: Structure of an Artin local ring {#fc04-c08-u007}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC04-C08-U007` · § Artin Rings; pp. 89–90; L3295 · after [`FC04-C08-U001`](#fc04-c08-u001), [`FC04-C08-U004`](#fc04-c08-u004), [`FC04-C01-U028`](c01.html#fc04-c01-u028), [`FC04-C01-U015`](c01.html#fc04-c01-u015), [`FC04-C01-U035`](c01.html#fc04-c01-u035) · [in Mathlib]{.route-mathlib}

### Proposition: Proposition 8.6 {#fc04-c08-u008}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC04-C08-U008` · § Artin Rings; pp. 89–90; L3298 · after [`FC04-C06-U014`](c06.html#fc04-c06-u014), [`FC04-C01-U028`](c01.html#fc04-c01-u028), [`FC04-C02-U031`](c02.html#fc04-c02-u031), [`FC04-C08-U006`](#fc04-c08-u006) · [in Mathlib]{.route-mathlib}
:::

For a Noetherian local ring (A,m), exactly one holds: mⁿ≠mⁿ⁺¹ for every n, or mⁿ=0 for some n, in which case A is an Artin local ring.

::: {.unit-lean}
**Formalized.** [`Mathlib/RingTheory/Nakayama.lean::Submodule.eq_bot_of_le_smul_of_le_jacobson_bot`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/RingTheory/Nakayama.html#Submodule.eq_bot_of_le_smul_of_le_jacobson_bot); [`Mathlib/RingTheory/HopkinsLevitzki.lean::isArtinianRing_iff_isNilpotent_maximalIdeal`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/RingTheory/HopkinsLevitzki.html); [`IsLocalRing.jacobson_eq_maximalIdeal`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/find/?pattern=IsLocalRing.jacobson_eq_maximalIdeal#doc) — If two consecutive powers of the maximal ideal of a Noetherian local ring coincide, Nakayama forces that power to be zero; conversely nilpotence produces a stationary zero power. Thus exactly the source dichotomy holds, and the nilpotent branch is equivalent to being Artinian. This assembly was compile-checked against P.
:::

### Theorem: Theorem 8.7 (structure theorem for Artin rings) {#fc04-c08-u009}

### Construction / comparison: The vector space m/m² {#fc04-c08-u011}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC04-C08-U011` · § Artin Rings; pp. 89–90; L3312 · after [`FC04-C01-U028`](c01.html#fc04-c01-u028), [`FC04-C01-U029`](c01.html#fc04-c01-u029), [`FC04-C02-U008`](c02.html#fc04-c02-u008), [`FC04-C02-U033`](c02.html#fc04-c02-u033), [`FC04-C02-U034`](c02.html#fc04-c02-u034) · [in Mathlib]{.route-mathlib}
:::

For a local ring (A,m) with residue field k=A/m, m/m² is naturally a k-vector space. If m is finitely generated, its generator images span m/m², so this vector space is finite-dimensional.

::: {.unit-lean}
**Formalized.** [`Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Cotangent.lean::Ideal.Cotangent`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Cotangent.html#Ideal.Cotangent); [`IsLocalRing.CotangentSpace`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/find/?pattern=IsLocalRing.CotangentSpace#doc); [`Ideal.toCotangent`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/find/?pattern=Ideal.toCotangent#doc); [`Mathlib/Algebra/Module/SpanRankOperations.lean::IsLocalRing.spanFinrank_maximalIdeal_eq_finrank_cotangentSpace_of_fg`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/Algebra/Module/SpanRankOperations.html#IsLocalRing.spanFinrank_maximalIdeal_eq_finrank_cotangentSpace_of_fg) — `Ideal.Cotangent m` is exactly `m/m²`; for the maximal ideal it carries the residue-field vector-space structure. Finite generation of `m` makes the cotangent space finite-dimensional, and generator images span it.
:::
