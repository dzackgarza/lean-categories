### Definition: Element integral over a subring {#fc04-c05-u001}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC04-C05-U001` · Integral dependence; pp. 59–60; L2284–2290 · after [`FC04-C01-U005`](c01.html#fc04-c01-u005), [`FC04-C01-U001`](c01.html#fc04-c01-u001) · [in Mathlib]{.route-mathlib}
:::

For A⊂B, x∈B is integral over A if it satisfies a monic polynomial x^n+a₁x^{n−1}+⋯+a_n=0 with a_i∈A.

::: {.unit-lean}
**Formalized.** [`Mathlib/RingTheory/IntegralClosure/IsIntegral/Defs.lean::RingHom.IsIntegralElem`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/RingTheory/IntegralClosure/IsIntegral/Defs.html#RingHom.IsIntegralElem); `IsIntegral` — Integrality is existence of a monic polynomial over the base whose evaluation at the element is zero, exactly the source definition.
:::
