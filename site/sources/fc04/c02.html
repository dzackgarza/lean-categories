Definition: A-module

FC04-C02-U001 · Modules and module homomorphisms; p. 17; L763–773 · after FC04-C01-U001, FC04-C01-U004

An abelian group M with scalar action A×M→M satisfying distributivity, associativity of scalar multiplication, and 1x=x; equivalently a ring homomorphism A→End(M).

Reusable comparisons: Basic module models

FC04-C02-U002 · Modules and module homomorphisms; p. 17; L775–785 · after FC04-C01-U006, FC04-C02-U001

Ideals are A-modules; k-modules are vector spaces; Z-modules are abelian groups; k[x]-modules are vector spaces with one linear operator; k[G]-modules are representations of G (with noncommutative caveat).

Definition: A-module homomorphism / A-linear map

FC04-C02-U003 · Modules and module homomorphisms; pp. 17–18; L787–795 · after FC04-C02-U001

f:M→N is additive and satisfies f(ax)=af(x).

Construction: HomA(M,N) as an A-module

FC04-C02-U004 · Modules and module homomorphisms; pp. 17–18; L797–803 · after FC04-C02-U003

Pointwise addition and scalar multiplication make module homomorphisms M→N into Hom_A(M,N).

Construction: Precomposition and postcomposition on Hom

FC04-C02-U005 · Modules and module homomorphisms; pp. 17–18; L805–815 · after FC04-C02-U003, FC04-C02-U004

u:M′→M and v:N→N″ induce f↦f∘u and f↦v∘f, both A-linear.

Canonical comparison: HomA(A,M)≅M

FC04-C02-U006 · Modules and module homomorphisms; pp. 17–18; L817 · after FC04-C02-U004

Evaluation at 1 gives the natural isomorphism Hom_A(A,M)≅M.

Definition: Submodule

FC04-C02-U007 · Submodules and quotient modules; p. 18; L821 · after FC04-C02-U001

A subgroup M′⊂M closed under scalar multiplication by A.

Construction / definition: Quotient module M/M′

FC04-C02-U008 · Submodules and quotient modules; p. 18; L821 · after FC04-C02-U007, FC04-C01-U009

The quotient abelian group with a(x+M′)=ax+M′; submodules above M′ correspond order-preservingly to submodules of the quotient.

Definition: Kernel of a module homomorphism

FC04-C02-U009 · Submodules and quotient modules; p. 18; L823–827 · after FC04-C02-U003, FC04-C02-U007

ker f={x∈M:f(x)=0}, a submodule of M.

Definition: Image of a module homomorphism

FC04-C02-U010 · Submodules and quotient modules; p. 18; L829–833 · after FC04-C02-U003, FC04-C02-U007

im f=f(M), a submodule of N.

Definition: Cokernel of a module homomorphism

FC04-C02-U011 · Submodules and quotient modules; p. 18; L835–841 · after FC04-C02-U008, FC04-C02-U010

coker f=N/im f.

Construction / comparison: Induced quotient map and first isomorphism theorem

FC04-C02-U012 · Submodules and quotient modules; p. 18; L843–847 · after FC04-C02-U008, FC04-C02-U009, FC04-C02-U010

If M′⊆ker f, f induces M/M′→N with kernel ker f/M′; in particular M/ker f≅im f.

Construction: Sum of submodules

FC04-C02-U013 · Operations on submodules; p. 19; L851 · after FC04-C02-U007

ΣMᵢ consists of finite-support sums and is the least submodule containing all Mᵢ.

Construction / comparison: Intersection and complete lattice of submodules

FC04-C02-U014 · Operations on submodules; p. 19; L853 · after FC04-C02-U007, FC04-C01-U040

Arbitrary intersections are submodules; submodules form a complete lattice under inclusion.

Proposition 2.1: Isomorphism theorems for quotients and sums

FC04-C02-U015 · Operations on submodules; p. 19; L855–869 · after FC04-C02-U008, FC04-C02-U012, FC04-C02-U013, FC04-C02-U014

(L/N)/(M/N)≅L/M for L⊇M⊇N; (M₁+M₂)/M₁≅M₂/(M₁∩M₂).

Construction: Ideal–module product 𝔞M

FC04-C02-U016 · Operations on submodules; p. 19; L871 · after FC04-C01-U006, FC04-C02-U001, FC04-C02-U007

Submodule of M consisting of finite sums Σaᵢxᵢ with aᵢ∈𝔞 and xᵢ∈M.

Definition: Colon ideal (N:P)

FC04-C02-U017 · Operations on submodules; p. 19; L873 · after FC04-C01-U006, FC04-C02-U007

For submodules N,P⊂M, (N:P)={a∈A:aP⊆N}, an ideal of A.

Definition: Annihilator of a module

FC04-C02-U018 · Operations on submodules; p. 19; L873 · after FC04-C02-U017

Ann(M)=(0:M)={a∈A:aM=0}.

Construction: Descent of scalars to A/𝔞 when 𝔞 annihilates M

FC04-C02-U019 · Operations on submodules; p. 19; L873 · after FC04-C01-U007, FC04-C02-U001, FC04-C02-U018

If 𝔞⊆Ann(M), define (x+𝔞)m=xm to make M an A/𝔞-module.

Definition: Faithful module

FC04-C02-U020 · Operations on submodules; p. 19; L875 · after FC04-C02-U018, FC04-C02-U019

M is faithful iff Ann(M)=0; if Ann(M)=𝔞, M is faithful over A/𝔞.

Definition / construction: Cyclic submodule and generating set

FC04-C02-U021 · Operations on submodules; p. 19; L881 · after FC04-C02-U013, FC04-C02-U001

Ax=(x) is the submodule of multiples of x; {xᵢ} generates M when M=ΣAxᵢ.

Definition: Finitely generated module

FC04-C02-U022 · Operations on submodules; p. 19; L881 · after FC04-C02-U021

An A-module admitting a finite generating set.

Construction: Direct sum of modules

FC04-C02-U023 · Direct sum and product; p. 20; L885–891 · after FC04-C02-U001

For a family (Mᵢ), ⊕Mᵢ consists of tuples with finite support, with componentwise operations.

Construction: Direct product of modules

FC04-C02-U024 · Direct sum and product; p. 20; L891 · after FC04-C02-U001, FC04-C02-U023

∏Mᵢ consists of all tuples; it agrees with the direct sum for finite index sets, but generally not infinite ones.

Reusable comparison: Ring product decompositions, ideals, and idempotents

FC04-C02-U025 · Direct sum and product; p. 20; L893–911 · after FC04-C01-U044, FC04-C02-U023

A=∏Aᵢ decomposes as a direct sum of coordinate ideals; conversely A=⊕𝔞ᵢ yields A≅∏A/𝔟ᵢ, and each 𝔞ᵢ=(eᵢ) for an idempotent identity eᵢ.

Definition: Free module

FC04-C02-U026 · Finitely generated modules; p. 21; L915 · after FC04-C02-U023

An A-module isomorphic to a direct sum of copies of A, denoted A^(I).

Convention: Finite free module notation

FC04-C02-U027 · Finitely generated modules; p. 21; L915 · after FC04-C02-U026

A finite direct sum of n copies of A is A^n; A^0 is the zero module.

Proposition 2.3: Finite generation via finite free quotient

FC04-C02-U028 · Finitely generated modules; p. 21; L917–921 · after FC04-C02-U008, FC04-C02-U022, FC04-C02-U027

M is finitely generated iff M is isomorphic to a quotient of A^n for some n>0.

Proposition 2.4: Determinant trick for endomorphisms

FC04-C02-U029 · Finitely generated modules; p. 21; L923–937 · after FC04-C01-U006, FC04-C02-U016, FC04-C02-U022

If M is finite, φ(M)⊆𝔞M, then φ satisfies a monic relation φn+a₁φ{n−1}+⋯+a_n=0 with aᵢ∈𝔞.

Corollary 2.5: Finite module with 𝔞M=M has near-1 annihilator

FC04-C02-U030 · Finitely generated modules; p. 21; L939–941 · after FC04-C01-U011, FC04-C02-U029

If 𝔞M=M, there is x≡1 mod 𝔞 with xM=0.

Proposition 2.6 (Nakayama): Nakayama lemma

FC04-C02-U031 · Finitely generated modules; p. 21; L943–953 · after FC04-C01-U037, FC04-C01-U038, FC04-C02-U022, FC04-C02-U030

For finite M and 𝔞⊆Jac(A), equality 𝔞M=M implies M=0.

Corollary 2.7: Nakayama modulo a submodule

FC04-C02-U032 · Finitely generated modules; p. 21; L955–957 · after FC04-C02-U008, FC04-C02-U031

For finite M, N⊂M and 𝔞⊆Jac(A), M=𝔞M+N implies M=N.

Construction / comparison: M/𝔪M as residue-field vector space

FC04-C02-U033 · Finitely generated modules; p. 21; L959 · after FC04-C01-U028, FC04-C01-U029, FC04-C02-U016, FC04-C02-U022

For local (A,𝔪) and finite M, M/𝔪M is a finite-dimensional k=A/𝔪 vector space.

Proposition 2.8: Lifting a residue-field basis to generators

FC04-C02-U034 · Finitely generated modules; p. 21; L961–963 · after FC04-C02-U032, FC04-C02-U033

If images of x₁,…,xₙ form a basis of M/𝔪M, then x₁,…,xₙ generate M.

Definition: Exact sequence

FC04-C02-U035 · Exact sequences; pp. 22–23; L967–983 · after FC04-C02-U009, FC04-C02-U010

A sequence is exact at Mᵢ when im fᵢ=ker fᵢ₊₁, and exact when exact at every term.

Definition: Short exact sequence

FC04-C02-U036 · Exact sequences; pp. 22–23; L983–985 · after FC04-C02-U035

A sequence 0→M′→M→M″→0 that is exact; any long exact sequence splits formally into such short exact pieces using images/kernels.

Proposition 2.9: Exactness detected by Hom

FC04-C02-U037 · Exact sequences; pp. 22–23; L987–1013 · after FC04-C02-U004, FC04-C02-U005, FC04-C02-U035

Right-exact M′→M→M″→0 and left-exact 0→N′→N→N″ are characterized by exactness of the induced Hom sequences for every test module.

Proposition 2.10: Kernel–cokernel exact sequence

FC04-C02-U038 · Exact sequences; pp. 22–23; L1015–1029 · after FC04-C02-U009, FC04-C02-U011, FC04-C02-U036

A morphism between two short exact sequences induces the six-term exact sequence from kernels through cokernels.

Construction: Boundary homomorphism in Proposition 2.10

FC04-C02-U039 · Exact sequences; pp. 22–23; L1029 · after FC04-C02-U038

For x″∈ker f″, lift to x∈M, lift f(x) through u′, then take its class in coker f′.

Definition: Additive function on a class of modules

FC04-C02-U040 · Exact sequences; pp. 22–23; L1033 · after FC04-C02-U036

λ is additive if λ(M′)−λ(M)+λ(M″)=0 for every short exact sequence in its class.

Reusable example: Dimension is additive

FC04-C02-U041 · Exact sequences; pp. 22–23; L1035 · after FC04-C02-U040

For finite-dimensional k-vector spaces, V↦dim V is additive.

Proposition 2.11: Euler relation for an exact sequence

FC04-C02-U042 · Exact sequences; pp. 22–23; L1037–1049 · after FC04-C02-U035, FC04-C02-U040

For an exact finite sequence whose terms and kernels lie in the class of an additive λ, Σ(-1)^i λ(M_i)=0.

Definition: A-bilinear map

FC04-C02-U043 · Tensor product of modules; pp. 24–26; L1053 · after FC04-C02-U003

f:M×N→P is A-linear separately in each variable.

Proposition 2.12: Tensor product universal property

FC04-C02-U044 · Tensor product of modules; pp. 24–26; L1055–1063 · after FC04-C02-U043

There is (T,g) with g:M×N→T bilinear such that every bilinear f factors uniquely through an A-linear T→P; (T,g) is unique up to unique isomorphism.

Construction: Tensor product as a quotient of a free module

FC04-C02-U045 · Tensor product of modules; pp. 24–26; L1065–1081 · after FC04-C02-U026, FC04-C02-U044
