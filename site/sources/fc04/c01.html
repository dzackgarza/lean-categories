Definition: Ring

FC04-C01-U001 · Rings and ring homomorphisms; p. 1; L133–149 · in Mathlib

A set with addition and multiplication satisfying the abelian-group, associative/distributive, commutative, and identity axioms.

Formalized. Mathlib/Algebra/Ring/Defs.lean::CommRing — Mathlib’s CommRing is a commutative unital ring; as in Atiyah–Macdonald, the degenerate one-element ring is permitted unless Nontrivial is separately assumed.

Convention: “Ring” convention

FC04-C01-U002 · Rings and ring homomorphisms; p. 1; L149 · after FC04-C01-U001 · in Mathlib

Throughout FC04, rings are commutative and have identity.

Formalized. Mathlib/Algebra/Ring/Hom/Defs.lean::RingHom — RingHom preserves 0, 1, addition, and multiplication, exactly the source convention for ring homomorphisms.

Definition: Zero ring

FC04-C01-U003 · Rings and ring homomorphisms; p. 1; L151–157 · after FC04-C01-U001 · in Mathlib

The permitted case 1=0, hence the one-element ring, denoted 0.

Formalized. Mathlib/Algebra/Ring/Subring/Defs.lean::Subring — A Subring is a subset closed under the ring operations and containing 1, with inherited structure.

Definition: Ring homomorphism

FC04-C01-U004 · Rings and ring homomorphisms; p. 1; L159–165 · after FC04-C01-U001 · in Mathlib

Map preserving addition, multiplication, and 1.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Defs.lean::Ideal — Ideal R is the additive submodule of R over itself, hence exactly an additive subgroup closed under multiplication by arbitrary ring elements.

Definition: Subring

FC04-C01-U005 · Rings and ring homomorphisms; p. 1; L167 · after FC04-C01-U001 · in Mathlib

Subset closed under addition and multiplication and containing the identity of the ambient ring.

Formalized. Ideal; Submodule.add_mem; Submodule.neg_mem; Submodule.smul_mem — The bundled closure fields give the stated additive-subgroup-plus-absorption criterion for ideals.

Definition: Ideal

FC04-C01-U006 · Ideals. Quotient rings; p. 2; L173 · after FC04-C01-U001 · in Mathlib

Additive subgroup 𝔞⊆A satisfying A𝔞⊆𝔞.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Span.lean::Ideal.span; Ideal.subset_span; Ideal.span_le — span S is the least ideal containing S, with the universal property used by the source.

Construction / definition: Quotient (residue-class) ring A/𝔞

FC04-C01-U007 · Ideals. Quotient rings; p. 2; L173 · after FC04-C01-U006 · in Mathlib

Cosets of an ideal with multiplication induced from A.

Formalized. Ideal.span ({x} : Set R); Ideal.mem_span_singleton; Ideal.span_singleton_mul_span_singleton — Singleton span is the principal ideal (x) and its membership API is divisibility by x.

Construction: Canonical quotient homomorphism A→A/𝔞

FC04-C01-U008 · Ideals. Quotient rings; p. 2; L173 · after FC04-C01-U004, FC04-C01-U007 · in Mathlib

x↦x+𝔞; a surjective ring homomorphism.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Quotient/Basic.lean::Ideal.Quotient; Ideal.Quotient.mk — The quotient ring and canonical map are exactly R/I by the ideal congruence.

Proposition 1.1: Ideal correspondence for a quotient

FC04-C01-U009 · Ideals. Quotient rings; p. 2; L177 · after FC04-C01-U007, FC04-C01-U008 · in Mathlib

Ideals of A containing 𝔞 correspond order-preservingly to ideals of A/𝔞 by inverse image.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Maps.lean::Ideal.relIsoOfSurjective; Ideal.Quotient.mk_surjective; Ideal.Quotient.mk_ker — Applying the surjective correspondence order-isomorphism to R → R/I identifies ideals of the quotient with ideals of R containing I.

Construction / comparison: Kernel, image, and first isomorphism for ring homomorphisms

FC04-C01-U010 · Ideals. Quotient rings; p. 2; L179 · after FC04-C01-U004, FC04-C01-U006, FC04-C01-U007 · in Mathlib

For f:A→B, ker f is an ideal, f(A) a subring, and A/ker f≅f(A).

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Quotient/Operations.lean::RingHom.quotientKerEquivRange — This is the first isomorphism theorem R/ker f ≃+* range f.

Notation: Congruence modulo an ideal

FC04-C01-U011 · Ideals. Quotient rings; p. 2; L181 · after FC04-C01-U006 · in Mathlib

x≡y (mod 𝔞) means x−y∈𝔞.

Formalized. Mathlib/Algebra/GroupWithZero/Basic.lean::subsingleton_iff_zero_eq_one; subsingleton_of_zero_eq_one — A commutative ring is the zero ring exactly when it is subsingleton, equivalently 0 = 1; no global nontriviality assumption is built into CommRing.

Definition: Zero-divisor

FC04-C01-U012 · Zero-divisors. Nilpotent elements. Units; pp. 2–2; L185 · after FC04-C01-U001 · in Mathlib

x is a zero-divisor if xy=0 for some y≠0.

Formalized. Mathlib/Algebra/GroupWithZero/NonZeroDivisors.lean::nonZeroDivisors; notMem_nonZeroDivisors_iff_left — The complement of nonZeroDivisors is exactly the elements annihilating some nonzero element; in a nontrivial ring this includes 0, matching the source convention.

Definition: Integral domain

FC04-C01-U013 · Zero-divisors. Nilpotent elements. Units; pp. 2–2; L185 · after FC04-C01-U001, FC04-C01-U012 · in Mathlib

Nonzero ring with no nonzero zero-divisors.

Formalized. Mathlib/Algebra/Ring/Defs.lean::IsDomain — IsDomain R combines nontriviality with the no-zero-divisors property, exactly the integral-domain definition.

Definition: Nilpotent element

FC04-C01-U014 · Zero-divisors. Nilpotent elements. Units; pp. 2–2; L187 · after FC04-C01-U001 · in Mathlib

x is nilpotent if x^n=0 for some n>0.

Formalized. Mathlib/Algebra/GroupWithZero/Defs.lean::mul_left_cancel₀; mul_right_cancel₀ — In a domain, multiplication by a nonzero element cancels on either side.

Definition: Unit and inverse

FC04-C01-U015 · Zero-divisors. Nilpotent elements. Units; pp. 2–2; L189 · after FC04-C01-U001 · in Mathlib

x is a unit if xy=1 for some y; y is unique and written x⁻¹.

Formalized. Mathlib/Algebra/GroupWithZero/Defs.lean::IsNilpotent — IsNilpotent x means x^n = 0 for some positive exponent (with the standard equivalent encoding used by Mathlib).

Definition / notation: Principal ideal

FC04-C01-U016 · Zero-divisors. Nilpotent elements. Units; pp. 2–3; L196 · after FC04-C01-U006 · in Mathlib

The ideal of multiples ax of x, denoted (x) or Ax.

Formalized. IsNilpotent (I : Ideal R); Mathlib/RingTheory/Nilpotent/Lemmas.lean::mem_nilradical — Ideals themselves carry multiplication, so IsNilpotent I is I^n=0; the condition that every element of I is nilpotent is equivalently I ≤ nilradical R via mem_nilradical.

Convention: Zero ideal notation

FC04-C01-U017 · Zero-divisors. Nilpotent elements. Units; pp. 2–3; L196 · after FC04-C01-U016 · in Mathlib

The ideal (0) is usually denoted 0.

Formalized. Mathlib/Algebra/Group/Units/Defs.lean::IsUnit — IsUnit x means x is represented by a unit, equivalently admits a multiplicative inverse in the commutative ring.

Definition: Field

FC04-C01-U018 · Zero-divisors. Nilpotent elements. Units; pp. 2–3; L197 · after FC04-C01-U001, FC04-C01-U015 · in Mathlib

A nonzero ring in which every nonzero element is a unit.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Span.lean::Ideal.span_singleton_eq_top — (x)=R ↔︎ IsUnit x is exactly the source characterization.

Proposition 1.2: Characterizations of a field

FC04-C01-U019 · Zero-divisors. Nilpotent elements. Units; pp. 2–3; L199–211 · after FC04-C01-U006, FC04-C01-U010, FC04-C01-U018 · in Mathlib

For A≠0: A is a field iff its only ideals are 0,(1) iff every homomorphism A→B to B≠0 is injective.

Formalized. Mathlib/Algebra/Field/Defs.lean::Field — Field is the nontrivial commutative ring structure in which every nonzero element is invertible.

Definition: Prime ideal

FC04-C01-U020 · Prime ideals and maximal ideals; pp. 3–4; L215 · after FC04-C01-U006 · in Mathlib

𝔭≠(1) and xy∈𝔭 implies x∈𝔭 or y∈𝔭.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Basic.lean::Ring.isField_iff_isSimpleOrder_ideal; RingHom.injective_iff_ker_eq_bot; field-source RingHom.injective — The ideal lattice is simple exactly for a field; a homomorphism is injective exactly when its kernel is zero, and a homomorphism from a field to a nontrivial ring has zero kernel, yielding the source’s three equivalent criteria.

Definition: Maximal ideal

FC04-C01-U021 · Prime ideals and maximal ideals; pp. 3–4; L217 · after FC04-C01-U006 · in Mathlib

𝔪≠(1) with no ideal strictly between 𝔪 and (1).

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Maximal.lean::Ideal.IsMaximal; Ideal.isMaximal_iff — This is maximality among proper ideals under inclusion.

Comparison: Prime/maximal ideals via quotients

FC04-C01-U022 · Prime ideals and maximal ideals; pp. 3–4; L217–219 · after FC04-C01-U007, FC04-C01-U013, FC04-C01-U018, FC04-C01-U020, FC04-C01-U021 · in Mathlib

𝔭 prime iff A/𝔭 is a domain; 𝔪 maximal iff A/𝔪 is a field.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Quotient/Basic.lean::Ideal.Quotient.isDomain_iff_prime; Ideal.Quotient.maximal_ideal_iff_isField_quotient — The quotient is a domain exactly when the ideal is prime, and a field exactly when it is maximal.

Comparison: Maximal ideals are prime; 0 prime iff domain

FC04-C01-U023 · Prime ideals and maximal ideals; pp. 3–4; L219 · after FC04-C01-U013, FC04-C01-U020, FC04-C01-U021 · in Mathlib

Every maximal ideal is prime; the zero ideal is prime exactly for integral domains.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Maximal.lean::Ideal.IsMaximal.isPrime; Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Prime.lean::Ideal.isPrime_bot — Maximal ideals are prime, and the zero ideal is prime precisely in the domain setting.

Construction / comparison: Contraction preserves prime ideals

FC04-C01-U024 · Prime ideals and maximal ideals; pp. 3–4; L221 · after FC04-C01-U004, FC04-C01-U020 · not formalized

For f:A→B and prime 𝔮⊂B, f⁻¹(𝔮) is prime; maximality need not contract (example Z→Q).

Not formalized. partial: Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Maps.lean::Ideal.IsPrime.comap — Prime contraction is pinned, but no checked declaration realizes the source’s accompanying failure of maximal contraction via the concrete inclusion ℤ → ℚ; current Mathlib and targeted GitHub search still expose only the general prime-comap theorem and unrelated ideal-contraction examples.

Theorem 1.3 / order-theory convention: Existence of maximal ideals

FC04-C01-U025 · Prime ideals and maximal ideals; pp. 3–4; L225–229, 255 · after FC04-C01-U021 · in Mathlib

Every nonzero ring has a maximal ideal. The source footnote defines a chain in a poset as a totally ordered subset and states the Zorn criterion used in the proof.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Maximal.lean::Ideal.exists_maximal — This is the Zorn-lemma existence of a maximal ideal in a nontrivial commutative ring.

Corollary 1.4: Proper ideals lie in maximal ideals

FC04-C01-U026 · Prime ideals and maximal ideals; pp. 3–4; L231–233 · after FC04-C01-U009, FC04-C01-U025 · in Mathlib

Every ideal 𝔞≠(1) is contained in a maximal ideal.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Maximal.lean::Ideal.exists_le_maximal — Every proper ideal is contained in a maximal ideal.

Corollary 1.5: Non-units lie in maximal ideals

FC04-C01-U027 · Prime ideals and maximal ideals; pp. 3–4; L235 · after FC04-C01-U015, FC04-C01-U026 · in Mathlib

Every non-unit belongs to a maximal ideal.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Nonunits.lean::exists_max_ideal_of_mem_nonunits; coe_subset_nonunits — Every nonunit lies in a maximal ideal, while every proper ideal consists only of nonunits; together these identify the nonunits with the union of maximal ideals.

Definition: Local ring

FC04-C01-U028 · Prime ideals and maximal ideals; pp. 3–4; L237 · after FC04-C01-U021 · in Mathlib

A ring with exactly one maximal ideal 𝔪.

Formalized. Mathlib/RingTheory/LocalRing/Defs.lean::IsLocalRing; Mathlib/RingTheory/LocalRing/Basic.lean::IsLocalRing.of_unique_max_ideal; Mathlib/RingTheory/LocalRing/MaximalIdeal/Basic.lean::IsLocalRing.maximal_ideal_unique; IsLocalRing.of_nonunits_add — Mathlib proves equivalence between the local-ring unit criterion, a unique maximal ideal, and additive closure of the nonunits.

Definition: Residue field of a local ring
