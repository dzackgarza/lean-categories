### Theorem (Jordan–Hölder): Composition factors are unique up to permutation and isomorphism {#fc04-c06-u027}

:::

### Corollary 6.11: Noetherian iff Artinian under a maximal-ideal product filtration {#fc04-c06-u030}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC04-C06-U030` · Chain Conditions; pp. 74–77; L2930–2932 · after [`FC04-C01-U021`](c01.html#fc04-c01-u021), [`FC04-C01-U041`](c01.html#fc04-c01-u041), [`FC04-C06-U012`](#fc04-c06-u012), [`FC04-C06-U029`](#fc04-c06-u029) · [in Mathlib]{.route-mathlib}
:::

If 0=𝔪₁⋯𝔪_n is a product of maximal ideals, then A is Noetherian iff A is Artinian.

::: {.unit-lean}
**Formalized.** [`Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Operations.lean::Ideal.IsPrime.prod_le`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Operations.html#Ideal.IsPrime.prod_le); [`Mathlib/RingTheory/KrullDimension/Basic.lean::Ring.KrullDimLE.mk₀`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/RingTheory/KrullDimension/Basic.html); [`Mathlib/RingTheory/HopkinsLevitzki.lean::isArtinianRing_iff_isNoetherianRing_krullDimLE_zero`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/RingTheory/HopkinsLevitzki.html) — If a finite product of maximal ideals is zero, every prime contains one factor by [`IsPrime.prod_le`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/find/?pattern=IsPrime.prod_le#doc), hence equals that maximal ideal; `KrullDimLE.mk₀` gives dimension at most zero, and Hopkins–Levitzki yields Noetherian ⇒ Artinian. The reverse Artinian ⇒ Noetherian is the same pinned equivalence. The assembled source theorem was compile-checked against P.
:::
