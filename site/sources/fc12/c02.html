The blow-up \(\epsilon:\widehat S\to S\) of a smooth surface at \(p\) is an isomorphism off \(p\), with exceptional curve \(E\simeq\mathbf P^1\) parametrizing tangent directions at \(p\); the closure of the inverse image of \(C\setminus\{p\}\) is the strict transform of a curve \(C\).

Definition / construction II.4

FC12-C02-U004 · Chapter II, source L482-L489 · not formalized

II.4 (rational maps). A rational map \(X\dashrightarrow Y\) is a morphism on a maximal dense open subset. For a smooth surface its indeterminacy set is finite, so images of curves and pullbacks of divisors, linear systems, and line bundles are well defined.

Not formalized. none; no complete checked declaration under strict bundle semantics — Mathlib has Scheme.RationalMap, a quotient of morphisms on dense open subschemes, and constructs its maximal dense domain.
