Fact I.17

Fact I.17. For an embedding \(j:X\hookrightarrow Y\) of smooth varieties with ideal sheaf \(I\), \(I/I^2\) is locally free of rank \(\operatorname{codim}(X,Y)\) and \(0\to I/I^2\to j^*\Omega_Y^1\to\Omega_X^1\to0\) is exact; for a smooth curve \(C\subset S\) this yields the adjunction formula \((K_S+C)|_C\simeq\omega_C\).
