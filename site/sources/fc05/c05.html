Definition 5.2.1 — Homology spectral sequence

FC05-C05-U002 · §5.2 Terminology; pp. 122–126; L3685–3697 · after FC05-C01-U013, FC05-C01-U014 · in Mathlib

In an abelian category A, a homology spectral sequence from E^a is a family E^r_{pq} with dr:Er_{pq}→E^r_{p-r,q+r-1}, (dr)2=0, and specified isomorphisms E{r+1}*{pq}≅H(Er,d^r)*{pq}; morphisms commute with d and induce the next-page maps.

Formalized. Dual Definition 5.2.3 — Cohomology spectral sequence

Mapping Lemma 5.2.4

FC05-C05-U005 · §5.2 Terminology; pp. 122–126; L3711 · after FC05-C05-U002, FC05-C05-U004 · in Mathlib

A morphism of spectral sequences that is an isomorphism in every bidegree on one page r is an isomorphism on every later page s≥r.

Definition 5.2.5 — Bounded spectral sequence and convergence

FC05-C05-U006 · §5.2 Terminology; pp. 122–126; L3713–3731 · after FC05-C05-U002, FC05-C05-U004 · not formalized

Degree-bounded means finitely many nonzero E^a_{pq} in each total degree, hence every spot stabilizes. Such a spectral sequence converges to H_* when H_n has a finite filtration with E^∞{pq}≅F_pH{p+q}/F_{p-1}H_{p+q}; dually for cohomology.

Not formalized. partial: adjacent spectral-sequence/homological infrastructure — P/H define page-by-page spectral sequences but no boundedness/convergence/abutment structure identifying E∞ with graded pieces of a filtered target.

Example 5.2.6 — First-quadrant filtrations and edge maps

Definition 5.2.7 — Collapse of a spectral sequence

FC05-C05-U008 · §5.2 Terminology; pp. 122–126; L3747 · after FC05-C05-U002, FC05-C05-U006 · not formalized

A spectral sequence collapses at E^r when E^r has exactly one nonzero row or column; if it converges, the unique E^r_{pq} of total degree n identifies with H_n.

Construction 5.2.8 — E^∞ via nested cycles and boundaries

FC05-C05-U009 · §5.2 Terminology; pp. 122–126; L3763–3777 · after FC05-C05-U002, FC05-C03-U045 · not formalized

For each bidegree there are nested subobjects Br⊂B{r+1}⊂Z{r+1}⊂Zr of E^a with Er≅Zr/B^r. Define B∞=∪_rBr, Z∞=∩_rZr and E∞=Z∞/B^∞ when these exist; in bounded sequences they equal the stable page.

Not formalized. partial: adjacent spectral-sequence/homological infrastructure — The spectral-object implementation has internal cycle/boundary constructions, but no checked generic source interface for nested B^r,Z^r, unions/intersections, and E∞=Z∞/B∞ for an arbitrary spectral sequence.

Definition 5.2.9 — Bounded-below spectral sequence

FC05-C05-U010 · §5.2 Terminology; pp. 122–126; L3781–3783 · after FC05-C05-U002 · not formalized

A homology spectral sequence is bounded below when, in every total degree n, E^a_{pq}=0 for all sufficiently small p; dually, a cohomology spectral sequence is bounded below when the terms of degree n vanish for sufficiently large p.

Not formalized. partial: adjacent spectral-sequence/homological infrastructure — No checked P/H declaration defines Weibel’s total-degree bounded-below predicate for a spectral sequence.

Definition 5.2.10 — Regular spectral sequence

FC05-C05-U011 · §5.2 Terminology; pp. 122–126; L3785 · after FC05-C05-U002, FC05-C05-U009 · not formalized

A spectral sequence is regular when for each (p,q) the outgoing differentials are zero for all sufficiently large r; equivalently Z∞*{pq}=Zr*{pq} eventually.

Not formalized. partial: adjacent spectral-sequence/homological infrastructure — No checked P/H declaration defines regularity as eventual vanishing of outgoing differentials or the equivalent eventual-cycle condition.

Definition 5.2.11 — Weak convergence, abutment, and convergence

FC05-C05-U012 · §5.2 Terminology; pp. 122–126; L3787–3799 · after FC05-C05-U009, FC05-C05-U011, FC05-C02-U063 · not formalized

Weak convergence to H_* means a filtration F_pH_n with E^∞{pq}≅F_pH{p+q}/F_{p-1}H_{p+q}. It approaches H_* if the filtration is exhaustive and Hausdorff, and converges if additionally the spectral sequence is regular and H_n≅lim H_n/F_pH_n; bounded-below approach already implies convergence.

Not formalized. partial: adjacent spectral-sequence/homological infrastructure — P/H have no weak-convergence/approach/completeness package or inverse-limit target condition matching the source definition.

Construction 5.2.13 — Multiplicative spectral sequence

Definition 5.3.1 — Serre fibration
