Chapter 3. Tor and Ext

FC05 — Weibel, An Introduction to Homological Algebra.

This chapter contains 10 definitions, 6 theorems, 8 propositions, 9 lemmas, 8 corollaries, 5 examples, 3 constructions and 17 other statements.

Of the 66 statements checked against Lean, 5 are formalized somewhere and 61 are not.

NoteWhich versions of Lean and Mathlib were searched
  • Project-first search was performed at repo HEAD f901738. LeanCategories/Homological/Extensions.lean exports LeanCategories.Homological.Extension and Extension.Equiv for arbitrary rings. Its module docstring mentions the Baer sum, but no Baer-sum operation or Ext¹ classification is implemented there. Pinned ShortComplex.Splitting supplies the split-sequence side, so U036 is the only project-existing C03 route.
  • Pinned Mathlib is P=db584cd6d46c92f209a44c0f1c829460d327499d, Lean 4.33.0. Module.FinitePresentation is defined for arbitrary semirings/rings and gives the exact finite-generator/finite-relation presentation of U014. CharacterModule.dual_surjective_iff_injective, specialized to scalar ring ℤ on the underlying additive map, gives U013. Generic Limits.lim and Functor.rightDerived provide the derived inverse-limit construction U043 and its countable-tower specialization U044 under the usual completeness/enough-injectives assumptions.
  • The chapter continues Weibel’s arbitrary-associative-ring convention. P/H Module.Flat is declared only under [CommSemiring R], and Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/TensorProduct/Basic.lean still uses a commutative scalar ring (with an explicit TODO/comment about the missing right-action generalization). Therefore the arbitrary-ring flatness/Tor, central-localization, character-module, and tensor-based rows are not credited from commutative Mathlib implementations.
  • Current Mathlib was rechecked at H=5aedf732b6987e8c26ab3c9ebc855314f82b045f. Targeted source search found no Baer sum/Yoneda-Ext classification, no explicit Tor/Ext calculations required by C03, no lim¹/Milnor/Roos development, and no Künneth or universal-coefficient theorem matching the source rows.
  • Pinned CategoryTheory/CofilteredSystem.lean provides Functor.IsMittagLeffler and stabilization/eventual-range theorems, but not Weibel’s additional “trivial Mittag–Leffler” definition nor the theorem Mittag–Leffler ⇒ lim¹=0; the file itself lists a related Stacks lemma as TODO. Under strict whole-row semantics U052 and U053 therefore remain unmatched.
  • The project/P/H extension searches found no Baer-sum construction, abelian-group structure on extension classes, or classification by Abelian.Ext. Generic intrinsic Ext and its projective/injective resolution computations were already mapped in FC05 C02; they do not by themselves realize the C03 extension-classification rows U037–U042.
  • External search on 2026-09-06 for Künneth, universal coefficient, Milnor exact sequences, Mittag–Leffler/right-derived limit, lim1, Baer sum, and Yoneda Ext found no proved source-general implementation. deancureton/sphere-six-complex@895c0a0661501581c34d42fea3c553d4f0ee43b1 has a file named ClassicalIntegralUCTBoundary.lean, but its general integral singular-cohomology UCT is introduced as public axiom classicalIntegralSingularCohomologyUCT; it is not a proof and is not a mapping route.
  • The older definition-only Weibel catalogue is not route authority. Its flat/Tor hints silently lose the source’s arbitrary-ring right/left-module generality, and its Mittag–Leffler/AB4* hints do not supply the complete source bundles checked here.
  • N denotes dated 2026-09-06 negative evidence after project-first inspection, P, H/current Mathlib, and targeted external Lean search. Generic derived-functor infrastructure, a commutative-only specialization, an axiom, or an adjacent definition does not count as realization of an explicit calculation/comparison theorem under strict bundle semantics.

Calculation 3.1.1 — Tor of Z/p

FC05-C03-U001 · §3.1; pp. 66–67; L1964–1970 · after FC05-C02-U056 · not formalized

For abelian B: Tor_0^Z(Z/p,B)=B/pB, Tor_1^Z(Z/p,B)={b:pb=0}, and Tor_n=0 for n≥2, from the length-one free resolution.

Not formalized. partial: generic homological/derived infrastructure — No checked explicit calculation of Tor₁^ℤ(ℤ/p,B) was found; the generic Tor owner is not an explicit computation.

Proposition 3.1.2 — Tor over Z has only degree-one torsion

FC05-C03-U002 · §3.1; pp. 66–67; L1972–1982 · after FC05-C03-U001, FC05-C02-U073, FC05-C02-U066 · not formalized

For abelian A,B, Tor_1^Z(A,B) is torsion and Tor_n^Z(A,B)=0 for n≥2.

Not formalized. partial: generic homological/derived infrastructure — No checked theorem computes all higher Tor over ℤ in the source form.

Proposition 3.1.3 — Tor with Q/Z detects torsion

FC05-C03-U003 · §3.1; pp. 66–67; L1984–1992 · after FC05-C03-U001, FC05-C02-U073 · not formalized

Tor_1^Z(Q/Z,B) is the torsion subgroup of B.

Not formalized. partial: generic homological/derived infrastructure — No checked theorem identifies Tor₁^ℤ(ℚ/ℤ,B) with the torsion subgroup.

Proposition 3.1.4 — Torsionfree abelian groups are Tor-acyclic

FC05-C03-U004 · §3.1; pp. 66–67; L1994–1996 · after FC05-C02-U073 · not formalized

If A is torsionfree abelian, Tor_n^Z(A,B)=0 for n>0 and every B.

Not formalized. partial: generic homological/derived infrastructure — Pinned torsion-free/flat results over Bezout domains are adjacent, but no checked whole row identifies all higher Tor-acyclicity in the source form.

Reusable comparison — Balancing Tor over a commutative ring

FC05-C03-U005 · §3.1; pp. 66–67; L1998 · after FC05-C02-U075, FC05-C03-U003 · not formalized

For commutative R, Tor_*R(A,B)≅Tor_*R(B,A); in particular Tor_1^Z(A,Q/Z) is the torsion subgroup of A.

Not formalized. partial: generic homological/derived infrastructure — The source balance is stated for commutative R, but pinned Tor explicitly lacks the two-variable balancing theorem.

Corollary 3.1.5 — Torsionfree iff first Tor vanishes

FC05-C03-U006 · §3.1; pp. 66–67; L2000–2004 · after FC05-C03-U004, FC05-C03-U005 · not formalized

For abelian A, Tor_1^Z(A,-)=0 iff A is torsionfree iff Tor_1^Z(-,A)=0.

Not formalized. partial: generic homological/derived infrastructure — No checked theorem gives the source torsion-free iff Tor₁(-,A)/Tor₁(A,-) vanishing criterion for all abelian groups.

Calculation 3.1.6 — Periodic Tor over Z/m

FC05-C03-U007 · §3.1; pp. 66–67; L2006–2016 · after FC05-C02-U056 · not formalized

For d∣m, the periodic free resolution of Z/d over Z/m gives the stated alternating formulas for Tor_n^{Z/m}(Z/d,B) in all degrees.

Not formalized. partial: generic homological/derived infrastructure — No checked periodic Tor calculation for ℤ/m was found.

Example 3.1.7 — Tor with a regular element

FC05-C03-U008 · §3.1; pp. 66–67; L2018 · after FC05-C02-U056 · not formalized

If r is a left nonzerodivisor on R, then Tor_0^R(R/rR,B)=B/rB, Tor_1={b:rb=0}, and higher Tor vanishes.

Not formalized. partial: generic homological/derived infrastructure — No checked regular-element quotient Tor computation was found.

Definition 3.2.1 — Flat module

FC05-C03-U009 · §3.2; pp. 68–72; L2048 · after FC05-C02-U054, FC05-C01-U058 · not formalized

A left module B is flat when -⊗_R B is exact; right flatness is dual. Projectives are flat, but flat need not be projective.

Not formalized. partial: generic homological/derived infrastructure — Weibel defines flatness for modules over arbitrary associative rings. P/H Module.Flat is declared only under [CommSemiring R] and lists noncommutative generalization as TODO.

Theorem 3.2.2 — Localization is flat

FC05-C03-U010 · §3.2; pp. 68–72; L2050 · after FC05-C03-U009, FC05-C02-U069 · not formalized

For a central multiplicative set S⊂R, S^{-1}R is flat as an R-module, realized as a filtered colimit of free modules.

Not formalized. partial: generic homological/derived infrastructure — Weibel uses localization at a central multiplicative set in a possibly noncommutative ring. P/H localization-flatness APIs are commutative-base.

Definition 3.2.3 — Pontryagin dual

FC05-C03-U011 · §3.2; pp. 68–72; L2070 · after FC05-C02-U030, FC05-C02-U024 · not formalized

For a left R-module B, B*=Hom_Ab(B,Q/Z) with right action (fr)(b)=f(rb).

Not formalized. partial: generic homological/derived infrastructure — P/H character modules are developed over commutative scalars; no checked owner gives the source right R-module structure on Hom_Ab(B,ℚ/ℤ) for arbitrary associative R.

Proposition 3.2.4 — Equivalent criteria for flatness

FC05-C03-U012 · §3.2; pp. 68–72; L2072–2102 · after FC05-C03-U009, FC05-C03-U011, FC05-C02-U022, FC05-C02-U056 · not formalized

For a left R-module B: flatness, injectivity of B*, injectivity of I⊗B→B for right ideals I, and Tor_1^R(R/I,B)=0 are equivalent.

Not formalized. partial: generic homological/derived infrastructure — The source flatness equivalence bundle is arbitrary-ring and includes the noncommutative character-module criterion; P/H do not cover that generality.

Lemma 3.2.5 — Pontryagin dual detects injections

FC05-C03-U013 · §3.2; pp. 68–72; L2104–2106 · after FC05-C03-U011, FC05-C02-U024 · in Mathlib

f:B→C is injective iff f:C→B* is surjective.

Formalized. CharacterModule.dual_surjective_iff_injective (apply after restricting the underlying additive map to ℤ) — The pinned character module is Hom_Ab(-,ℚ/ℤ) at scalar ring ℤ; dualization is surjective exactly when the original map is injective, which is the source detection statement for any underlying abelian-group map.

Formalized. Module.FinitePresentation; Module.FinitePresentation.exists_fin' — Pinned finite presentation is defined for arbitrary semirings/rings, not only commutative ones, and gives exactly a finite-generator finite-relation presentation.

Lemma 3.2.6 — σ is an isomorphism for finitely presented modules

Theorem 3.2.7 — Finitely presented flat modules are projective

FC05-C03-U017 · §3.2; pp. 68–72; L2130–2142 · after FC05-C03-U009, FC05-C03-U014, FC05-C03-U016, FC05-C03-U013, FC05-C02-U013 · not formalized

Every finitely presented flat R-module is projective.

Not formalized. partial: generic homological/derived infrastructure — P/H prove finitely-presented-flat implies projective in commutative settings, but the source row is for arbitrary associative rings.

Flat Resolution Lemma 3.2.8 — Tor can be computed from flat resolutions

FC05-C03-U018 · §3.2; pp. 68–72; L2144–2156 · after FC05-C03-U009, FC05-C02-U056, FC05-C02-U044 · not formalized

A resolution of either variable by flat modules computes Tor by tensor homology, not only projective resolutions.

Not formalized. partial: generic homological/derived infrastructure — No noncommutative flat-resolution/Tor owner exists in P/H.

Proposition 3.2.9 — Flat base change for Tor

FC05-C03-U019 · §3.2; pp. 68–72; L2158–2164 · after FC05-C03-U010, FC05-C03-U018 · not formalized

If R→T and T is flat over R, then Tor_nR(A,C)≅Tor_nT(A⊗_R T,C) for R-module A and T-module C.

Not formalized. partial: generic homological/derived infrastructure — No checked source-general flat-base-change theorem for Tor was found.

Corollary 3.2.10 — Two-sided flat base change for Tor

FC05-C03-U020 · §3.2; pp. 68–72; L2166–2172 · after FC05-C03-U019, FC05-C03-U009 · not formalized

Lemma 3.2.11 — Central scalar action on Tor

FC05-C03-U021 · §3.2; pp. 68–72; L2176–2178 · after FC05-C02-U056 · not formalized

Multiplication by a central r on A induces multiplication by r on Tor_n^R(A,B).

Not formalized. partial: generic homological/derived infrastructure — The scalar action is on arbitrary-ring Tor and depends on the missing noncommutative Tor owner.

Corollary 3.2.12 — Tor inherits quotient-ring module structure

FC05-C03-U022 · §3.2; pp. 68–72; L2180 · after FC05-C03-U021 · not formalized

If A is an R/r-module, then Tor_*^R(A,B) is annihilated by r and hence is an R/r-module.

Not formalized. partial: generic homological/derived infrastructure — The quotient-ring Tor module structure depends on the same missing arbitrary-ring Tor layer.

Corollary 3.2.13 — Localization criterion for vanishing of Tor

FC05-C03-U023 · §3.2; pp. 68–72; L2182–2192 · after FC05-C03-U020 · not formalized

For commutative R, Tor_n^R(A,B)=0 iff all prime localizations vanish, equivalently all maximal localizations vanish.

Not formalized. partial: generic homological/derived infrastructure — No checked localization criterion for Tor vanishing at all maximals was found.

Lemma 3.3.1 — Ext over Z vanishes above degree one

FC05-C03-U024 · §3.3; pp. 73–75; L2198–2204 · after FC05-C02-U048, FC05-C02-U023 · not formalized

For abelian groups A,B, Ext_Z^n(A,B)=0 for n≥2.

Not formalized. partial: generic homological/derived infrastructure — No checked theorem states the full Ext^n_ℤ=0 calculation for n≥2 in this source form.

Calculation 3.3.2 — Ext from Z/p

FC05-C03-U025 · §3.3; pp. 73–75; L2206–2210 · after FC05-C02-U079, FC05-C03-U024 · not formalized

Ext_Z^0(Z/p,B) is the p-torsion subgroup, Ext_Z^1(Z/p,B)=B/pB, and higher Ext vanishes.
