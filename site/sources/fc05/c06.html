Definition — Left G-module and G-mod

FC05-C06-U001 · §6.1 Definitions and First Properties; pp. 160–166; L4852 · in Mathlib

For a group G, a left G-module is an abelian group A with an action of G by additive automorphisms. G-mod identifies with left ZG-modules and with functors from the one-object category G to Ab.
