### **Definition 5.2.1 — Homology spectral sequence** {#fc05-c05-u002}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC05-C05-U002` · §5.2 Terminology; pp. 122–126; L3685–3697 · after [`FC05-C01-U013`](c01.html#fc05-c01-u013), [`FC05-C01-U014`](c01.html#fc05-c01-u014) · [`mathlib`]{.route-mathlib}
:::

In an abelian category A, a homology spectral sequence from E^a is a family E^r_{pq} with d^r:E^r_{pq}→E^r_{p-r,q+r-1}, (d^r)^2=0, and specified isomorphisms E^{r+1}*{pq}≅H(E^r,d^r)*{pq}; morphisms commute with d and induce the next-page maps.
