Definition/convention — Exactness for module sequences

FC05-C01-U001 · §1.1; pp. 1–4; L255–261 · in Mathlib

Fix an associative ring R; mod-R denotes right R-modules. A sequence A→B→C is exact at B when ker(g)=im(f).

Formalized. ShortComplex.moduleCat_exact_iff_range_eq_ker; LinearMap.exact_iff — For module maps, categorical exactness is equivalent to equality of range and kernel, exactly the source convention.

Definition 1.1.1 — Chain complex; cycles, boundaries, homology

FC05-C01-U002 · §1.1; pp. 1–4; L263–269 · after FC05-C01-U001 · in Mathlib

A chain complex has d_n:C_n→C_{n-1} with d²=0; Z_n=ker d_n, B_n=im d_{n+1}, H_n=Z_n/B_n.

Formalized. HomologicalComplex; ChainComplex; HomologicalComplex.cycles; opcycles; homology — The differential-square-zero object, cycles/boundaries model, and homology quotient are all canonical pinned APIs.

Construction/category — Category Ch(mod-R) and chain maps

FC05-C01-U003 · §1.1; pp. 1–4; L273–277 · after FC05-C01-U002 · in Mathlib

Chain maps are degreewise maps commuting with differentials; composition is degreewise, giving Ch(mod-R).

Formalized. HomologicalComplex; category instance; HomologicalComplex.Hom — Morphisms are degreewise maps commuting with differentials and compose degreewise.

Definition 1.1.2 — Quasi-isomorphism

FC05-C01-U004 · §1.1; pp. 1–4; L285 · after FC05-C01-U002, FC05-C01-U003 · in Mathlib

A chain map is a quasi-isomorphism when it induces isomorphisms on every homology group.

Formalized. QuasiIso; HomologicalComplex.quasiIso_iff — QuasiIso is exactly isomorphism on every homology object.

Definition/convention — Cochain complexes and cohomology

FC05-C01-U005 · §1.1; pp. 1–4; L293 · after FC05-C01-U002, FC05-C01-U004 · in Mathlib

Reindex C^n=C_{-n}; define cocycles, coboundaries, H^n, cochain maps, and quasi-isomorphisms dually.

Formalized. CochainComplex; HomologicalComplex.homology — The pinned upward complex convention supplies cochain complexes, maps, cohomology, and quasi-isomorphisms.

Definition/convention — Bounded complexes and standard subcategories

FC05-C01-U006 · §1.1; pp. 1–4; L295–297 · after FC05-C01-U002 · in Mathlib

Defines bounded, bounded above/below, amplitude [a,b], and Ch_b, Ch_-, Ch_+, Ch_{≥0}; gives cochain analogues.

Formalized. HomologicalComplex.IsStrictlySupported; CochainComplex.IsStrictlyGE; IsStrictlyLE; ObjectProperty.FullSubcategory — Strict support on intervals/half-lines gives bounded, bounded-above/below, and amplitude subcategories; the same machinery applies after chain/cochain reindexing.

FC05-C01-U007 · §1.1; pp. 1–4; L301 · after FC05-C01-U002 · not formalized

For a geometric simplicial complex, the alternating face map gives a simplicial chain complex; its homology is simplicial homology.

Not formalized. partial: AbstractSimplicialComplex; AlgebraicTopology.alternatingFaceMapComplex; simplicial-set homology — P/H formalize abstract/geometric simplicial complexes and simplicial-set chain homology separately, but no checked bridge sends a geometric simplicial complex to the source chain complex/homology. Application 1.1.4 — Singular homology

FC05-C01-U008 · §1.1; pp. 1–4; L305–311 · after FC05-C01-U002, FC05-C01-U004 · not formalized

The free module on singular k-simplices with alternating face differential gives S_(X); H_n(X;R)=H_n(S_(X)), with simplicial-to-singular quasi-isomorphism for triangulated X.

Definitions — Ab-categories and additive functors

FC05-C01-U009 · §1.2; pp. 5–9; L317–325 · in Mathlib

An Ab-category has abelian-group Hom sets and bilinear composition; an additive functor preserves addition on Hom groups.

Formalized. Preadditive; Functor.Additive — Hom groups with bilinear composition and addition-preserving functors are the pinned categorical interfaces.

Definition/construction — Additive category; zero object, products and coproducts

FC05-C01-U010 · §1.2; pp. 5–9; L327–333 · after FC05-C01-U009, FC05-C01-U003 · in Mathlib

An additive category is an Ab-category with finite products and coproducts; in Ch these are degreewise, hence Ch is additive.

Formalized. Preadditive; HasZeroObject; finite (co)products/biproducts; HomologicalComplex additive instances — Pinned additive-category infrastructure supplies zero objects and finite biproducts; chain-complex limits/colimits are degreewise.

Definitions/constructions — Subcomplexes, quotient complexes, degreewise kernels/cokernels

FC05-C01-U011 · §1.2; pp. 5–9; L337–343 · after FC05-C01-U002, FC05-C01-U003 · in Mathlib

A subcomplex is degreewise stable under d; quotient differentials descend, and kernels/cokernels of chain maps are formed degreewise.

Formalized. HomologicalComplexLimits; kernels/cokernels in HomologicalComplex — Kernels and cokernels of complex maps are created degreewise; quotient complexes are the corresponding cokernels.

Definition 1.2.1 — Categorical kernel, cokernel, monic and epic

FC05-C01-U012 · §1.2; pp. 5–9; L345 · after FC05-C01-U010 · in Mathlib

Defines kernels and cokernels by universal properties in an additive category; monic/epi mean zero kernel/cokernel.

Formalized. Limits.kernel; Limits.cokernel; Mono; Epi; abelian kernel/cokernel APIs — The universal constructions and their mono/epi characterizations are canonical.

Definition 1.2.2 — Abelian category and image factorization

FC05-C01-U013 · §1.2; pp. 5–9; L354–360 · after FC05-C01-U012 · in Mathlib

An abelian category is additive, has kernels/cokernels, and every mono/epi is a kernel/cokernel; im(f)=ker(coker f), yielding epi–mono factorization.

Formalized. CategoryTheory.Abelian; Abelian.image; Abelian.coimageImageComparison — Pinned abelian categories have kernels/cokernels and canonical epi–mono image factorization.

Definitions/conventions — Exact sequences and complexes in an abelian category

FC05-C01-U014 · §1.2; pp. 5–9; L372–376 · after FC05-C01-U013, FC05-C01-U002 · in Mathlib

Exactness is defined by im=ker; defines abelian subcategory and extends chain complexes, maps, and homology to any abelian category A.

Formalized. ShortComplex.Exact; ComposableArrows.Exact; HomologicalComplex — Exactness is the categorical image=kernel condition and extends uniformly to complexes in any abelian category.

Theorem 1.2.3 — Ch(A) is abelian

FC05-C01-U015 · §1.2; pp. 5–9; L378–380 · after FC05-C01-U014, FC05-C01-U011 · in Mathlib

For abelian A, Ch(A) is abelian; a chain map is monic or epic exactly when each component is.

Formalized. HomologicalComplexAbelian; componentwise mono/epi instances — For abelian A, the homological-complex category is abelian and monic/epic maps are detected degreewise.

Example 1.2.4 — Double complexes

FC05-C01-U016 · §1.2; pp. 5–9; L386–400 · after FC05-C01-U002 · not formalized

A bicomplex C_{p,q} has horizontal/vertical differentials squaring to zero and anticommuting; rows/columns are complexes; bounded and first-quadrant variants are named.

Not formalized. partial: HomologicalComplex₂ in HomologicalBicomplex.lean — P/H use the iterated-complex convention in which the two differentials commute before totalization signs are inserted. No checked owner represents Weibel’s raw anticommuting bicomplex data as stated.

Sign Trick 1.2.5 — Double complexes as complexes of complexes

FC05-C01-U017 · §1.2; pp. 5–9; L402–408 · after FC05-C01-U016, FC05-C01-U003 · not formalized

Replacing d^v on column p by (-1)^p d^v turns vertical maps into chain maps and identifies double complexes with Ch(Ch).

Not formalized. partial: HomologicalComplex₂; ComplexShape.ε₁; ComplexShape.ε₂; HomologicalComplex₂.total — Pinned totalization implements the standard signs after choosing the commuting iterated-complex convention, but no checked declaration gives the source equivalence obtained by replacing the vertical differential by (-1)^p dᵛ.

Total Complexes 1.2.6 — Direct-sum and product totalizations

FC05-C01-U018 · §1.2; pp. 5–9; L410–424 · after FC05-C01-U016, FC05-C01-U017 · not formalized

Defines Tot^⊕ and Tot^Π on diagonals with differential dh+dv; for bounded diagonals they coincide; existence is tied to coproducts/products.

Not formalized. partial: HomologicalComplex₂.total (coproduct/direct-sum totalization) — P/H provide the direct-sum/coproduct total complex with standard signs. No product totalization or theorem identifying the two on bounded diagonals was found in P/H/current/external Lean.

Definitions — Complete and cocomplete abelian categories

FC05-C01-U019 · §1.2; pp. 5–9; L424 · after FC05-C01-U018 · in Mathlib

Names abelian categories with all products complete and with all coproducts cocomplete, controlling existence of the two totalizations.

Formalized. Limits.HasProducts; Limits.HasCoproducts; HasLimits; HasColimits — The source completeness/cocompleteness conditions are exactly existence of all products/coproducts (equivalently the corresponding limits/colimits in this additive setting).

Truncations 1.2.7 — Good and brutal truncations

FC05-C01-U020 · §1.2; pp. 5–9; L430–438 · after FC05-C01-U002 · in Mathlib

Defines τ_{≥n}, τ_{<n} preserving homology in their ranges, and brutal truncations σ obtained by replacing boundary/cycle terms by C_n.

Formalized. HomologicalComplex.truncGE; truncLE; stupidTrunc; support lemmas — Pinned canonical truncations preserve the intended homology ranges; stupidTrunc is the brutal truncation.

Translation 1.2.8 — Translation/shift of complexes

FC05-C01-U021 · §1.2; pp. 5–9; L440–455 · after FC05-C01-U002, FC05-C01-U003 · in Mathlib

Defines C[p]n=C{n+p} with differential (-1)^p d, H_n(C[p])=H_{n+p}(C), and translation as a functor on maps.

Formalized. homological-complex shift functors; HomologicalComplex.shiftFunctor infrastructure — Pinned shifts implement translated complexes with the standard differential sign and shift homology accordingly.

Theorem 1.3.1 — Long exact homology/cohomology sequence

FC05-C01-U022 · §1.3; pp. 10–14; L480–492 · after FC05-C01-U015, FC05-C01-U014 · in Mathlib

A short exact sequence of chain complexes has natural connecting maps and a long exact homology sequence; dually for cochain complexes.

Formalized. HomologicalComplex.HomologySequence; ShortComplex.ShortExact; HomologySequence.exact — A short exact sequence of complexes gets connecting morphisms and the natural long exact homology sequence in an arbitrary abelian category; cohomology is the upward-indexed specialization.

Snake Lemma 1.3.2 — Snake lemma kernel–cokernel sequence

FC05-C01-U023 · §1.3; pp. 10–14; L517–535 · after FC05-C01-U014 · in Mathlib

A commutative two-row diagram of modules with exact rows yields the standard exact sequence from kernels through cokernels, with endpoint extensions under mono/epi hypotheses.

Formalized. ShortComplex.SnakeInput; ShortComplex.SnakeInput.snake_lemma — Pinned snake-lemma infrastructure yields the kernel-to-cokernel exact sequence with endpoint refinements.

Reusable comparison — Snake lemma in arbitrary abelian categories
