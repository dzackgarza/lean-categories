### Definition — simplicial category Δ; simplicial and cosimplicial objects {#fc05-c08-u001}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC05-C08-U001` · §8.1 Simplicial Objects; pp. 254–258; L7570 · [in Mathlib]{.route-mathlib}
:::

Let Δ be the category of finite ordinals [n]={0<1<…<n} and monotone maps. A simplicial object in C is a contravariant functor Δ→C; a cosimplicial object is a covariant functor Δ→C.

::: {.unit-lean}
**Formalized.** [`Mathlib/AlgebraicTopology/SimplexCategory/Basic.lean::SimplexCategory`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/AlgebraicTopology/SimplexCategory/Basic.html); [`Mathlib/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialObject/Basic.lean::CategoryTheory.SimplicialObject`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/AlgebraicTopology/SimplicialObject/Basic.html#CategoryTheory.SimplicialObject), [`CategoryTheory.CosimplicialObject`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/find/?pattern=CategoryTheory.CosimplicialObject#doc) — Pinned Mathlib models objects `[n]`, monotone maps, and the contravariant/covariant functor definitions exactly.
