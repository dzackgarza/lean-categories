Chapter 2. Derived Functors

FC05 — Weibel, An Introduction to Homological Algebra.

This chapter contains 18 definitions, 5 theorems, 4 propositions, 7 lemmas, 7 corollaries, 8 examples, 6 constructions and 27 other statements.

NoteWhich versions of Lean and Mathlib were searched
  • Project-first search was performed at repo HEAD f901738. LeanCategories/Homological/DerivedDimension.lean owns the source-facing higher-left-derived vanishing predicate LeanCategories.Homological.IsCoacyclicFor; this is the canonical project route for U041. Other C02 project homological files reexport or specialize Mathlib owners rather than replacing them.
  • Pinned Mathlib is P=db584cd6d46c92f209a44c0f1c829460d327499d, Lean 4.33.0. P directly supplies categorical projectives/injectives, enough-projective/enough-injective structures, projective and injective resolutions and comparison homotopies, generic left/right derived functors, intrinsic derived-category Ext and both resolution computations, adjunction/exactness machinery, sheaf stalk/skyscraper and (co)image functors, arbitrary (co)limits in abelian categories from products/coproducts, filtered categories, ModuleCat filtered colimits, AB5, and the Hom-complex/homotopy-category cohomology theorem.
  • Two source-shaped infrastructure assertions were compile-checked against P: abelian sheaves on a topological space infer EnoughInjectives through Mathlib’s Grothendieck-category machinery, and an abelian category with arbitrary coproducts (respectively products) infers all small colimits (respectively limits). These justify U036/U061/U063 without weakening their existence claims.
  • The source is explicitly written for an arbitrary associative ring with right and left modules. Mathlib’s standard TensorProduct and the monoidal/closed structure on ModuleCat R still require a commutative scalar ring; TensorProduct.liftAddHom itself notes that the first action should be a right action but is currently represented using commutativity. Therefore the noncommutative tensor-Hom, tensor, Tor, tensor-complex, and Tor-balancing rows U031 and U054-U057/U074-U075 are not credited from commutative-base Mathlib.
  • Current Mathlib was rechecked at H=5aedf732b6987e8c26ab3c9ebc855314f82b045f. Searches at H did not add a δ-functor abstraction, Horseshoe lemma, generic syzygy owner, noncommutative module tensor/Tor, Tor balancing, Acyclic Assembly Lemma, balanced-multivariable-functor definitions, or the derived direct-sum/filtered-colimit preservation theorems left unmatched here.
  • Open Mathlib PR search on 2026-09-06 for δ-functors, Horseshoe, Tor balancing, homology/colimit, and sheaf enough-injectives found no applicable open PR. Thus no C02 route is attributed to an open Mathlib PR.
  • leanprover-community/lean-liquid@087fffad55dc1dd8d54ab35c9816926a45b8c0fd was rechecked at its current HEAD. Its legacy Lean 3 src/for_mathlib/universal_delta_functor/basic.lean implements a covariant cohomological δ-functor category, morphisms, universality, effaceability, and universal_of_effaceable, but the repository has no LICENSE/COPYING file, uses Lean 3.48 with Mathlib 5947fb69058f2fa5ac8a4476182707a52f5cf2be, and does not directly implement the homological dual/source left-derived package. It is therefore evidence of prior Lean work but not an acceptable reference-port under this mapping contract.
  • U071 has one exact revision-specific external owner: TauCetiProject/TauCeti@a8e6879667d6e38093b899315881b4f7b8e4b55a, Apache-2.0, Lean v4.34.0-rc2, Mathlib 5fcc6656b48daf74b23607447c65ea30934f1b8f.   • Pinned CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Tor.lean explicitly records that the two Tor variable constructions are expected to be isomorphic but that this balancing has not been proved there. This is positive evidence for the strict negatives U057/U075 rather than grounds to count them as Mathlib coverage.
  • The older definition-only Weibel catalogue is not route authority. Its generic Tor and “balanced functor” hints lose the source’s noncommutative right/left-module generality and do not provide Weibel’s balanced-functor predicates; the Sweep-II rows here use the stricter checked interfaces instead.

Definition 2.1.1 — Homological and cohomological δ-functors

FC05-C02-U001 · §2.1; pp. 30–32; L984–1004 · after FC05-C01-U014 · not formalized

A graded family of additive functors with natural connecting maps satisfying long-exactness and naturality; negative degrees are set to zero.

Not formalized. partial: pinned long-exact homology sequences and connecting-map naturality — P/H have the ingredients for individual long exact sequences but no homological/cohomological δ-functor structure with graded functors and connecting natural transformations.

Searched: N

Definition — Projective object

FC05-C02-U009 · §2.2; pp. 33–37; L1064–1074 · after FC05-C01-U013 · in Mathlib

P is projective when maps P→C lift across every epimorphism B→C.

Formalized. CategoryTheory.Projective — Pinned Projective is the categorical lifting property against epimorphisms.

Proposition 2.2.1 — Projective modules are direct summands of free modules

FC05-C02-U010 · §2.2; pp. 33–37; L1076–1078 · after FC05-C02-U009 · in Mathlib

Formalized. Module.Projective; direct-summand-of-free characterization in Algebra/Module/Projective.lean — Pinned module projectivity is equivalent to being a direct summand of a free module.

Not formalized. partial: projective modules over product rings; vector-bundle/projective-module infrastructure — No checked P/H/current/external declaration realizes both source example families, in particular the tangent-bundle coordinate-ring example.

Definition — Enough projectives

FC05-C02-U012 · §2.2; pp. 33–37; L1085–1087 · after FC05-C02-U009 · in Mathlib

An abelian category has enough projectives if every object receives an epimorphism from a projective object.

Formalized. EnoughProjectives; ModuleCat.enoughProjectives — The pinned predicate is exactly that every object admits an epimorphism from a projective object; module categories have the instance.

Lemma 2.2.3 — Projectivity detected by Hom exactness

FC05-C02-U013 · §2.2; pp. 33–37; L1089–1097 · after FC05-C02-U009, FC05-C01-U060, FC05-C01-U058 · in Mathlib

M is projective iff Hom_A(M,-) is exact.

Formalized. projective exactness APIs in CategoryTheory/Preadditive/Projective/Basic.lean — Pinned projective-object APIs characterize projectivity by exactness/surjectivity of representable Hom.

Definition — Complex of projectives

FC05-C02-U014 · §2.2; pp. 33–37; L1099 · after FC05-C01-U002, FC05-C02-U009 · in Mathlib

A chain complex is a complex of projectives when every component is projective.

Formalized. ∀ n, Projective (K.X n); CochainComplex.isKProjective_of_projective — The source condition is represented directly by the degreewise Projective instances; bounded degreewise-projective complexes are promoted to K-projective complexes.

Definition 2.2.4 — Left and projective resolutions

FC05-C02-U015 · §2.2; pp. 33–37; L1105–1111 · after FC05-C01-U014, FC05-C02-U014 · in Mathlib

A left resolution P→M is an exact augmented nonnegative complex; it is projective when each P_i is projective.

Formalized. ProjectiveResolution; HasProjectiveResolutions — Pinned projective resolutions are exact augmented nonnegative complexes with projective terms.

Lemma 2.2.5 — Existence of projective resolutions

FC05-C02-U016 · §2.2; pp. 33–37; L1113–1118 · after FC05-C02-U012, FC05-C02-U015 · in Mathlib

Every module, and every object in an abelian category with enough projectives, has a projective resolution.

Formalized. HasProjectiveResolutions; EnoughProjectives.hasProjectiveResolutions — Enough projectives canonically supplies projective resolutions; module categories satisfy the hypotheses.

Comparison Theorem 2.2.6 — Maps lift to projective resolutions uniquely up to homotopy

FC05-C02-U017 · §2.2; pp. 33–37; L1122–1126 · after FC05-C02-U015, FC05-C01-U035 · in Mathlib

A map M→N lifts from a projective resolution of M to any resolution of N; any two lifts are chain homotopic.

Formalized. ProjectiveResolution.lift; liftHomotopy; homotopyEquiv — Maps lift to projective resolutions and any two lifts are homotopic; projective resolutions are homotopy equivalent.

Porism 2.2.7 — Comparison theorem for projective complexes

FC05-C02-U018 · §2.2; pp. 33–37; L1128–1134 · after FC05-C02-U014, FC05-C02-U017 · in Mathlib

The source need only be a complex of projectives with appropriate augmentation; the same existence/uniqueness-up-to-homotopy argument applies.

Formalized. CochainComplex.IsKProjective; isKProjective_of_projective; K-projective orthogonality — Pinned K-projective theory generalizes the porism: bounded complexes of projectives have maps into acyclic targets null-homotopic, which supplies the comparison existence/uniqueness mechanism beyond chosen resolutions.

Horseshoe Lemma 2.2.8 — Compatible projective resolutions for a short exact sequence

FC05-C02-U019 · §2.2; pp. 33–37; L1162–1196 · after FC05-C02-U015, FC05-C01-U014 · not formalized

For 0→M′→M→M″→0 and projective resolutions of the ends, constructs P_n=P′_n⊕P″_n resolving M in a short exact sequence of complexes.

Not formalized. partial: projective resolutions; biproducts; short exact complexes — No Horseshoe construction or source-shaped compatible middle resolution was found in P/H/current Mathlib, open PRs, or targeted external Lean search.

Searched: N

Definition — Injective object

FC05-C02-U020 · §2.3; pp. 38–42; L1206–1210 · after FC05-C01-U013 · in Mathlib

I is injective when maps A→I extend across every monomorphism A→B.

Formalized. CategoryTheory.Injective — Pinned Injective is exactly extension of maps across monomorphisms.

Definition — Enough injectives

FC05-C02-U021 · §2.3; pp. 38–42; L1212 · after FC05-C02-U020 · in Mathlib

An abelian category has enough injectives if every object embeds in an injective object; products of injectives are injective when products exist.

Formalized. EnoughInjectives; Injective (∏ᶜ c) instance — The pinned predicate is enough injectives, and arbitrary existing products of injectives are injective.

Baer’s Criterion 2.3.1 — Injectivity criterion for modules

FC05-C02-U022 · §2.3; pp. 38–42; L1214–1222 · after FC05-C02-U020 · in Mathlib

A right R-module E is injective iff every map J→E from a right ideal J⊂R extends to R.

Formalized. Module.Baer; Module.Baer.iff_injective — Pinned Baer criterion is exactly extension from right ideals and is equivalent to module injectivity.

Corollary 2.3.2 — Injective modules over a PID are divisible

FC05-C02-U023 · §2.3; pp. 38–42; L1226 · after FC05-C02-U022 · not formalized

For R=Z or a PID, an R-module is injective iff it is divisible.

Not formalized. partial: Module.Baer; Module.Baer.of_divisible for ℤ — P proves the divisible-implies-injective construction for abelian groups and has generic Baer criterion, but no checked theorem/assembly covers the full source statement for arbitrary PIDs in both directions.

Searched: N

Example 2.3.3 — Injective divisible abelian groups
