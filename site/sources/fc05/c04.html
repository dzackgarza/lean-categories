Definitions 4.1.1 — Projective, injective, and flat dimensions

FC05-C04-U001 · §4.1; pp. 91–94; L2761–2781 · after FC05-C02-U015, FC05-C02-U027, FC05-C03-U018 · not formalized

For a right R-module A, pd(A), id(A), and fd(A) are the minimum lengths of projective, injective, and flat resolutions respectively, or infinity if no finite such resolution exists.

Not formalized. partial: adjacent dimension/commutative-algebra infrastructure — P owns projectiveDimension and injectiveDimension, but neither project nor P/H defines flat dimension for Weibel’s arbitrary associative-ring modules; the three-definition bundle is incomplete.

Global Dimension Theorem 4.1.2 — Equivalent definitions of right global dimension

Named comparison — Wedderburn classification and Maschke example for semisimple rings

Theorem 4.2.2 — Characterizations of semisimple rings
