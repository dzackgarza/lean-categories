Definition — Lie algebra over a commutative ring

FC05-C07-U001 · §7.1 Lie Algebras; pp. 216–218; L6378–6382 · in Mathlib

Fix a commutative ring k. A Lie algebra is a nonassociative k-algebra with a bilinear bracket. After the word “satisfies,” the extraction preserves the displayed Jacobi identity but does not preserve any companion defining axiom; no absent condition is reconstructed here.
