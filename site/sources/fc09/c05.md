A Lubin-Tate series for a prime element $\pi$ of K is by definition a power series $e(X)\in\mathcal{O}_{K}X$ with the properties $$ e (X) \equiv \pi X \bmod \deg 2 \quad \text { and } \quad e (X) \equiv X ^ {q} \bmod \pi , $$ where $q = q_{K}$ denotes, as always, the number of elements in the residue class field of K.
