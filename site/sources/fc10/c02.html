FC10-C02-U001 · Chapter II, source L952 · after FC10-C01-U005 · not formalized

For a discrete valuation \(v\) and a real number \(0<a<1\), define \(|x|=a^{v(x)}\) for \(x\ne0\) and \(|0|=0\); this is an ultrametric absolute value.

Definition / construction

FC10-C02-U002 · Chapter II, source L972 · after FC10-C02-U001 · not formalized

For \(v\) discrete, let \(\widehat{K}\) be the completion of \(K\) for the topology defined by its absolute value; the topology does not depend on the choice of the number \(a\). Then \(\widehat{K}\) is a valued field whose absolute value extends that of \(K\).
