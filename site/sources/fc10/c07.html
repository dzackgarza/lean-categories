FC10-C07-U001 · Chapter VII, source L3797 · in Mathlib

Let \(G\) be a group, written multiplicatively, and \(A\) an abelian group, written additively. \(G\) acts on the left on \(A\) if a homomorphism \(G \to \operatorname{Aut}(A)\) is given; equivalently, if a map \((s,a) \mapsto s.a\) from \(G \times A\) to \(A\) is given satisfying \[ 1.a = a, \qquad s.(a + a') = s.a + s.a', \qquad (s.t).a = s.(t.a). \]

Formalized. Mathlib/RepresentationTheory/Basic.lean::Representation; Mathlib/RepresentationTheory/Rep/Basic.lean::Rep — A G-module is represented by the corresponding group action/representation.

FC10-C07-U002 · Chapter VII, source L3811 · after FC10-C07-U001 · in Mathlib

A \(G\)-homomorphism is a homomorphism of \(G\)-modules. A \(G\)-module \(A\) is projective (respectively injective) when \(\operatorname{Hom}_G(A,-)\) (respectively \(\operatorname{Hom}_G(-,A)\)) is exact.

FC10-C07-U003 · Chapter VII, source L3813 · after FC10-C07-U001 · not formalized

A \(G\)-module \(A\) is induced if it has the form \(\mathbb{Z}[G] \otimes X\) for an abelian group \(X\), the tensor product taken over \(\mathbb{Z}\); equivalently, if \(A\) contains a subgroup \(X\) with \(A = \bigoplus_{s \in G} s.X\). Every \(G\)-module is a quotient of an induced one: writing \(A_{0}\) for the abelian group underlying \(A\), every element of \(\mathbb{Z}[G] \otimes A_{0}\) is a sum of elements \(s \otimes x\) with \(x \in A_{0}\), \(s \in G\), and sending such an element to \(s.x \in A\) defines a homomorphism \[ \pi \colon \mathbb{Z}[G] \otimes A_{0} \to A, \] which is a surjective \(G\)-homomorphism.

Not formalized. Ordinary group (co)homology is formalized, but this full functorial/exact/nonabelian statement is not packaged by the checked APIs.
