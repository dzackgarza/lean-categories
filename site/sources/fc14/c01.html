Convention: ambient field and dimension convention

FC14-C01-U001 · Chapter I opening; p. 1 · in Mathlib

F denotes an arbitrary commutative field. Lie algebras are finite-dimensional over F unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Formalized. Lean/Mathlib core::Field; Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/FiniteDimensional/Defs.lean::FiniteDimensional — Field F and FiniteDimensional F L are exactly the standing coefficient/dimension hypotheses.

Definition: Lie algebra, bracket, and Jacobi identity

FC14-C01-U002 · §1.1; p. 1 · in Mathlib

A Lie algebra over F is an F-vector space L with a bilinear bracket [x,y] satisfying [x,x]=0 and the Jacobi identity [x,[y,z]]+[y,[z,x]]+[z,[x,y]]=0. Anticommutativity [x,y]=−[y,x] follows; conversely it implies [x,x]=0 when char F≠2.

Formalized. Mathlib/Algebra/Lie/Basic.lean::LieRing, LieAlgebra, LieEquiv — LieRing packages alternation and Jacobi, LieAlgebra bilinearity, and LieEquiv is a bracket-preserving linear equivalence.

Definition: Lie-algebra isomorphism

FC14-C01-U003 · §1.1; p. 1 · after FC14-C01-U002 · in Mathlib

An isomorphism L→L′ is a vector-space isomorphism preserving brackets.

Formalized. Mathlib/Algebra/Lie/Basic.lean::LieRing, LieAlgebra, LieEquiv — LieRing packages alternation and Jacobi, LieAlgebra bilinearity, and LieEquiv is a bracket-preserving linear equivalence.

Definition: Lie subalgebra

FC14-C01-U004 · §1.1; p. 1 · after FC14-C01-U002 · in Mathlib

A subspace K⊆L is a Lie subalgebra when [K,K]⊆K; it inherits a Lie-algebra structure.

Formalized. Mathlib/Algebra/Lie/Subalgebra.lean::LieSubalgebra — A LieSubalgebra F L is exactly a linear subspace closed under the bracket and inherits the Lie structure.

Construction: general linear Lie algebra gl(V)

FC14-C01-U005 · §1.2; p. 2 · after FC14-C01-U002 · in Mathlib

For a vector space V, End(V) becomes a Lie algebra under [x,y]=xy−yx; this Lie algebra is gl(V). Relative to a basis, gl(V)≅gl(n,F) with [e_ij,e_kl]=δ_jk e_il−δ_li e_kj.

Formalized. Mathlib/Algebra/Lie/OfAssociative.lean::LieRing.ofAssociativeRing, LieAlgebra.ofAssociativeAlgebra; Mathlib/Algebra/Lie/Matrix.lean::lieEquivMatrix’ — Mathlib gives every endomorphism algebra the commutator Lie bracket and identifies endomorphisms of a coordinate module with matrices as Lie algebras; the matrix-unit bracket formula is the entrywise specialization.

Definition: linear Lie algebra

FC14-C01-U006 · §1.2; p. 2 · after FC14-C01-U004, FC14-C01-U005 · in Mathlib

A linear Lie algebra is a Lie subalgebra of gl(V).

Formalized. Mathlib/Algebra/Lie/Subalgebra.lean::LieSubalgebra; Mathlib/Algebra/Lie/OfAssociative.lean::Module.End.instLieModule — A linear Lie algebra is exactly a LieSubalgebra of the commutator Lie algebra Module.End F V.

Notation: commutator subspace [H,K]

FC14-C01-U012 · §1.2; p. 3 · after FC14-C01-U002 · in Mathlib

For subalgebras H,K⊆L, [H,K] denotes the subspace spanned by commutators [x,y] with x∈H,y∈K.

Definition: F-algebra

FC14-C01-U013 · §1.3; p. 4 · after FC14-C01-U001 · in Mathlib

An F-algebra here means an F-vector space equipped with a bilinear multiplication, not assumed associative.

Formalized. Mathlib/Algebra/Lie/NonUnitalNonAssocAlgebra.lean::NonUnitalNonAssocAlgebra — Mathlib’s nonunital nonassociative algebra interface is precisely a module with bilinear multiplication, with no associativity assumption.

Definition: derivation and Der(A)

FC14-C01-U014 · §1.3; p. 4 · after FC14-C01-U005, FC14-C01-U013 · not formalized

A derivation δ:A→A is F-linear and satisfies δ(ab)=aδ(b)+δ(a)b. Der(A) is a vector subspace of End(A) closed under commutators, hence a Lie subalgebra of gl(A).
