### Definition/construction: integral Chevalley form L(Z) {#fc14-c07-u008}

The Z-span L(Z) of a Chevalley basis is a free Z-lattice in L and a Lie algebra over Z. It is independent of the chosen base and, up to Z-isomorphism, of the Chevalley basis.
