### Definition: Lie algebra, bracket, and Jacobi identity {#fc14-c01-u002}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC14-C01-U002` · §1.1; p. 1 · [`mathlib`]{.route-mathlib}
:::

A Lie algebra over F is an F-vector space L with a bilinear bracket [x,y] satisfying [x,x]=0 and the Jacobi identity [x,[y,z]]+[y,[z,x]]+[z,[x,y]]=0. Anticommutativity [x,y]=−[y,x] follows; conversely it implies [x,x]=0 when char F≠2.

::: {.unit-lean}
**In Lean.** Mathlib/Algebra/Lie/Basic.lean::LieRing, LieAlgebra, LieEquiv — `LieRing` packages alternation and Jacobi, `LieAlgebra` bilinearity, and `LieEquiv` is a bracket-preserving linear equivalence.
:::
