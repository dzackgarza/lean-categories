Convention — surfaces, curves, and points in Chapter V

FC06-C05-U001 · §1 Geometry on a Surface; pp. 357–368; L9649 · after FC06-C02-U273, FC06-C02-U091 · not formalized

A surface means a nonsingular projective surface over the fixed algebraically closed field k. A curve on a surface means any effective divisor, possibly singular, reducible, or nonreduced; points are closed unless stated otherwise.
