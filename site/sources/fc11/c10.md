Proposition 10.2.2 (Classification (I)). 1. For $p$ odd, any (non-degenerate) symmetric (quadratic) $p$ -adic lattice is isometric to an orthogonal direct sum of rank one $p$ -adic lattices of the form $\langle u p ^ { k } \rangle$ , where $u \in \mathbb { Z } _ { p } ^ { \times }$ and $k \geq 0$ . 2. Dyadic symmetric, respectively quadratic lattices split into an orthogonal direct sum of rank one lattices of the form $\langle u \cdot 2 ^ { k } \rangle$ , $u \in \{ \pm 1 , \pm 3 \}$ , where $k \geq 0$ , respectively $k \geq 1$ , together with copies of $U _ { k }$ and of $V _ { k }$ , $k \geq 0$ .

Proposition 10.2.6. A non-degenerate symmetric $p$ -adic lattice $( L , b )$ has a Jordan splitting, i.e., $$ L = L _ { 0 } \oplus L _ { 1 } ( p ) \oplus \cdots \oplus L _ { r } ( p ^ { r } ) , $$ where each $L _ { j }$ is unimodular. The rank of $L _ { j }$ as well as its discriminant, $\operatorname { d i s c } ( L _ { j } ) \in$ $\mathsf { D } ( \mathbb { Z } _ { p } )$ , is uniquely determined by $( L , b )$ .
