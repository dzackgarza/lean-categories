### Definition / construction {#fc11-c03-u002}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC11-C03-U002` · Chapter 3, source L1969-L1973 · [in Mathlib]{.route-mathlib}
:::

Over a field of characteristic different from $2$, a quadratic form $q$ has associated symmetric bilinear form $x\cdot y=\frac12(q(x+y)-q(x)-q(y))$, so that $x\cdot x=q(x)$.

::: {.unit-lean}
**Formalized.** [`Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/Basic.lean::QuadraticMap.associatedHom, QuadraticMap.associated_apply, QuadraticMap.associated_eq_self_apply`](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/QuadraticForm/Basic.html) — When 2 is invertible, associatedHom is one half of the polar form q(x+y)-q(x)-q(y), and associated_eq_self_apply gives b(x,x)=q(x), exactly the source convention.
:::

The concept of a contiguous chain of orthogonal bases will be used to show that the Hasse invariant does not depend on the particular diagonalization. By definition, such a chain starts from a given orthogonal basis and at each step one forms a new orthogonal basis by replacing a finite number of vectors of the basis, making sure that at least one vector stays the same.

::: {.unit-lean}
