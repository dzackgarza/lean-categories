### Definition/result: noetherian rings and Hilbert basis theorem {#fc16-c01-u002}

### Definition/result: integral elements, integral closure, integrally closed domains {#fc16-c01-u008}

### Construction/result: generalized rings of quotients and local rings {#fc16-c01-u014}

### Definition/result: M-adic topology, Krull dimension, regular local rings {#fc16-c01-u016}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC16-C01-U016` · §0.12; p. 3 · after [`FC16-C01-U002`](#fc16-c01-u002), [`FC16-C01-U008`](#fc16-c01-u008)
:::

The powers of the maximal ideal define the separated M-adic topology; Krull dimension is maximal prime-chain length; a local ring is regular when this equals the minimal number of maximal-ideal generators. Regular local rings are integrally closed domains.

The source defines separability in arbitrary characteristic via p-power linear independence and separably generated extensions; for finitely generated extensions the two notions agree, perfect bases give separability, and separability is transitive in towers.

### Definition/result: field derivations and separability criterion {#fc16-c01-u019}

::: {.unit-meta}

An F-derivation E→L is additive, Leibniz, and vanishes on F. For separably generated E/F, Der_F(E,L) has dimension tr.deg_F E; separability is characterized by extension of derivations, and derivations vanish on pth powers in characteristic p.
