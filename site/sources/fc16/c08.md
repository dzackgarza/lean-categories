### Lemma: completeness transfers across bijective equivariant homogeneous morphisms {#fc16-c08-u002}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC16-C08-U002` · §21.1; p. 133 · after [`FC16-C01-U090`](c01.html#fc16-c01-u090), [`FC16-C02-U024`](c02.html#fc16-c02-u024), [`FC16-C02-U025`](c02.html#fc16-c02-u025)
:::

If connected G acts transitively on irreducible X,Y and φ:X→Y is a bijective G-equivariant morphism with Y complete, then X is complete.

### Theorem: Borel fixed point theorem {#fc16-c08-u003}

### Definition: Borel subgroup {#fc16-c08-u004}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC16-C08-U004` · §21.3; p. 134 · after [`FC16-C07-U005`](c07.html#fc16-c07-u005), [`FC16-C02-U006`](c02.html#fc16-c02-u006)
:::

A Borel subgroup is a maximal closed connected solvable subgroup of G; Borel subgroups of G and G° coincide.

### Theorem: conjugacy of Borel subgroups and projectivity of G/B {#fc16-c08-u005}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC16-C08-U005` · §21.3; pp. 134–135 · after [`FC16-C08-U004`](#fc16-c08-u004), [`FC16-C08-U003`](#fc16-c08-u003), [`FC16-C04-U003`](c04.html#fc16-c04-u003), [`FC16-C01-U038`](c01.html#fc16-c01-u038)
:::

For any Borel subgroup B of connected G, G/B is projective and every Borel subgroup is G-conjugate to B.

### Corollary: maximal tori and maximal connected unipotent subgroups {#fc16-c08-u006}

::: {.unit-meta}

:::

The maximal tori and maximal connected unipotent subgroups of G are precisely those occurring in Borel subgroups; each class is conjugate in G.

### Definition: parabolic subgroup {#fc16-c08-u008}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC16-C08-U008` · §21.3; pp. 135–136 · after [`FC16-C04-U009`](c04.html#fc16-c04-u009), [`FC16-C01-U092`](c01.html#fc16-c01-u092)
:::

A closed P≤G is parabolic when G/P is projective, equivalently complete.

### Corollary: parabolic subgroups are exactly those containing a Borel subgroup {#fc16-c08-u009}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC16-C08-U009` · §21.3; p. 135 · after [`FC16-C08-U008`](#fc16-c08-u008), [`FC16-C08-U005`](#fc16-c08-u005), [`FC16-C08-U003`](#fc16-c08-u003)
:::

A closed subgroup P is parabolic iff it contains a Borel subgroup; connected H is Borel iff H is solvable and G/H is projective.

### Corollary: Borel/parabolic/torus/unipotent subgroups under epimorphisms {#fc16-c08-u011}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC16-C08-U011` · §21.3; p. 136 · after [`FC16-C02-U012`](c02.html#fc16-c02-u012), [`FC16-C08-U005`](#fc16-c08-u005), [`FC16-C08-U009`](#fc16-c08-u009), [`FC16-C08-U006`](#fc16-c08-u006)

An epimorphism of connected algebraic groups maps Borel subgroups, parabolics, maximal tori and maximal connected unipotent subgroups onto subgroups of the same type, and every such target subgroup arises this way.

### Proposition: nilpotent Borel criteria {#fc16-c08-u014}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC16-C08-U014` · §21.4; p. 137 · after [`FC16-C07-U010`](c07.html#fc16-c07-u010), [`FC16-C08-U005`](#fc16-c08-u005), [`FC16-C08-U006`](#fc16-c08-u006)
:::

If a Borel subgroup is nilpotent then it equals G; equivalently G is nilpotent iff it has a unique maximal torus.

### Corollary/definition: connected centralizer of a maximal torus as Cartan subgroup {#fc16-c08-u015}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC16-C08-U015` · §21.4; p. 137 · after [`FC16-C08-U014`](#fc16-c08-u014), [`FC16-C06-U025`](c06.html#fc16-c06-u025), [`FC16-C08-U006`](#fc16-c08-u006)
:::

For maximal torus T, C_G(T)° is nilpotent and self-normalizing up to identity component; it is called a Cartan subgroup at this stage.

### Lemma: density criterion from a homogeneous fixed-point set {#fc16-c08-u016}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC16-C08-U016` · §22.1; pp. 138–139 · after [`FC16-C04-U009`](c04.html#fc16-c04-u009), [`FC16-C01-U090`](c01.html#fc16-c01-u090), [`FC16-C01-U066`](c01.html#fc16-c01-u066)
:::

For closed connected H≤G, the union of conjugates X=⋃_g gHg^{-1} is closed if G/H is complete; if an element of H fixes only finitely many points of G/H then X contains a dense open subset of G.

### Theorem: density theorem for Borel, torus, unipotent and Cartan conjugates {#fc16-c08-u017}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC16-C08-U017` · §22.2; pp. 139–140 · after [`FC16-C08-U016`](#fc16-c08-u016), [`FC16-C08-U005`](#fc16-c08-u005), [`FC16-C08-U006`](#fc16-c08-u006), [`FC16-C06-U008`](c06.html#fc16-c06-u008), [`FC16-C08-U015`](#fc16-c08-u015)
:::

For connected G, the union of conjugates of a Borel subgroup is all G; the union of conjugates of a maximal torus is the semisimple locus, that of the Borel unipotent radical is the unipotent locus, and conjugates of a Cartan subgroup contain a dense open subset.

### Corollary: semisimple/unipotent elements lie in connected nilpotent groups {#fc16-c08-u018}

Every semisimple or unipotent element of connected G lies in a connected nilpotent subgroup.

### Corollary: Borel subgroups of torus centralizers {#fc16-c08-u024}

### Result: Cartan subgroup structure summary {#fc16-c08-u025}
