### Definition: left-invariant derivations {#fc16-c03-u001}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC16-C03-U001` · §9.1; p. 65 · after [`FC16-C02-U027`](c02.html#fc16-c02-u027), [`FC16-C01-U019`](c01.html#fc16-c01-u019)
:::

A derivation of K[G] is left invariant when it commutes with left translations; these form L(G), the Lie algebra of G under commutator.

### Theorem: Lie algebra as tangent space at identity {#fc16-c03-u002}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC16-C03-U002` · §9.1; pp. 65–66 · after [`FC16-C03-U001`](#fc16-c03-u001), [`FC16-C01-U078`](c01.html#fc16-c01-u078), [`FC16-C02-U011`](c02.html#fc16-c02-u011)
:::

Evaluation at e gives a vector-space isomorphism L(G)≅T(G)_e, functorial for algebraic-group morphisms and compatible with Lie brackets.

### Definition/construction: right convolution action of the tangent Lie algebra {#fc16-c03-u003}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC16-C03-U003` · §9.2; pp. 66–67 · after [`FC16-C03-U002`](#fc16-c03-u002), [`FC16-C02-U027`](c02.html#fc16-c02-u027)
:::

A tangent vector x∈g acts on K[G] by right convolution f↦f*x; the commutator of convolution operators defines the Lie bracket on g.

### Examples: Lie algebras of classical matrix groups {#fc16-c03-u004}

::: {.unit-meta}

Lie(GL_n)=gl_n; Lie(SL_n)=sl_n; triangular, diagonal and unipotent triangular groups have the expected matrix Lie algebras, with analogous classical formulas.

### Proposition: differentials of multiplication, inversion and commutator {#fc16-c03-u009}

::: {.unit-meta}
`FC16-C03-U009` · §10.1; p. 71 · after [`FC16-C01-U085`](c01.html#fc16-c01-u085), [`FC16-C03-U002`](#fc16-c03-u002)
:::

At the identity, dμ(x,y)=x+y and di(x)=−x; the differential of the commutator morphism y↦yxy^{-1}x^{-1} is (1−Ad x).

### Proposition: differentiating dual and tensor representations {#fc16-c03-u023}

::: {.unit-meta}

:::
