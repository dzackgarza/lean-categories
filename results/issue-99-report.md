## Item 1 — the path representation preserves declared parameters and selected edge sequences

`Route` already carries typed endpoints and the ordered `Array StructuralEdge`
(`Semantic.lean:1717`), each step carrying its `EdgeRef` label and `FunctorExpr`
(`Semantic.lean:1662`). Added, so that the retention is a statement and not a convention:

- `Route.refs_retains_steps` (`RouteDiscovery.lean:305`) — a route's step labels are exactly
  `Route.refs`; labels are `EdgeRef.functor` / `EdgeRef.classifierForget` /
  `EdgeRef.constructMap c inner` (`Entry.lean:43-50`).
- `comparisonCandidates_routes_retained` (`RouteDiscovery.lean:294`) — a witness's routes are the
  registered row's routes, in the direction it is read.

## Item 2 — comparison selection retains exact witnesses and direction; competing cells stay distinguishable and ambiguous

New (no comparison-selection layer existed; `state.cells` is empty across the whole registry):

- `ComparisonDirection` (`:54`), `ComparisonContext` (`:66`) — `direct`, `whiskerLeft`,
  `whiskerRight`, `constructMap`; the context of a whiskered or constructor-induced reading is
  recorded, its legality belongs to the composite being built.
- `ComparisonWitness` (`:81`) — retains the whole `CellEntry` (stable id, declaration, both routes,
  endpoints), the direction and the context. Selection never instantiates the declaration, so the
  parameter telescope is the registered one, checked at registration by `validateCell`
  (`Semantic.lean:2707`).
- `SemanticState.comparisonCandidates` (`:133`) — registered rows relating a route pair, in the
  orientation that reads the first route first; a non-invertible row is never a backward candidate
  (`comparisonCandidates_backward_invertible`, `:203`).
- `mem_comparisonCandidates_iff` (`:162`) — soundness *and* completeness of selection against
  `IsComparisonCandidate` (`:142`): every candidate is a registered row read in a supported
  direction, and every registered row relating the pair in a supported direction is a candidate.
  Nothing is deduplicated.
- `comparisonCandidates_pair_retained` (`:215`) — two parallel registered cells with distinct ids
  both appear as candidates with distinct ids.
- `ComparisonSelection` (`:93`), `SemanticState.selectComparison` (`:230`) —
  `unrelated | unique w | competing ws`; `selectComparison_eq_competing_of_two` (`:260`) reports the
  competing outcome and returns *both* witnesses; `selectComparison_eq_unique_iff` (`:247`) fixes the
  unique case; `selectComparison_eq_unrelated_iff` (`:237`).

Probes (`ComparisonProbes.lean`, kernel `decide`): two parallel invertible cells on one pair give
`competing` with the three candidates in registration order including the invertible
reverse-direction row (`id99.competing` `:70`, `id99.competingIds` `:76`); a single row gives
`unique` (`:82`); a non-invertible row is not read backwards, so the reversed pair is `unrelated`
(`:88`) while its own direction is `unique` (`:98`); an unrelated pair is `unrelated` (`:93`).

No claim is made that `unrelated` decides equality of composites: that is the word problem, which
the issue withdrew.

## Item 3 — the finiteness obligation, as a named hypothesis

- `Reaches` (`:314`) is `Relation.ReflTransGen` of a step relation.
- `reaches_measure_le` (`:318`) — a strictly decreasing measure bounds every reached point. That is
  all a measure gives.
- `FiniteSublevels` (`:327`) and `finite_reach_of_finite_sublevels` (`:330`) — reachability is finite
  under **finite sublevel sets**.
- `finite_reach_of_enclosure` (`:337`) — reachability is finite under an **independently checked
  finite enclosure**.
- `mem_insert_targets_of_reaches` (`:344`), `finite_reach_of_finite_edges` (`:357`) — when the step
  relation is presented by finitely many edges, the enclosure is checked *by the presentation*
  (`{s} ∪ targets of the edges`), so the hypothesis is discharged by exhibiting the edges.
- `counterStep_reach_infinite` (`:374`) and `counterStep_not_finite_sublevels` (`:387`) — a strictly
  decreasing measure (`Prod.fst`) whose sublevel sets are infinite reaches an infinite set from
  `(1, 0)`. The measure alone is therefore not an adequate finiteness argument, which is exactly the
  body's sentence.
- `IsWalk` (`:395`), `reaches_getElem_of_isWalk` (`:399`), `walk_length_le_card` (`:415`),
  `walk_length_le_card_of_finite_edges` (`:430`) — under a checked enclosure every simple walk is no
  longer than the enclosure, so a fuel bound follows from the hypothesis instead of being a constant.
  Without the hypothesis the constant stays what it is.

Registry-level statement: `SemanticState.RouteStep` (`:443`) is the search's own one-step relation,
and `SemanticState.finite_reach_of_enclosure` (`:451`) carries the enclosure hypothesis to it. That
hypothesis is discharged nowhere for the real registry, and this is not an omission of the unit: the
registry's edge set is not fixed by its rows. `SemanticState.edgesFrom` (`Semantic.lean:1698`)
derives `c(U) : c(A) → c(B)` from every edge `U : A → B` at every construct node, and
`CategoryExpr` is closed under `construct` (`Syntax.lean:258-271`), so one registered functor row
already yields the infinite family `Arrⁿ(A) → Arrⁿ(B)`. `edgesFrom` is also `partial`, so no proof
unfolds it. Finiteness of the row set therefore does not give finiteness of the edge set, which is
why the hypothesis is stated and not discharged — and why the issue's own remark that a finite array
of base registrations does not prove finite closure under constructor-generated edges holds here.

## Item 4 — existing outcomes preserved; explicitly specified composites not rejected

- Registry-permutation, missing/reversed comparison, multiple-route and competing-cell outcomes are
  untouched: `Registry/OperationBindingTests.lean` (44 lines: explicit binding retained, two
  incomparable monoid routes survive, no order-based choice) and
  `Semantics/Algebra/AdditiveOperationsTests.lean:65-86` are unchanged and still elaborate.
- Explicit composites: `SemanticState.routeEdges?` (`Semantic.lean:1744`) checks a caller-given
  `Array EdgeRef` step by step against `edgesFrom`, with no simplicity restriction and no fuel.
  Probes: a two-cycle specimen's implicit enumeration returns exactly one simple route
  (`id99.simpleRoutesOnly`, `ComparisonProbes.lean:114`) while the explicitly specified revisiting
  composite `a → b → a → b` is accepted (`id99.explicitRevisiting`, `:120`). Cyclic composites are
  therefore preserved separately from simple-route discovery, as the body requires.
