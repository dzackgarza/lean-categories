## Chapter 19. Application to Moduli of K3 Surfaces

### Period-carrier correction and realization — 2026-09-22

The earlier U021 comparison claimed that TauCeti's full polarized-Hodge
`PeriodDomain.Point` was "exactly the abstract period-point carrier needed
here" on the K3 intersection lattice. That claim is retracted. The cited
revision `bc404ebee90736d974da54ffd8fb7c7993d2f86d` returned HTTP 404 for
the recorded file. The already recorded Appendix-B revision
`f962d4a1e6a5eb6a87cd76a3900bc845df3f5950` was retrieved successfully,
including its Apache-2.0 license, and its actual constructors were read.

At that revision, `Geometry/Hodge/PeriodDomain.lean`, lines 71--81, requires
`IsPolarization` on the full fixed lattice. `Geometry/Hodge/Polarization.lean`,
lines 26--33 and 68--81, uses the convention
`0 < i^(2p-n) Q(u,conjugate u)` on every Hodge component. In weight two this
has the opposite sign from the source's positive intersection pairing on the
period line. Switching to `Q=-b` addresses that sign on the primitive
polarized complement, but imposes negativity of `b` on all of `(1,1)`;
that is not the source's full K3 intersection-form domain with its
Lorentzian `(1,1)` part. No comparison theorem or contradictory Hodge object
has been authored. The reference is useful for the tensor-conjugation
construction, not as an interchangeable full-lattice carrier.

The corrected supporting data now live in
`LeanCategories/Lattices/Valued/PeriodDomainData.lean`. This uses the
source's explicit positive isotropic projective-line definition from B-U032,
native `Projectivization.submodule`, native tensor complexification and
`LinearMap.BilinForm.baseChange`, and the reference's
`concreteLatticeConjIntLinear` from `Geometry/Hodge/Conjugation.lean`,
lines 455--457. Only that two-line data construction is adapted; no Hodge
filtration, conjugation laws, or polarization proofs are ported. This
resolves the newly exposed supporting-owner lookup with existing native
owners and an exact inspected reference, rather than inventing a Hodge stack.

`K3PeriodDomainData.lean` specializes to the existing actual
`k3Lattice = 3U ⊥ 2E₈(-1)`. It gives the fixed-positive-class locus (U021),
the fixed-sublattice orthogonality locus (U025), and the integral lattice
`NS(u)` (U033). The latter is an intersection of native pairing-functional
kernels, not an arbitrary supplied module. The projective conditions use
the actual native line, so no new representative-independence proof is
needed to state them. General submodule inputs retain all of the source's
primitive-embedding specializations without proving embedding results.

The earlier unsupported full-polarization-carrier claims in U012/U031,
C20-U015/U037, C21-U019/U030 and B-U032/U034 must not be consumed unchanged.
Their pending ports must use the corrected projective-line owner where
the source calls for the K3 intersection-form domain. B-U032's general
higher-dimensional isotropic-plane clause is not realized by the line
specialization. C19-U022 remains the next same-owner construction: the
integral orthogonal action and its arithmetic quotient have not been
supplied by merely naming a generic orbit quotient.

with SHA-256
`5bc968dad21b0288f2b3d49612d94e750cd759724a16c8c25724c79410641a33`.
On 2026-09-24, printed pp. 346--347 were compared directly with U021.
`K3DegreePolarizationClassData k` records the source's primitive positive
integral class `ℓ` together with the exact normalization `ℓ · ℓ = 2k`,
and `k3DegreePolarizedPeriodDomainData` is exactly
`D(ℓ⊥) = {[u] ∈ D(Λ) | u · ℓ = 0}`. The more general
`k3PolarizedPeriodDomainData` remains available for arbitrary supplied
positive classes. The algebraicity, one-`O(Λ)`-orbit, ampleness,
root-hyperplane, and moduli statements remain result clauses and are not
consumed by this acceptance.

The focused source checks found no proof/result commands in either new Lean
owner, and every direct import resolves to an existing source file. These
are static checks, not successful Lean elaboration. `git diff --check`
passes for the explicit source/provenance paths.

The subsequent exact owner-interface read caught and corrected a source
error in the K3 wrappers: `BilinModuleCat.pairing` is an unbundled two-input
function, while the period constructions require `LinearMap.BilinForm`.
They now consume `BilinModuleCat.bilinMap`, the existing bundled adjoint
from `Modules/Bilinear/Valued/Fixed.lean`, lines 244--258. The positive-square
test still legitimately evaluates `pairing ℓ ℓ`. The generic owner retains
the integral-module instance explicitly, so these constructions consume
the bundled lattice's module structure rather than fixing a separately
inferred instance. This was a static source correction, not a compiler pass.

For U031, the admitted Peters--Sterk PDF was checked at printed p. 350.
The source first forms the real `(1,1)` space orthogonal to the period line,
its light-cone components, and the `(-2)` roots orthogonal to the period.
Removing those root hyperplanes and taking connected components gives the
Weyl chambers in each light-cone component; if the root set is empty, the
chamber is the entire component.  The local
`K3WeylChamberData.lean` realization uses the corrected projective-line
`K3PeriodPointData`, the actual K3 intersection form, the fixed K3 basis
only as a concrete real-coordinate model, and Mathlib
`connectedComponentIn`.  It does not consume TauCeti's incompatible full
`PeriodDomain.Point`.  The assertions that the light cone has exactly two
components, that the displayed root halves form an exhaustive partition,
the fundamental-domain properties, and realization by strongly marked K3
surfaces remain result content.  Focused target-pin elaboration of the new
owner is green.
