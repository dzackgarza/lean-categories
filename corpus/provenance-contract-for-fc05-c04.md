# Provenance contract for FC05 C04

- Project-first search was performed at repo HEAD `f90173886bc881c2d43c0a7eb2d9fe7f110ac9a5`. `LeanCategories/Homological/GlobalDimension.lean` owns the right global and injective-global suprema and proves the division-ring global-dimension-zero example; it explicitly does not implement weak/flat dimension. `LeanCategories/Homological/IsQuasiFrobenius.lean` exactly owns U017. `LeanCategories/Homological/IsHereditary.lean` owns the right-hereditary predicate but not the full comparison bundle in U027 nor the global-dimension theorem U028. No project implementation of Koszul complexes, Cohen–Macaulay/Gorenstein theory, von Neumann regular rings, or local cohomology comparisons closes the remaining rows.
- Pinned Mathlib is `P=db584cd6d46c92f209a44c0f1c829460d327499d`, Lean 4.33.0. The positive P routes were declaration-probed successfully in the project environment. P supplies semisimple rings/Wedderburn–Artin/Maschke (U014–U015), polynomial scalar extension (U035), commutative-local and noncommutative Jacobson/Nakayama theory (U039–U041), finite-projective local freeness (U042), lifting freeness through a regular element (U044), the same-ring projective-dimension formula for quotienting a module by a regular element (U046), local/residue-field conventions (U047), and the regular-local definition/cotangent-space characterization (U048).
- The chapter retains Weibel's arbitrary-associative-ring convention until the source explicitly specializes. This matters for U001–U013 and the first change-of-rings blocks. P/H `Module.Flat` and the Ext-form Baer theorem discussed below are commutative-base interfaces; a commutative specialization is not credited for an arbitrary-ring row. In particular, current Mathlib's `ModuleCat.injective_iff_subsingleton_ext_quotient_one` is under `[CommRing R]`, whereas U013 is stated for arbitrary associative rings and left ideals. P has the arbitrary-ring Baer extension criterion but not its Ext quotient reformulation, and targeted external search found no noncommutative Ext-form owner, so U013 remains unmatched.
- Current Mathlib was rechecked immediately before promotion at `H=5aedf732b6987e8c26ab3c9ebc855314f82b045f`. H adds `Mathlib/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Ext/Baer.lean` and module-category projective/injective-dimension transport/localization files, but these do not fill the arbitrary-ring global/weak-dimension theory, residue-field Ext/Tor criteria, Auslander–Buchsbaum, Koszul, Gorenstein, or regular-local-UFD gaps. `Mathlib/Algebra/Homology/LocalCohomology.lean` still explicitly lists equivalence with right-derived torsion, Koszul-limit, and Čech constructions as future work. `Mathlib/RingTheory/RegularLocalRing/Defs.lean` still explicitly lists localization of regular local rings as TODO.
- Mathlib PR `#26218` was rechecked at head `2a84ea75981e352045999fd11bc7ef6b8fab549f`. Its `Mathlib/RingTheory/Depth/Basic.lean`, `Depth/Rees.lean`, and `CohenMacaulay/Basic.lean` define depth via Ext, prove the regular-sequence/Rees characterizations, and define local Cohen–Macaulayness. U050 has since been ported/adapted into `LeanCategories/ForMathlib/LocalGradeCohenMacaulay.lean` (original integration `466c41d`), so its Sweep-III route is now `project-existing` while retaining this exact Apache-2.0 reference provenance. The 2026-09-14 source check repaired the port to include U047's standing Noetherian-local convention explicitly. U055 remains theorem/reference work. The related polynomial-CM PR `#28599` was also inspected but does not by itself close another C04 row.
- The current `tom111/BEI-lean` head `5b3ddff1ffac4a12d16da43df6fc443501f4d680` contains downstream/backported Cohen–Macaulay files corresponding to those open Mathlib PRs. Because the upstream open PRs are the revision authority, no C04 route is attributed to the downstream copy.
- Koszul search was refreshed exhaustively on 2026-09-14 through the live formalization-corpus under `Koszul complex`, `KoszulComplex`, exterior/wedge differential, central-sequence, tensor-product, and power-transition formulations, including the indexed non-Lean proof assistants. The strongest implementation is `CBirkbeck/uniform-sheafy-tate-domains-lean@2f45865e87d20dd460cc5a14d04850bf03be4570` (Apache-2.0, Lean 4.33 / the same pinned Mathlib): `Adic spaces/FJP/KoszulFiniteFree.lean` constructs the finite free subset-model differential and proves `koszulDifferential_comp`, and documents the standard exterior sign convention. It is intentionally `[CommRing R]`, so it is preserved as prior art but cannot replace Weibel's U068/U070 construction for a central sequence in an arbitrary associative ring. `facebookresearch/atlas-lean@e8b31c5cb0bec89b487ce33fe525a2c0b0f8b9c6` (Apache-2.0) has a narrower field/polynomial `KoszulComplex` structure whose differential and `d²=0` are inputs, not a canonical construction. No indexed Rocq/Agda/Isabelle/HOL/Mizar source contained a Koszul complex implementation at the frozen generality; the Isabelle `Koszul` hits were signature-Gröbner terminology. The same CBirkbeck tree has ring-map/scalar-rescaling naturality but no `K(x^(i+1)) → K(x^i)` power transition, and live power-transition queries found no alternative owner, so U080 remains unmatched. The older DG-algebra evidence for U071 is unchanged.
- After that search exhausted the ecosystem at the source generality, Sweep III U068 authored the missing owner in `LeanCategories/Homological/KoszulComplex.lean`. `koszulBoundaryLinearMap_comp` proves `d²=0` for central sequences over arbitrary associative rings, and `koszulComplex`/`canonicalKoszulComplexRealization` construct the actual chain complex and basis formula. The external CBirkbeck/Atlas implementations remain attribution-bearing prior art; they are not the route because of their stricter commutativity/field hypotheses.
- Auslander–Buchsbaum/regular-local-UFD search was also refreshed. Current `Brian-Nugent/Nugent_Lean@0c86a5f27b2b4cc13c54584815af8f6b6eab9c73` advertises the relevant regular-local criterion/UFD endpoint, but `LeanMath/RLR=UFD/Testing.lean` still contains multiple unresolved `sorry` holes and the repository tree exposes no license file. It is not a proved or acceptable mapping route. Targeted current/external search found no completed replacement for U063–U067.
- Semisimple theory is not used to over-credit ring-dimension rows. P proves every module over a semisimple ring projective and injective, and those clauses are reusable; U016's additional ring global-dimension, Tor/weak-dimension, and arbitrary-ring flatness clauses remain unsupported in the source generality. The same clause-wise rule is applied to change-of-rings, hereditary, and regular-local rows: adjacent ingredients are recorded without silently supplying missing theorem clauses.
