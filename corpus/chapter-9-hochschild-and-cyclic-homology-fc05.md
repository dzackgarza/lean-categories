Write a pure n-chain as `(m;a₁,…,aₙ)`. The source faces are right action by a₁ at i=0, multiplication of aᵢaᵢ₊₁ at 0<i<n, and left action by aₙ at i=n; sⱼ inserts 1 after j algebra entries. Each formula is k-multilinear: an input either occurs once unchanged, once in one bilinear multiplication, or once in a bilinear module action; distributivity and scalar compatibility prove the add/smul clauses in each coordinate. `PiTensorProduct.lift` and `TensorProduct.lift` therefore give actual linear maps, not requested realizations. Here is the complete face-relation comparison for i<j. Disjoint interior contractions leave the same ordered word; adjacent interior contractions use `(ab)c=a(bc)`. The adjacent first-end case uses `(ma)b=m(ab)`, the adjacent last-end case uses `(ab)m=a(bm)`, and the pair of opposite ends uses `(am)b=a(mb)`. An endpoint and a nonadjacent interior contraction operate on disjoint entries. These cases exhaust i<j and prove `dᵢdⱼ=dⱼ₋₁dᵢ` on all pure tensors; the native two tensor extensionality lemmas extend them to linear-map equality. Two degeneracies insert the same two units in the same order. For `dᵢsⱼ`, an index before the inserted unit gives `sⱼ₋₁dᵢ`, the two adjacent indices give the identity by the algebra/module unit laws, and an index after it gives `sⱼdᵢ₋₁`; this includes j=0, j=n and degree zero. Thus every one of the six generator-relation hypotheses of the cited simplicial constructor is obtained from the displayed native algebra/bimodule laws, rather than supplied as a new simplicial axiom. For cochains take `Hom_k(R^{⊗n},M)`: the first coface is `a₁ f(a₂,…,aₙ₊₁)`, interior cofaces precompose adjacent multiplication, the last is `f(a₁,…,aₙ)aₙ₊₁`, and codegeneracies precompose unit insertion. These are linear both in f and its tensor argument by the same coordinate check. Evaluate any cosimplicial identity on a tuple: the same disjoint/adjacent/interior and two endpoint cases give its equality by associativity, commuting actions and unit laws; Hom extensionality and tensor extensionality then give the six equalities used by the cosimplicial constructor. This is an independent Hom construction, not an unjustified identification with the linear dual of the chain object. The constructor readback isomorphisms identify all degrees and operators with precisely these formulas; the empty-tensor equivalence and unit/evaluation equivalences identify degree zero with M. Native alternating complexes and homology then give the source H_n and H^n. In degree one the chain differential is `m⊗r ↦ mr−rm`, retaining the noncommutative bimodule distinction. No freeness, flatness, finite-dimensionality, symmetry of M, or commutativity of R was introduced. |
