### U030 explicit unfolding and discriminant data, 2026-09-23

`HypersurfaceUnfoldingData.lean` constructs equation (18.3), its zero-locus
family over chosen neighbourhoods, the parameter projection, the fibre-critical
locus and its image in the base. It reuses the existing Euclidean coordinate
owner and native finite sums/products, `HasFDerivAt`, subtypes and set images.
The source's chosen monomial exponents are inputs; the family and discriminant
are outputs. This is thin source-specific assembly, not a new support theory.

The original June-2024 PDF was read at printed p. 334 / PDF page 341; its
SHA-256 is `5bc968dad21b0288f2b3d49612d94e750cd759724a16c8c25724c79410641a33`.
Its printed map `(z,u₀,u) ↦ (F(z,u₀,u),u)` has the independent constant
source parameter `u₀`, supplying the first target direction; the remaining
parameters supply the others. That ambient map cannot literally have the
intended critical values for a holomorphic family. The following sentence
also calls a locus in the base a locus of critical points. The implementation
uses the preceding zero-hypersurface family: project `F=0, d_z F=0` to its
parameter base. The defective ambient-map display remains recorded under
LC-05; the preceding zero-hypersurface family and the standard
singular-fibre meaning of the discriminant determine the intended construction.
`HasFDerivAt` records an actual zero differential, excluding
nondifferentiable points without a totalized-derivative convention or new proof.

The source specializes to holomorphic `f`, chosen Tjurina-basis monomials and
sufficiently small neighbourhoods. No basis-existence, semiuniversality,
hypersurface, local-triviality or monodromy result is proved here. Target-pin
acceptance 2026-09-23: focused
`lake build LeanCategories.Topology.HypersurfaceUnfoldingData` is green.
The definition layer is therefore accepted against the coherent
zero-hypersurface interpretation; the printed ambient-map defect remains a
source complaint rather than a Lean obligation.

### U025 fibre and homology construction, 2026-09-23

The original June-2024 author PDF was read on printed pp. 332--333
(PDF pages 339--340), including the section's even-dimensional convention.
Its exact URL/hash is retained in `provenance-contract-for-fc11-c09.md`.
The source uses an open neighbourhood `‖z‖² < η`, not a closed ball or
a bound `‖z‖ < η`, and the parameter belongs to the punctured open disk.

`LeanCategories/Topology/MilnorFiberData.lean` constructs those subsets,
the tube's restricted projection, and the actual level-set fibre with its
subspace topology. The ambient owner is native `EuclideanSpace ℂ (Fin (n+1))`,
whose norm is Euclidean rather than the finite-product sup norm.
`MilnorFiberHomologyData.lean` then applies the native
`AlgebraicTopology.singularHomologyFunctor (ModuleCat ℤ) n` with integral
coefficients to that fibre. No abstract supplied fibre, free module of
chosen rank, or alternative singular-chain theory is introduced.

The supporting-owner read inspected pinned Mathlib
`Analysis/InnerProductSpace/PiL2.lean`,
`AlgebraicTopology/SingularHomology/Basic.lean` (lines 32--57), and
`Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Abelian.lean`. The project intersection search
also read the inherited, untracked `FourManifoldIntersectionData.lean`:
its supplied degree-four evaluation functional on raw cochains does not
construct the geometric intersection pairing on this fibre's `H_n`.
That inherited owner was not modified or imported.

The remaining pairing is now realized in
LeanCategories/Topology/MilnorFiberIntersectionData.lean by reusing the
existing geometric chain-intersection owner from SingularLinkingNumberData.
That owner was generalized from degrees (2,1) to arbitrary prescribed
degrees, without changing the linking-number specialization. For the actual
Milnor fibre, concrete degree-n cycles are mapped through the native cycles
object and homology quotient. A homology pairing candidate is then selected
by the exact graph requiring every transverse pair of cycle representatives
to evaluate to their oriented chain intersection number. This does not accept
an arbitrary bilinear form or fundamental-evaluation map as input.

Focused target-pin builds of SingularLinkingNumberData and
MilnorFiberIntersectionData are green on 2026-09-25. U025 is therefore
accepted at the definition layer. Existence of transverse representatives,
independence of representatives, descent, bilinearity, symmetry for even
complex dimension, freeness/rank, homotopy type and local triviality remain
Sweep-IV results. The defining topological subsets and raw homology make
sense for general f and radii; the source specializes to a holomorphic
representative with isolated critical point and sufficiently small positive
parameters.
