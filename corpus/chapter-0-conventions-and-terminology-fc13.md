## Front matter — conventions and terminology

| FC13 ID | Verdict | Route | Lean target(s) | Provenance | Interface comparison |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `FC13-C00-U001` | exact commutative-unital ring/homomorphism/subring convention | `mathlib` | `Mathlib/Algebra/Ring/Defs.lean::CommRing`; `Mathlib/Algebra/Ring/Hom/Defs.lean::RingHom`; `Mathlib/Algebra/Ring/Subring/Defs.lean::Subring` | P=db584cd6d46c92f209a44c0f1c829460d327499d | `CommRing` is the commutative ring structure with identity; `RingHom` is the bundled unital ring homomorphism (through its multiplicative-hom component); and the pinned `Subring` definition explicitly shares the ambient 0 and 1. Together these are exactly Matsumura’s standing convention. |
| `FC13-C00-U002` | exact project-owned ideal extension/contraction interface | `project-existing` | `LeanCategories/Algebra/Ideals.lean::LeanCategories.Algebra.idealMapOrderHom`, `LeanCategories.Algebra.idealComapOrderHom`, `LeanCategories.Algebra.idealMapFunctor_obj`, `LeanCategories.Algebra.idealComapFunctor_obj` | A=3b2239e161592472b34e02ad3b7da308f9ce7280 | At project HEAD, extension along `f : A →+* B` is definitionally `Ideal.map f I` and contraction is `Ideal.comap f J`; the object theorems expose those equalities. Pinned Mathlib defines `Ideal.map` as the ideal span of the image and `Ideal.comap` as the preimage, so this project-owned pair is exactly the source `IB=f(I)B` / `f⁻¹(J)` notation semantics. |
| `FC13-C00-U003` | exact finitely generated-ideal interface | `mathlib` | `Mathlib/RingTheory/Ideal/Span.lean::Ideal.span`, `Ideal.span_insert` | P=db584cd6d46c92f209a44c0f1c829460d327499d | `Ideal.span s` is explicitly the ideal generated by a subset `s`; `Ideal.span_insert` identifies adjoining one generator with the join of the corresponding principal ideal and the previous span. Iterating over a finite generator set gives exactly the source ideal `(a₁,…,aₙ)=Σ aᵢA`. |
| `FC13-C00-U004` | exact non-strict-inclusion/strict-chain interface | `mathlib` | `Mathlib/Data/Set/Defs.lean::Set.Subset`; `Mathlib/Order/Monotone/Defs.lean::StrictMono` | P=db584cd6d46c92f209a44c0f1c829460d327499d | `Set.Subset` (and the induced subobject order `≤`) is non-strict and therefore permits equality, while `StrictMono f` requires `a<b → f a<f b`. Thus a module/ideal chain represented by a strictly monotone indexed family has exactly the source “ascending chain means strictly increasing” convention. |
| `FC13-C00-U005` | exact positive-prime characteristic convention | `mathlib` | `Mathlib/Algebra/CharP/Defs.lean::CharP`; `Mathlib/Data/Nat/Prime/Defs.lean::Nat.Prime` | P=db584cd6d46c92f209a44c0f1c829460d327499d | `CharP R p` records that the characteristic is `p`; the additional hypothesis `Nat.Prime p` says `p≥2`, hence positive and prime. The conjunction, rather than `CharP` alone, exactly represents Matsumura’s convention when the symbol `p` is used for positive characteristic. |
